Full description

GBS St.Gallen is a leading vocational training institution in Eastern Switzerland. We train around 4,500 vocational learners in over 45 occupations of basic vocational training. We are known for innovative teaching methods and the continuous development of our offerings – always with the aim of optimally preparing our learners for the future.

What to expect

  • Instruction of learners in the development of animated content for interactive means of communication
  • Providing the basic knowledge for planning, creating and implementing animations in the field of 2D and 3D
  • Fulfillment of the professional mandate for teachers at vocational colleges
  • Further education in the pedagogical-didactic field or the willingness to complete one

What sets it apart

  • Communicative personality with pronounced social competence, exemplary appearance and great pleasure in dealing with learners and teaching
  • Several years of professional experience in animation, motion design or 3D as well as in-depth knowledge of 2D and/or 3D animation
  • Experience with storytelling, motion design and visual communication
  • Understanding digital media and interactive applications as well as interest in current technological developments and artificial intelligence (AI)
  • Enjoying experimental and creative working methods
  • Ability to convey complex design and technical content in an understandable way, ideally experience in vocational training or adult education
  • Willingness to develop your own exercises, projects and teaching materials

What we offer

  • An interesting and challenging job in a motivated environment
  • Employed under the Supplementary Regulation on the Employment of Teachers at Vocational and Training Centres Interested and motivated? We look forward to your complete application documents. Please submit your dossier by 20 September 2026 via the link below.

Contact: Franziska Eriksen, Head of Interactive Media Designer EFZ, franziska.eriksen@gbssg.ch
