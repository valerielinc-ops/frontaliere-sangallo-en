Full description

“We inspire our customers – personally and digitally.” We are therefore continuously developing our mobile banking app. As a Senior Mobile Engineer, you will take over the technical lead and the technical coordination of the further development of our SGKB app. At the same time, you stay close to the development and implement challenging mobile solutions together with the team.

This is what you will do.

  • You coordinate technical topics between mobile engineering, backend, product ownership, architecture, security and external development partners. You structure open questions, dependencies and risks and prepare technical concepts as well as the basis for decision-making for the involved committees.
  • You develop sophisticated functions for our mobile banking app and accompany them from technical clarification to productive release.
  • Together with the team, you will further develop the mobile architecture and the use of Kotlin Multiplatform.
  • You support other engineers through reviews, professional exchange and joint solving of complex tasks.
  • You strengthen quality and delivery capability through maintainable code, automated tests, CI/CD and reliable release processes.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in the development and regular delivery of productively used mobile apps bring you, ideally in the banking environment. You have already assumed technical coordination responsibility, for example as a senior engineer or technical lead.
  • You bring in-depth knowledge of Kotlin and Android or Swift and iOS and are ready to deal with both platforms in depth.
  • The ability to structure complex questions, bring together different perspectives and document decisions in a comprehensible way characterizes you.
  • Experience with mobile architectures, API integration, asynchronous programming and state management complements your professional profile.
  • Knowledge of automated tests, CI/CD, app releases, mobile security and data protection round off your technical know-how.
  • With your binding nature, you can safely communicate with technical and non-technical stakeholders and enjoy working with different stakeholders.
