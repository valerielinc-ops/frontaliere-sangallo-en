  • The optimization of processes as well as the close cooperation with other areas are also part of your field of responsibility. Your profile
  • You have a University of Applied Sciences degree in Facility Management with a specialization in Hospitality Management or an apprenticeship as an operational manager or Operations manager Facility Management HF combined with appropriate professional experience.
  • Your several years of leadership and management experience distinguishes you, and you convince an empathetic leader with strong leadership skills and high customer orientation.
  • Experience in the Swiss healthcare system is beneficial, especially in the psychiatric environment.
  • You will bring knowledge of reporting and controlling as well as experience in project management.
  • You think entrepreneurially, act solution-oriented and are enthusiastic about quality, innovation and sustainable development.
