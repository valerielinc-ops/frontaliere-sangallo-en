Full description

The public prosecutor’s office serves the population of the canton of St. Gallen and is committed to a fair criminal justice system. As an independent investigative and prosecuting authority, we conduct criminal proceedings in accordance with the principles of the rule of law. At the Gossau site, more than 30 employees are committed to the enforcement of the law and thus make an important contribution to the peace of law in our society.

What to expect

  • You conduct criminal investigations against accused persons and either conclude them with your own order or file charges with the district court.
  • You represent the indictment in district and cantonal court.
  • You're doing picket service.
  • In case of interest and suitability, it is optionally possible to assume a management function.

What distinguishes you

  • You are a flexible, hands-on and resilient personality.
  • You plan and prioritize your work to conduct numerous procedures at the same time.
  • You are programmatically guided by the law and act legally flawlessly.
  • You act faithfully, take responsibility and delegate situationally.
  • You tactically tackle challenges and find targeted solutions.
  • You are decisive and consistently ensure the implementation of your decisions.
  • You work team-oriented and actively participate in the collegial exchange. What you bring
  • Completed legal studies, attorney's patent desired
  • Professional experience in law enforcement
  • Category B driving licence (passenger car)
  • If you are interested in taking on a management position: several years of professional experience

What we offer

  • Young dynamic environment and collaborative cooperation in a team
  • Modern working environment and contemporary employment conditions with flexible working hours
  • Demanding and meaningful activity
  • Regular professional exchange in the team as well as individual coaching by supervisors
  • Place of work in walking distance to Gossau station

Contact: lic. iur. Christian Bächle, Chief Prosecutor a.i., 058 229 94 94
