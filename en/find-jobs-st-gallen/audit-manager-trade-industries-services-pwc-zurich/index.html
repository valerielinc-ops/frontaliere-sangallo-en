Your team

In auditing, we build trust by providing companies with an independent and objective audit of their financial information, processes and controls. In this way, we make an important contribution to the transparency and reliability of financial systems and support sound business decisions. In the area of Trade, Industries & Services, you work with national and international customers from different industries and accompany them with complex issues in a dynamic environment. Together with a diverse team, you use innovative technologies, develop future-oriented solutions and actively shape the future of auditing. Your impact

  • You lead and coordinate audits of annual and consolidated accounts of national and international customers from different industries on site or remotely.
  • You will assess business processes and support customers with challenging accounting issues in accordance with OR, IFRS, Swiss GAAP FER and US GAAP.
  • You carry out special assignments and audits, accompany various project assignments and get involved in the Trade, Industries & Services community.
  • You promote and coach employees, support their professional and personal development and contribute to an inspiring team culture.
  • You drive the digitalization of auditing by integrating modern data analysis and automation tools into auditing processes.

Your skill set

  • You are in possession of a recognised Swiss auditor’s diploma or equivalent qualification such as ACCA, ACA or CPA.
  • You have 4 to 7 years of professional experience in auditing, ideally in the Big Four environment.
  • Strong coaching mentality as well as the joy of passing on knowledge and promoting talents are your priority.
  • You have a high affinity for digital solutions and interest in data analysis and audit tools such as Alteryx, Power BI or similar technologies.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
