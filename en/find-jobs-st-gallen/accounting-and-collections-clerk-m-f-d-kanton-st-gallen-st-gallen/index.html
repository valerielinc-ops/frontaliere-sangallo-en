Full description

The public prosecutor's office with around 250 employees consists of four regional investigative offices, a cantonal investigative office responsible for the entire cantonal territory, the staff services and the youth prosecutor's office. The Public Prosecutor's Office is an independent investigative and prosecuting authority of the Canton of St.Gallen, which is responsible for the uniform enforcement of the state criminal claim and conducts criminal proceedings in the event of crimes, offences and transgressions. Accounting is responsible for collecting claims arising from criminal judgments of the Public Prosecutor’s Office of the Canton of St.Gallen and the St.Gallian courts.

What to expect

Together with the section management and seven employees, you will perform the following tasks in the Central Debt Collection Office:

  • Activation and control of reminder runs
  • Answering telephone and written questions
  • Conclusion and monitoring of partial payment agreements
  • Complete legal collection according to SchKG incl. Bankruptcy and estate proceedings
  • Accounting/processing of receipts
  • Recovery of claims on loss notes
  • Case forwarding for the conversion of fines and fines into substitute prison sentences resp. Non-profit work

  • Companionship with customers in difficult life situations
