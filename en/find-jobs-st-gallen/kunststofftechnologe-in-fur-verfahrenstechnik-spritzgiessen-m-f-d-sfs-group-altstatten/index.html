Full description

Do you bring know-how in plastics processing, enjoy working on optimal processes and have an eye for quality, efficiency and cost-effectiveness? Then you are exactly right with us. Take responsibility for samples, process optimization and the industrialization of our products and bring your ideas directly into production. Your Tasks:

  • Planning and professional implementation of samples in cooperation with the Head of Process Engineering, Head of Production from preparation to implementation
  • Continuous improvement of injection molding processes and workflows with focus on quality, process reliability, cycle time and economy, in particular using systematic and computational methods
  • Setting up and monitoring processes for parts with increased quality requirements or safety-critical products
  • Preparation and maintenance of production documents, production papers and work instructions for serial production and management, management and security of all machine setting data
  • Cooperation in offers and projects as well as support in the development and implementation of new tools
  • Optimization of working documents and information flows between the participating departments as well as proactive sharing of relevant information Your profile:
  • Completed apprenticeship as a plastics technologist
  • Several years of experience and profound knowledge in the plastics industry
  • Additional training or practical knowledge in mechanics and/or tool making are advantageous
  • Ideally, knowledge of ISO/TS 16949, APQP, FMEA, SPC, PPAP and Global 8D
  • Team-oriented, self-reflected and solution-oriented with a motivating role model function
  • Independent, structured and reliable with pronounced organizational talent We offer:
  • Varied area of responsibility as well as space for personal responsibility and initiative
  • Clearly structured training phase with cross-department onboarding program
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual participation in success, numerous sports offers & employee events
  • Other benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
