Full description

The Department of Scholarships and Student Loans supports people in post-compulsory training with cantonal training contributions. It advises applicants and processes around 2,500 applications for scholarships and student loans every year. The Department of Scholarships and Student Loans is part of the Service for Finance and Computer Science in the Department of Education of the Canton of St. Gallen.

What to expect

  • Ensuring the examination of the application within the framework of the legal requirements
  • Leadership and organization of a small team
  • Design and optimization of the digital application processing process
  • Advice to applicants
  • Cooperation in the examination of applications
  • Ensuring information on scholarships and student loans to the public
  • Exchange with other cantons in specialised bodies

What sets it apart

  • Basic commercial education with further education or a degree in economics or law
  • Conceptual and structured thinking
  • Good communication in word and writing
  • High service orientation and pleasure in customer contact
  • Interest in the digitalization of administrative processes

What we offer
