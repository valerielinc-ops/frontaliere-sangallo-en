Full description

The Cantonal Forestry Office is the competence center for all questions about the St.Gall Forest. Together with the five forest regions, it is committed to an efficient, diverse and adaptable forest as well as a sustainable forest and timber industry. The forest regions are active in a wide range of topics – from forest planning and forestry project management to forest protection, forest conservation and forest protection, to biodiversity, natural hazards and public relations. The Cantonal Biodiversity Strategy 2026–2033 will further intensify efforts to promote biodiversity in forests. At the same time, the forest region 3 Sargans faces special challenges in forest rejuvenation and in dealing with the topic of forest wildlife.

What to expect

  • Conception, organisation and administration of forest biodiversity measures (exclusion of forest reserves and waste wood islands, maintenance of forest edges, promotion of biotope trees and dead wood, special projects, etc.)
  • Support for forest regions or Forestry areas in the implementation of the measures
  • Expert advice to stakeholders in the field of forest biodiversity
  • Management and coordination of projects
  • Collection of rejuvenation data
  • Contacts with forest owners and hunting associations, initiation and implementation of projects at the Wald-Wild interface

What sets it apart

  • Degree as a Federally Diplomated Forester, Forest Engineer or comparable training
  • Experience and affinity in forest biodiversity and wildlife
  • Conceptual and networked thinking as well as independent and goal-oriented working methods
  • Strong communication personality with secure oral and written expression as well as skill in dealing with forest owners, hunters and other stakeholders
  • GIS knowledge is beneficial
  • Driving licence Cat. B

What we offer

  • High level of independence and personal responsibility
  • Interesting, responsible and varied activity
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated and collaborative team
  • Flexible working time models and flexible working hours
  • Comprehensive range of training opportunities
