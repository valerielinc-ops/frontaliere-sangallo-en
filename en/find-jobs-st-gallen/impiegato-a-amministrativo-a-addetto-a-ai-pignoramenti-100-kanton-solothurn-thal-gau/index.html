Full description

The Thal-Gäu Management Office performs the legal task of foreclosure for monetary claims and guarantees. For the Thal-Gäu Management Office we are looking for a clerk at the Balsthal site as of 01.04.2027 or by agreement, 100% seizures.

Your responsibility

  • They work in the enforcement service of the seizure and exploitation proceedings.
  • They shall serve enforcement documents and enforce attachments at the Office or at the debtor.
  • They execute wage, property and real estate attachments, carry out exploitations, arrests, retentions as well as official custody and advise customers in matters of management law.

Your profile

  • They have a commercial apprenticeship degree or an equivalent education, good IT skills and advantageously already have experience in debt recovery law as well as good foreign language skills.
  • The ability to assert oneself in the field of tension, an independent, structured and efficient working method as well as a sense of responsibility, secure appearance and negotiating skills, resilience and teamwork round off your profile.
  • You have a valid driving licence (category B).
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
