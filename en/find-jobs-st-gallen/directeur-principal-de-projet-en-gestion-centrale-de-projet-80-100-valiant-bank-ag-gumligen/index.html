Full description

Simplify together. Because life is full of possibilities. At the same time, it became complex. That is why we are looking for employees who work with us to promote simplicity.

They shall

  • lead strategic overall banking projects (including regulatory projects)
  • assume overall responsibility for all project phases, including project budget, goal definition and controlling
  • Plan, coordinate and lead interdisciplinary project teams
  • Develop project orders and project descriptions in coordination with the relevant stakeholders (level of business or department management)
  • Create decision-making bases, status reports and management reviews for the project board and management
  • continuously participate in the professionalization of the structures and instruments of project management
  • Become part of Valiant’s Project Portfolio Management and Controlling (PPMC) team

They bring

  • several years of practical experience in the management and implementation of complex projects, ideally in the banking environment
  • Appropriate training at university/specialist level and project-specific training(s)
  • Expertise in the application of waterfall and agile project models
  • Curiosity and openness also in the implementation of regulatory projects in the banking environment
  • High resilience and performance as well as excellent analytical and conceptual skills
  • Pronounced power of persuasion with assertiveness as well as high independence, initiative and reliability
  • German mother tongue or advanced German language level (minimum C1)

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1200 people – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
