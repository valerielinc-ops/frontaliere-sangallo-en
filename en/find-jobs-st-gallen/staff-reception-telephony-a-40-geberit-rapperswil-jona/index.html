Full description

MAIN TASKS

You are the first port of call for our visitors, customers and business partners and provide a positive first impression with your friendly and professional manner. As a central hub at the reception and in the telephony, you contribute every day to ensuring that internal and external contacts feel in good hands at Geberit. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • You receive and care for visitors, customers and business partners professionally and warmly.
  • You serve the telephone exchange and make incoming and outgoing calls for various Geberit companies.
  • You communicate with internal and external contacts in German, English, French and ideally Italian.
  • You take on a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks.
  • You help coordinate meetings and ensure that the reception runs smoothly.

Ideally, you’ll work Mondays all day as well as Thursdays and Fridays in the afternoon and be flexible for additional assignments during holidays.

PROFILE

You have a completed commercial basic training or a comparable training and feel comfortable in a service-oriented function with many interfaces. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • Experience in reception, customer service or administration is an advantage
  • Pronounced service orientation and a safe, well-kept appearance
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Organizational talent as well as the ability to maintain an overview even in a lively environment
  • Joy in dealing with people and strong communication skills
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office applications; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good German, English and French skills, Italian skills are a plus

ADDITIONAL COMMENTS

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
