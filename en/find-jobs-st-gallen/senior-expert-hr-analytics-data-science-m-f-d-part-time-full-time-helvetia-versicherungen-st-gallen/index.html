Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • You will design and develop HR Analytics, Data Science and Business Intelligence solutions in close cooperation with the Group
  • You create meaningful dashboards, reporting, forecasts and decision bases including various projections
  • You analyze HR data, identify trends and derive concrete recommendations for action
  • You define and harmonize key figures, data models and calculation logics
  • You ensure the quality, consistency and availability of HR data
  • You advise HR, executives and management and work closely with finance, IT and data management
  • You are responsible for ad-hoc analyses and executive reports and create presentations for senior management and executives
  • You support compensation reporting and compensation analytics as well as analyses of wage developments and equal pay topics
  • Identify new analytics and automation potential and promote the use of data science, machine learning and artificial intelligence in HR

Requirements

  • University degree in Data Science, Business Informatics, Business Administration, Statistics, Mathematics or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of experience in HR Analytics, People Analytics, Data Science, HR Controlling or Business Intelligence
  • Practice in the development of dashboards and KPI systems, for example with Power BI or SAP Analytics Cloud. Excel skills are also excellent
  • In-depth knowledge in statistical analyses and data-based decision models
  • Strong consulting skills and the ability to communicate complex results in an understandable way
  • Experience in personnel cost planning, system introductions or compensation & benefits is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
