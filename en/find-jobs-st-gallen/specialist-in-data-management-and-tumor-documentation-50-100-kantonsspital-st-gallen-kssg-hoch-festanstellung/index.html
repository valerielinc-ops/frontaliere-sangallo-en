Specialist in Data Management and Tumor Documentation 50-100 %

Medical Oncology/Hematology

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Collection, maintenance and plausibility testing of clinical and oncological patient data in accordance with DKG requirements
  • Preparation and evaluation of key figures for DKG certifications, audits and quality reports
  • Preparation of statistics, evaluations and overviews for quality assurance and further development of the centres
  • Researching and supplementing missing findings, treatment courses and follow-up data
  • Participation in standardizing documentation processes and ensuring data quality

What you bring for this job

  • Completed medical, nursing, health science or documentary training, ideally with experience in oncological documentation
  • Safe handling of computer systems and MS Office
  • Careful, structured and independent working method
  • High awareness of quality and responsibility as well as strong team and communication skills
  • Cross-entry possible

Our offer to you

  • A responsible and versatile job in a committed, dynamic team
  • Active design options for the development and further development of tumor documentation in the center
  • Structured familiarisation and continuous technical support
  • Diverse internal training opportunities
  • Attractive remuneration according to the guidelines of the public service with additional retirement provision and flexible working time models to promote work-life balance
