Full description

Your tasks

  • You lead stationary sports and movement groups as well as various central movement offers in adult and age psychiatry, e.g. medical training therapy (MTT), Nordic walking, game sports and body perception offers.
  • You carry out individual therapies with our patients and promote specific physical, emotional and social resources.
  • In case of professional suitability and interest, it is possible to work with patients in the forensic setting.
  • You plan independently, document and evaluate your therapeutic services.
  • If appropriately suited and interested, it is possible to take on temporary tasks in the management of the therapy team. Your profile
  • You have completed studies in physiotherapy or sports or movement sciences. A foreign diploma requires SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck.
  • Passion and joy in movement and sport distinguish you.
  • Experience in working with psychiatric clinical pictures and psychosomatic connections you ideally bring with you. You are particularly interested in working with patients in the forensic-psychiatric setting.
  • Independent work is just as important to you as initiative, flexibility and a solution-oriented approach.
  • The cooperation in the team and the interprofessional exchange are an enrichment for you.
  • With your motivating and appreciative nature, you succeed in getting patients excited about exercise and helping them actively use their own resources.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German at level C1 round off your profile.
