Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a Dipl to strengthen our team. Care specialist HF/FH surveillance care paediatrics.

Their tasks

  • Caring for infants from 2 months of age, children and adolescents and their caregivers at the IMC
  • Responsible for securing and monitoring patients after an operation or complex care situation
  • Early detection and management of acute and emergency situations in direct cooperation with the medical service and the dipl. Expert/intensive care
  • Initiation and design of patient reports, nursing discussions and interprofessional case discussions
  • Documentation and implementation of the entire care process (ePPD) as well as validation of electronic patient documentation
  • Recording of services using LEP® and SAP MM (care material and medicines)
  • Active contribution to a smooth daily routine

Your profile

  • Diploma as Dipl. Nurse HF or FH with a focus on paediatrics (SRC recognition in foreign diplomas)
  • Professional experience in caring for infants, children and adolescents
  • Continuing NDK surveillance course paediatrics or existing interest to complete it
  • Safe, cooperative and flexible action in an interdisciplinary professional field
  • Enjoyment of handling medical-technical devices

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together

Your work area Station 1 has 41 beds and combines neonatology, intermediate care (IMC) and the interdisciplinary pediatric and neonatal intensive care unit, divided into two teams (IPS/IMC and NEO1/NEO2). At the interdisciplinary/paediatric intermediate care we care for children from the 2nd month of life with increased monitoring and care needs. This includes patients with medical, surgical and orthopaedic diseases. This means that you can expect a versatile and demanding working environment with a wide range of paediatric treatments. We work closely and interprofessionally with the pediatric and neonatological intensive care unit. Our actions are focused on children and their families. We actively involve parents and relatives in the care and care process and accompany them individually, sensitively and competently. A holistic, family-oriented care and an appreciative cooperation are a matter of course for us.

Further information about the department can be found here: Station 1
