Full description

As a Process Engineer in the Wheat & Rye business, you will immerse yourself in the heart of the global grain and food industry. You will commission state-of-the-art milling plants worldwide, experience different countries and cultures, work closely with our customers, share your expertise through training, and directly contribute to ensuring that mills around the world operate efficiently, safely and to the highest quality standards.

Aufgaben

  • You independently commission new machines and milling plants worldwide and ensure their successful handover to our customers
  • You train and advise our customers and their employees, conveying technological and process engineering expertise in a practical way
  • You conduct mill inspections and provide additional services for existing plants, systematically identifying opportunities to improve process-related performance indicators
  • Based on individual customer requirements, you develop technological processes and process diagrams and support their implementation
  • You support Sales, Quotation Management, Project Management, Engineering, Automation, R&D and Product Management with technological and process-related questions

Anforderungen

  • Completed vocational training as a grain miller or process technologist in the milling and animal feed industry, supplemented by further training as a master craftsperson, technician or an equivalent qualification, ideally from the German School of Milling in Braunschweig or the Swiss School of Milling in St. Gallen
  • Several years of experience in the operation and maintenance of grain mills, combined with basic knowledge of electrical engineering and a strong affinity for automation
  • Good German and English skills (at least B1), together with strong communication skills when working with different stakeholder groups; additional foreign languages are an advantage
  • Strong customer and service orientation, a high degree of personal responsibility, and confidence using modern digital tools, particularly Microsoft 365
  • High degree of flexibility and willingness to travel worldwide (over 60%), as well as openness and enjoyment in working with people from different cultures
