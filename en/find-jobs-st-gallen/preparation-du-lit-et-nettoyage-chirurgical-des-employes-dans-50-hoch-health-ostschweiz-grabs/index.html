Your tasks and perspectives

  • Preparation of the beds on the stations and completion of the associated work
  • Preparation and sorting of laundry as well as their reliable delivery and sorting directly on the various stations

What you bring for this job

  • Clean, thorough, exact and independent work

  • Good physical and psychological condition for physically demanding work
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German (level B1/B2) as well as solid basic PC knowledge
