Full description

Smart stores, innovative business models and the trade of the future inspire you? In our team, we are driving the further development of Migros teo and other smart store concepts. We combine innovation, technology and customer benefit to create new shopping experiences. If you not only want to watch, but also actively participate, we look forward to your application.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Gossau headquarters, Industriestrasse 47, 9200 Gossau

Mansioni

  • You are actively working on the further development of innovative Smart Store projects, such as Migros teo and our food vending machines.
  • You analyze key figures, develop dashboards and reports and derive concrete recommendations for action for team management and the team.
  • You support the project manager in the assortment management of the food vending machines, submit your own optimization suggestions and take part in negotiations.
  • You will contribute to the development of new solutions to improve the customer experience and the operational excellence of our smart stores (e.g. prevention of waste and lootering, including video viewing).
  • You document existing processes according to applicable standards and continuously identify potential for improvement.
  • You take responsibility for your own sub-projects as well as various ad hoc orders and bring your ideas into an innovative and dynamic environment.

Requirements

  • Initial professional experience: in the area of project management, process management and/or retail of advantage.
  • Bachelor's degree (university, university of applied sciences): You are currently enrolled in a master's degree programme.
  • You like to work in a team, convince through your authentic appearance and bring a positive mindset.
  • You enjoy analysing complex issues in a dynamic environment and developing pragmatic solutions for our customers.
  • You work independently, structured and efficient, take responsibility and distinguish yourself through initiative and a quick understanding.
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately and enjoy learning new things.
  • You have very good user knowledge in Microsoft 365 and use modern AI tools sovereignly and responsibly.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
