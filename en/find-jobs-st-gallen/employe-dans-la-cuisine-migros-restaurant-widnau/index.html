Full description

The gastronomy of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether Migros restaurant, Migros take away, shared gastronomy or catering services – we offer every customer a tasteful experience.

Mansioni

  • You help to prepare daily barbecue dishes and cold seasonal foods that bring our guests joy and pleasure.
  • You support, advise and serve our customers in the restaurant and at the cash desk with professionalism and cordiality.
  • You present the dishes in the salad and cake buffet creatively and matching the time of day and the season to offer guests a visual and culinary experience.
  • You make sure that the hygiene regulations according to food safety are adhered to and consistently implemented.

Requirements

  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience: in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further training: in gastronomy
  • You love being in touch with people and enjoy getting our guests excited.
  • You enjoy working in a team, being reliable and supporting your colleagues.
  • You are a team player, flexible and tackle everywhere.
  • Independent and accurate work is a matter of course for you.
  • German (very good knowledge)
