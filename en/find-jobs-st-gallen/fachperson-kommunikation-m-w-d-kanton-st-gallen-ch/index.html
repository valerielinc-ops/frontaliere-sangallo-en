Professional communication (m/f/d) — Kanton St. Gallen

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The General Secretariat of the Department of the Interior supports the head of the department in her leading role with services in the areas of administration, law, finance, computer science, personnel and communications and performs essential cross-sectional functions for the whole department.

What to expect

  • You plan and coordinate the internal and external communication of the department with the competent authorities and the department management.
  • You write and edit target group-oriented content for various communication channels.
  • During presentations, speeches and other communication tasks, you support and advise the department management.
  • You will be involved in organizing events and taking on a variety of coordination tasks.
  • You accompany administrative and political processes around government business.

What distinguishes you

  • You have a bachelor’s degree in communication or a commercial basic education with EFZ and a federal specialist card as a PR specialist or a federal diploma as a PR consultant.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience in communication, in a comparable function or in project and coordination work.
  • You have a strong sense of language and enjoy preparing challenging content in an understandable, precise and addressable manner.
  • You can quickly think into a wide range of topics and thus critically question texts and procedures.
  • You are a politically minded person and can quickly familiarize yourself with the structures and processes of our canton.
  • You work independently, structured and service-oriented and have a secure feeling for different stakeholders.

What we offer

  • Many meaningful topics and an inspiring working atmosphere at the pulse of socio-political developments
  • A development-oriented working environment and an appreciative cooperation
  • A workplace in the heart of the UNESCO World Heritage district of St.Gallen - a unique symbiosis of historical architecture and modern administration.
  • In the General Secretariat, you will work at the interface to the offices in the Department of the Interior as well as to the departmental management and government councillor and thus gain direct insights into political events as well as into decision-making processes and developments.
  • The job is temporary until approx. October 2027. The possibility of continued employment will be examined in early summer 2027.

Contact: Department of the Interior, General Secretariat
