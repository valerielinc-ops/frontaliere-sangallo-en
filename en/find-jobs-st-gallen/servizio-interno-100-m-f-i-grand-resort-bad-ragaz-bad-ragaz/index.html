Tasks

  • Operation of dishwashers and kitchen utensil machines (Casserole)
  • Careful settling of tableware, cutlery, glasses and cassettes
  • General basic and maintenance cleaning in the kitchen, office and connecting corridors according to hygiene regulations
  • Provision of dishes, cutlery and glasses for the service

Requirements

  • Independent, careful and team-oriented working method
  • Readiness for flexible assignments on weekends and public holidays
  • First professional experience in the field of office or cleaning, ideally in a hotel or catering company is an advantage
  • Communication in German or English

We offer

  • Free Tickets Tamina Gorge
  • Test sleep after passing the test period

  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origins in the healing thermal water of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place stands for regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis, we continue to develop the resort consistently – with the aim of becoming the leading Wellbeing & Healthy Living Resort in Europe and creating a place of strength for generations.

Around 800 employees shape the experience of our guests every day. Different talents, cultures and personalities work together on one goal: to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common aspiration to make this special place a bit better every day – One Resort – One Team.
