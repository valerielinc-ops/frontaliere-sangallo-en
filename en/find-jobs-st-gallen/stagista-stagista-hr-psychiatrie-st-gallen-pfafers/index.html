Full description

You want to immerse yourself in the HR world, experience recruiting up close and actively participate from the beginning? Then you are exactly right with us! As part of our HRM team, you will gain valuable practical experience, take on responsibility and gain versatile insights into the exciting world of human resources. Your tasks

  • You shape the first impression: From the creation of appealing advertisements to the viewing of application documents to the first personal contact with our applicants – by phone and email.
  • With professional and appreciative communication, you ensure a positive candidate experience – even in the event of cancellations.
  • You are actively involved in the organization of our check-in days for new employees and independently take over individual program points.
  • You answer questions from applicants and support us with potential candidates via social media, email or telephone.
  • You support our HR Business Partners in administrative tasks and in day-to-day operations.
  • You will learn about different HR processes and gain valuable practical experience in areas such as onboarding and employer branding.
  • If you are interested and suitable, you will also participate in an exciting project for the further development of our applicant management. Your profile
  • You are currently completing a bachelor's or master's degree and are interested in HR, especially recruiting.
  • You are communicative, open and enjoy contact with different people.
  • You work independently, structured and reliable, show your own initiative and like to take part.
  • You have a quick apprehension, enjoy working on new topics and keep an overview even in lively situations.
  • You are adept at using Microsoft Office and enjoy working with digital tools.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1). With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second step, we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also have the opportunity to get to know your future team. After our discussions, we will make the final decision, which we will tell you personally, whereby we will also pick up your decision. If we agree, we will draw up your employment contract. In the following days you will receive your employment contract electronically. With the contract, you will also receive relevant information about your employment and the day of introduction.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
