Full description

Are you familiar with wood, have a good technical understanding and enjoy working with production facilities? For our customer in 9200 Gossau SG we are looking for a plant operator for the sorting and wedge zinc plant in permanent employment. Then this place could suit you exactly. Your tasks

  • Sorting and wedge zinc systems operated and monitored
  • Set up and convert production facilities
  • Sorted wood by quality
  • Detect wood defects and sort out defective wooden parts
  • Monitor production processes and ensure quality
  • Observing compliance with occupational safety

You bring that with you

  • Training or professional experience in the wood sector
  • Familiarity with wood and its properties
  • Technical understanding and interest in production facilities
  • Reliable working methods and quality awareness
  • Joy of practical work and machine operation
  • Flowing knowledge of German
  • Readiness for shift work

You can look forward to that.

  • Long-term permanent employment in Gossau SG
  • Varied activities between machine operation, wood sorting and quality control
  • Working with sawn wood and various production facilities
  • Possibility to use your technical skills in setting up and converting
  • Self-responsible work with direct influence on the quality of the wood products
