Full description

Tasks: As an ICT system specialist, you ensure the stable operation of the entire IT infrastructure. This includes, inter alia, the following core tasks:

  • Ensuring the entire operation of the IT system and application landscape with around 350 clients and about 120 servers incl. Network, Server, Active Directory and Data Center
  • Main responsible support and further development of our M365 environment
  • Ensuring IT security / cybersecurity using Microsoft Defender and other tools such as ZPE Zapp, Burpsuite and Pincastle
  • Planning and execution of cloud migrations
  • Independent receipt and processing of support requests in 2nd level support
  • Further development, continuous optimization and automation of existing systems and applications
  • Monitoring, coordination and support of interfaces between different systems
  • Administration and management of the entire IT infrastructure including monitoring, security and backup with focus on a modern and secure infrastructure and system landscape
  • Planning and implementation of IT projects
  • Assumption of piquetting services to ensure accessibility and handling of urgent issues outside regular working hours Requirements You have completed an apprenticeship in computer science and bring proven experience in IT infrastructure and IT support. It motivates and inspires you to actively shape our cloud migrations. In addition, you have:
  • Completed computer science platform development EFZ or comparable training with relevant further education
  • Several years of professional experience in the computer science environment with a focus on M365 and experience in support
  • In-depth expertise in the Windows environment (on-prem server, active directory and clients as well as Azure Cloud with EntraID and Intune) as well as network infrastructure, virtualization and Linux.
  • Experience in cyber security as well as knowledge of common security solutions and tools such as Microsoft Defender, Burp Suite or PingCastle
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented as well as cooperative working methods and high resilience
  • Communication strength and safe handling of different stakeholders
