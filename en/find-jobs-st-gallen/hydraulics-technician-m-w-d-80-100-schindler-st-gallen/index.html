Moving up one floor together as a Hydraulics Technician (m/f/d) 80-100% Are you enthusiastic about technical systems and enjoy complex hydraulic systems? In our Hydraulic Service Center (HSC), which is centralized throughout Switzerland, we, as specialists in hydraulic elevators, ensure that our hydraulic elevators run reliably. With your technical skills and your willingness to find solutions, you will take on repairs and conversions to keep the systems in top shape. Become part of a team that masters challenges with passion and creates sustainable added value for our customers. Your new challenges:

  • With your expertise in repairs and modifications, you will replace mechanical, hydraulic and electrical components on hydraulic lift systems.
  • Replacement of control and hydraulic components in the elevator shaft and machine room
  • Your dexterity and technical understanding are fully utilized when assembling and commissioning elevator hydraulics.
  • If there is a problem, you tackle it: you analyze problems, find solutions and get the systems going again
  • You have an eye for the essentials and carry out condition analyzes and inventory of existing elevator systems
  • You are the first contact person for our customers during your work on site
  • Together we will continue to optimize! You support the continuous improvement process of the growing hydraulic service center and the internal processes with your ideas and know-how. Your ideal requirements:
  • With your independent and structured way of working. You take on responsibility and have the right to deliver clean work.
  • With your solution orientation and your focus on service. You always have the goal in mind of finding the best solutions for our customers.
  • With your commitment and drive to push things forward and continually improve them.
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship in the field of hydraulics, agricultural machinery mechanics, mechanics, agricultural mechanics or electrical engineering.
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the field of hydraulics or elevator technology: you know how to keep an overview even under pressure.
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland: you love variety and new challenges locally.
  • You are an independent and reliable personality with a high level of initiative
  • Enjoy dealing with customers
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Passenger car ticket (Cat. B) Our benefits:
