Full description

The St.Gallen Investigation Office, located directly at St.Gallen Central Station, is responsible for criminal proceedings in the area from the city of St.Gallen to the airport of St.Gallen-Altenrhein (court districts of St.Gallen and Rorschach). 18 prosecutors, 11 clerks with legal powers, 8 assistants and 5 persons work in training or internships. Together, we ensure efficient and legal prosecution in the region.

What you're expecting

  • You are responsible for criminal proceedings arising from the general criminal law (StGB) and ancillary criminal law (in particular AIG, BetmG, SVG and WG) – from the case to the conclusion of the proceedings, where a fine is likely to be considered as a fine, a fine or a prison sentence of no more than six months.
  • You are conducting interviews with accused persons, information persons and witnesses and providing the necessary evidence.
  • You are concluding criminal proceedings, in particular by means of a criminal order, an order for recruitment or a non-approach order.

What makes you special

  • You have completed your training and have several years of professional experience.
  • A diploma as a legal specialist HF or Kriminal police experience is an advantage.
  • You convince by your resilience, integrity and personal maturity.
  • You work precisely, efficiently and independently.

What we offer

  • A dedicated team with an open and appreciative working culture awaits you.
  • You take on a responsible and socially relevant activity.
  • We offer attractive employment conditions, contemporary working models and good development opportunities.

Contact: Carmen Zaugg, Head of Group and Attorney General, 058 229 40 07
