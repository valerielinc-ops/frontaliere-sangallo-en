Full description

The Migration Office operates daily in the exciting political environment of all migration issues. We are responsible for the permits under foreigners law, enforce the Swiss alien and asylum law, issue the biometric Swiss passports and foreigner ID cards and provide care and integration in our asylum centres.

What to expect

  • Issuance and extension of residence permits and border workers
  • Extension and mutation of foreign national permits of different categories
  • Processing of customer concerns in personal contact
  • Written, telephone and personal contact with customers, authorities and legal representatives
  • Further development within the specialist career with the opportunity to expand the area of responsibility and gradually assume additional responsibility

What sets it apart

  • Completed commercial training (profile E or M), preferably in public administration
  • Professional experience in the administrative field and interest in the field of foreigners law
  • Open, independent and responsible personality
  • Strong teamwork skills and very good IT user knowledge

What we offer

  • Interesting and versatile activity with varied issues in foreigners law
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Centrally located workplace in the heart of St.Gallen
  • Modern canteen with healthy and varied catering offers
  • Discounted East Wind subscription for your mobility

Contact: For further information, Dario Cellere, head of department, is at your disposal on 058 229 31 91. Further information about the Migration Office and our tasks can be found at www.migrationsamt.sg.ch .
