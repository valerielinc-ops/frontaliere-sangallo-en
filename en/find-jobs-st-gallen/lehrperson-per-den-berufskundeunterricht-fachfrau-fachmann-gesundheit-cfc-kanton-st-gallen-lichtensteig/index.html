Full description

The Vocational and Continuing Education Centre Toggenburg (BWZT) is an important educational institution in Eastern Switzerland. Around 1,200 apprentices in the occupational areas of health/social affairs, food/gastronomy and construction/energy acquire their vocational, school basic education at the BWZT. The bridge offers and further education offers of the Vocational Academy complete our educational offer. What to expect:

  • Planning and implementation of contemporary, practical and competence-oriented specialist teaching
  • Active co-creation of training programmes and teaching materials in collaboration with the team
  • Individual support and support for learners
  • Participation in team meetings, training and school development projects What sets it apart:
  • Technical/university degree in nursing, at least HF degree
  • Degree as vocational trainer (SVEB 1, SVEB 2, FA or equivalent)
  • Ideally, training and teaching experience as a vocational school teacher or the willingness to acquire the appropriate pedagogical qualification
  • Enjoyment in the provision of expertise and in accompanying young people and young adults
  • Creativity, commitment and didactic skill in teaching design
  • High level of social, communication and team competence
  • Very good IT user knowledge (Microsoft 365, Moodle or comparable learning platforms) What we offer: We offer an independent, responsible and varied activity in a lively vocational school. It awaits you a school and work climate characterized by appreciation with open doors and short decision-making paths. A motivated team will actively support you.
