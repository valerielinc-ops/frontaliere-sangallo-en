Full description

From the feasibility study to the successful realization: You control complex construction projects with competence, vision and passion. If you like to take on responsibility, coordinate different stakeholder groups and lead projects to success, you are exactly right with us.

Mansioni

  • You assume overall responsibility for the development and realization of complex and demanding new buildings and repair projects.
  • It is easy for you to reconcile the diverse needs of users, companies and investors - Chapeau!
  • You will take the lead in reviewing the feasibility and profitability and lead the interdisciplinary planning teams to success.
  • As an experienced construction specialist, you create cost calculations corresponding to the project phase. You plausibleize the construction costs, assess construction structures and recognize the critical path in construction processes.
  • The management of an effective project quality management is familiar to you and important - us too.
  • Of course, know You are in submission and contracting and are a fair negotiation professional.
  • All the threads come together: You maintain contact with planners, authorities and neighborhoods as well as all internal project participants with your winning nature.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in the main responsibility as head of demanding construction projects, ideally with profound knowledge in the client-side construction project management are shown in your CV.
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): as a construction project manager or architect with specialisation in construction project management or an equivalent education.
  • Service-oriented action as well as a sovereign, self-assured and authentic appearance with respectful assertiveness and a good ability to conflict characterize you.
  • If you add a healthy pinch of humor, you fit perfectly into our team.
  • If you are highly motivated to bring your dynamic personality and your own initiative into our versatile project teams, then contact us!
  • German (very good knowledge)
