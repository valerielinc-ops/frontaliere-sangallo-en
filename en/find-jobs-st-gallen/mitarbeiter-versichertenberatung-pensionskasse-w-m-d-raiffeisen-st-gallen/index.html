Full description

03.09.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The Raiffeisen Pension Fund is responsible for the occupational provision for the more than 200 affiliated employers of the Raiffeisen Group throughout Switzerland. It is a legally independent unit with management, board of directors and a meeting of delegates as the highest body. To complement our customer-oriented team in St. Gallen (near railway station), with the possibility of home office, we are looking for a motivated, team-capable and communicative employee insured consultancy (m/f/d) with admission by arrangement.

What do you expect?

  • You independently and professionally advise our insured in German and ideally in another national language
  • You independently process the pension transactions of our insured persons
  • They support the further development of processes and digital channels
  • We support you in your personal and professional development, on the job and with internal and external training and further education.

What do you bring?

  • You have completed a higher education (business/law) or a further education in the field of BVG or social insurance
  • You will ideally bring professional experience in the field of BVG or social insurance
  • You have ideally good French or Italian skills
  • You are a motivated personality who wants to develop further in the field of occupational pensions
  • You have a flair for digital systems and data as well as good analytical and conceptual skills

“We are a professional pension institution with around 15,000 insured persons, which places a strong focus on insurance consulting and further drives the digitalization of business processes.” Ueli Brunner Head of insurance consultancy Why Raiffeisen?
