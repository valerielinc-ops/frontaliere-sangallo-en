HR Trainee (80 - 100%) HR Analytics and Compensation

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Analysis and preparation of remuneration data for wage rounds, market comparisons, equal pay analyses, benchmarks and other compensation issues
  • Participation in HR analyses on workforce structures, fluctuation, recruitment or other personnel-relevant issues
  • Creation and further development of dashboards, reports and self-service solutions
  • Ensuring data quality and documentation of key figures and calculation logics
  • Optimization and automation of data processes using new analysis and AI tools

What you bring for this job

  • Bachelor degree (business administration) at a university or university of applied sciences (degree within the last two years)
  • Very good analytical skills, pleasure in numbers and real interest in HR, compensation and business issues
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High willingness to learn and joy of working together in a team
  • Trustworthy and responsible handling of sensitive personnel and remuneration data

Our offer to you

  • Insight into different HR and management issues of a large healthcare company - Professional experience in HR is not absolutely necessary
  • Building broad analytics and HR competencies
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various Fringe Benefits (reduced internal catering, sports and health courses as well as prevention offers as part of the "gsundHOCH2" campaign)
  • Permanent employment option under the trainee programme
