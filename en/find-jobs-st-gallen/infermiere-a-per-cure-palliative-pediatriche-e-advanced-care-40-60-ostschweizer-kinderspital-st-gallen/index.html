Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a nurse for paediatric palliative and advanced care (40-60%) to join the paediatric palliative and advanced care team.

Their tasks

  • Care for children, adolescents and their families with complex chronic illnesses within an interdisciplinary treatment team
  • Accompanying, advising and coordinating families in demanding situations involving all relevant specialists and institutions (e.g. paediatricians, paediatricians, therapists and other care partners)
  • Promoting the quality of life of the child or Young people and their family as well as participation in complex decision-making processes according to the principle of shared decision making
  • Moderation of round tables and helper conferences
  • Co-responsibility for symptom management as well as early recognition, triagulation and support of families in acute crisis situations, including the organisation of home visits and other necessary interventions
  • Preparation of planned hospitalizations and support of care transitions, especially when leaving the home environment
  • Creation of individual, multi-professional care, emergency and care plans for coordinated care throughout the network
  • Co-responsibility for the 24/7 rotating picture service with competent telephone emergency advice in close cooperation with the medical background service

Your profile

  • Dipl. Nurse HF or FH as well as several years of experience in the pediatric field
  • Further training in paediatric palliative care (CAS, MAS or equivalent training) desired
  • Willingness to support the team’s 24/7 piketting service (with a medical background)
  • An established, reflected personality characterized by a high self-responsible ability to act
  • Ability to act quickly, calmly and structured in acute emergency medical situations
  • Strong ability to observe changes in the child / adolescent
  • Empathic communication and organizational talent are required
  • Driving licence and own vehicle required

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together
  • Tension, because with a view of the new building (2026) a turbulent time will come

Your work area Palliative care for children and adolescents means the active and comprehensive care of children and adolescents with a life-limiting or life-threatening disease. It encompasses all aspects of medicine, care and accompaniment and is an expression of an attitude towards life and the person, which should not be limited to the immediate end of life.
