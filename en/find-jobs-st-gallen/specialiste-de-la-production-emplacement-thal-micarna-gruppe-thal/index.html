Full description

Micarna, part of the Migros Group, stands for high-quality food, modern production and reliable processes. In Thal, dedicated teams with a high level of expertise ensure the smooth operation of our meat processing plants. To strengthen our maintenance team, we are looking for a motivated, practice-oriented personality in daytime service. Mansioni

  • You ensure the technical availability of production, infrastructure and energy supply facilities.
  • You perform mechanical maintenance, maintenance and repair work on machinery and equipment.
  • You analyze disturbances systematically, remedy them sustainably and support low-disturbance production.
  • You are actively involved in technical projects as well as in optimizations and improvements in the company.
  • You recognize weaknesses in the plant environment and implement measures to increase availability.
  • You live an open communication and inform the responsible authorities at an early stage in the event of major outages.
  • Requisiti
  • You ideally have 3 to 5 years of practical maintenance experience in a production environment: and you can prioritize and work efficiently and independently in a stressful environment.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the mechanical field
  • You have technical understanding and knowledge of machines, plants and material properties.
  • You ideally have a permit according to NIV 13 resp. NIV 15 as well as the driving license of the Cat. B" and also bring good MS-Office knowledge.
  • You take a structured, analytical and solution-oriented approach and know how to safely handle various measuring and working devices. You work responsibly, team-oriented and consistently comply with safety, hygiene and operational requirements.
  • You are willing to work during the day service, usually from 09:00 to about 18:30, to perform the pikettdienst according to planning and to take on occasional weekend assignments by arrangement.
  • German (proof of negotiation)
