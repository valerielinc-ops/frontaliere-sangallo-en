Full description

Are you caring and enjoy looking after small children? Are you responsible and able to keep an overview even with a larger group of children? Then we look forward to your application!

Tasks

  • Childcare for young children up to 9 years old of our members
  • Play, craft, paint and sing with the children
  • Ensure cleanliness and order in the Kinderparadies
  • Be creative and help with decorating the Kinderparadies

Requirements

  • Experience with children: up to 9 years old or own children
  • Completed basic or further training: education in care is an advantage
  • You are a cheerful and empathetic person
  • You live in the region (commute should not exceed 20 minutes)
  • You are flexible regarding working hours and willing to help out on weekends, public holidays or during holiday absences
  • You have a valid work permit
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Central workplace: Our studios are located in well-connected central locations
  • Mobile subscription: National flat-rate subscription from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom / digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Fee-free private and investment account with preferential interest rates
  • Employee offers: Various discounts in the area of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 10%, Generali 25%, Axa Insurance up to 15%, etc.)
  • "Du" culture: We stand for respectful cooperation at eye level
