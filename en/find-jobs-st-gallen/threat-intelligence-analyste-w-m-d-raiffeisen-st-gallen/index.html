Full description

31.08.2026 St. Gallen Zurich

Become part of our team!

As a threat intelligence analyst, you analyze and evaluate the current and future cyber threat situation. You identify relevant attacker groups, campaigns and trends, derive recommendations for action and support the continuous development of our cyber security strategy. For our Cyber Intelligence & Response Team at the locations of Zurich Airport (The Circle) and St. Gallen, we offer a new role as Threat Intelligence Analyst (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Analysis and evaluation of current cyber threats, attack campaigns and TTPs at operational and strategic level.
  • Observation and interpretation of the national and international cyber threat landscape.
  • Further development and establishment of Cyber Threat Intelligence at Raiffeisen.
  • Identification of risks and derivation of measures to strengthen the security facility.
  • Working closely with CSIRT, threat hunting and other security teams to detect and defend against threats.
  • Creation of situation assessments, threat reports and decision-making bases for relevant stakeholders.

What do you bring?

  • Completed computer science or completed degree in computer science.
  • Several years of experience in IT security or cyber security
  • Knowledge of threat intelligence, red teaming or pentesting is an advantage
  • Further education or certification in the field of cyber security are advantageous
  • Analytical, independent and goal-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German and English at least level B2 in word and writing. German is the main working language.
