Graduate Nurse Postgraduate Studies (NDS) Intensive Care Nursing 80-100%

Clinic for Surgical Intensive Care Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and prospects

  • Varied and challenging work in a dynamic environment
  • Independent, competent and high-quality care and nursing of patients requiring intensive care, taking into account current care concepts and standards
  • Structured and individual induction
  • Competent guidance and support during further training by an experienced training team
  • Care of caregivers and relatives

What you bring to the table for this position

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF / FH or a recognised foreign diploma in nursing
  • At least 6 months of practical experience in the acute care sector
  • Empathetic attitude, resilience, flexibility, team spirit and humor
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Resilience, a flair for technical matters and autonomous action

Our offer to you

  • Individual support and part-time postgraduate studies in intensive care with financial security, cost coverage and reimbursement options
  • Leave of absence for school blocks and paid self-study days
  • Shift compensation days to compensate in three-shift operation
  • Various offers for employees in the areas of health promotion, job and family as well as mobility
  • Committed collaboration in a highly specialized interdisciplinary environment
  • Employment in one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland in the immediate vicinity of Lake Constance and the Alpstein Mountains
