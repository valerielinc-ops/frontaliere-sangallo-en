Full description

We create prospects for people to lead an independent life and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office of Correctional Services is an office within the Department of Security and Justice and employs around 260 people in various professional fields. The Platanenhof Youth Home supports adolescents and young adults who have been committed to civil and criminal law according to socio-pedagogical principles and concepts. New social, educational and professional development perspectives are worked out with the young people in a closed or open setting.

What to expect

  • Challenging everyday life with female and male adolescents aged 12-18 who are in crisis
  • Intensive work with accompanying persons, careful socio-pedagogical clarification and development of recommendations, writing of clarification reports
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation and involvement of the relevant environment
  • Administrative tasks

What sets you apart

  • High social and communication skills, resilience, flexibility, assertiveness and enjoyment of working in a team of 8-10 people
  • Empathetic and consistent attitude in a dynamic and fast-paced - often unpredictable - Environment
  • Independent and structured way of working
  • Very good oral and written German skills
  • Interest in taking advantage of flexible and irregular working hours, night attendance and weekend shifts
  • Ideally, experience in working with young people in difficult life situations

What we offer

  • Varied and meaningful work in a pedagogically highly developed environment
  • A professional and motivated team
  • High level of awareness with regard to employee management
  • A wide range of training and further education opportunities as well as practical advice and supervision
