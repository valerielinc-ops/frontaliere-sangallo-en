Full description

In the IT architecture department, we shape the strategic development of our IT landscape and create the basis for stable, secure and sustainable solutions. We define guidelines for technologies, applications and integrations and ensure that new requirements are implemented efficiently, sustainably and in line with our target architecture.

In order to strengthen our team at the Zurich Airport and St. Gallen locations, we are looking for a committed, initiative and responsible personality as an architect service layer (f/m/d).

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the architectural design and governance of the service layer across projects and initiatives
  • You derive target images, principles and standards for services, APIs and integration from the enterprise architecture
  • Ensure that services are clearly cut, consistent and reusable, and make a sustainable contribution to the overall architecture
  • You accompany projects and support Solution architects and teams in fundamental architectural and design decisions
  • You conduct architecture reviews and evaluate solutions
  • You identify redundancies, inconsistencies and optimization potential within the service layer
  • Empower teams through workshops, reviews and coaching in dealing with APIs and service-oriented concepts
  • Ensure regulatory and security requirements are considered from the start

Requirements

  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience as a solution architect, technical architect or senior engineer with architectural responsibility
  • In-depth understanding of service-oriented architectures, APIs and integration concepts
  • Experience in the design and implementation of services and API-based solutions
  • Ability to communicate architectural decisions in an understandable, pragmatic and effective manner
  • Balanced combination of conceptual thinking and technical detail
  • Very good knowledge of German (min. C1)

We offer

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.
