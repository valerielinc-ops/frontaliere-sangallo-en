Role overview

Who you are

As a Checkout Services Manager, you will be responsible, together with your team, to ensure a comfortable check-out and payment process for our customers.

This includes securing a fast, easy and customer-friendly check out, a cost-effective payment process and that payments are handled in a reliable and efficient way.
