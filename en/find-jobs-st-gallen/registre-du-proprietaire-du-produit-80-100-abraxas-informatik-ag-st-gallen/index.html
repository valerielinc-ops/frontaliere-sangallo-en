Full description

Do you want to take product responsibility, bring together different requirements and set the right priorities together with customers, partners and the development team? For the further development of our central personal register, we are looking for an experienced personality with a strong product understanding, analytical thinking, technical understanding and enjoyment of customer contact. Your role

  • You take on the professional responsibility for our central personal register and actively shape its further development.
  • You are responsible for and prioritize the product backlog and ensure that the right topics are implemented at the right time.
  • You analyze and evaluate requirements together with customers, business analysts and the development team and derive the product priorities from this.
  • You coordinate the integration of environmental systems and contribute your understanding of interfaces, data flows and system contexts.
  • You coordinate internal and external stakeholders and represent the product to customers and partners.
  • You are actively involved in customer projects and contribute your expertise to tenders and offers.

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as a product owner in an agile environment.
  • Technical understanding, especially for interfaces, data flows and system integrations.
  • Secure appearance and pleasure in working with customers, partners and development teams.
  • An independent, responsible and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Very good knowledge of German.

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% pension
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
