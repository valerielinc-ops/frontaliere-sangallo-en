Full description

As a Solution Architect: you take on a central and responsible role. Together with our technical teams and partners, you will shape the future IT architectures of our customers in the public sector (federal government, cantons and municipalities) and other institutions from the public sector in order to create the necessary conditions to drive the digital progress of Switzerland. Digitalization, new technologies, cloud and outsourcing services are our focus! As a Solution Architect, you are part of our Solution Architects team of six. We are looking for you as a committed and responsible personality with a sound technical background and an eye for the big picture. Your role

  • Design (IT architectures, IT standards and technical concepts for offers) of on-premise & cloud solutions (private, hybrid and public cloud from Microsoft, AWS or Google)
  • Processing of public tenders and offers (technical lead, design and development of the technical solution, pricing, presentation of the solution to our customers)
  • Collaboration in the design of new IT solutions and future services
  • Consulting and supporting our customers in their IT projects or strategies
  • Development of concepts of complex IT platforms in cooperation with our system engineers and external partners
  • Identify and introduce new innovations, technologies and tools that drive business success.
  • Accompanying the project teams to implement the project requirements

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or business informatics (FH/Uni/ETH/HF) or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in IT architecture, ideally in the development of complex IT systems
  • In-depth knowledge of cloud technologies (e.g. Azure/Microsoft 365, Google, AWS) and their architecture principles
  • Strong communication and moderation skills to convey complex technical content in an understandable way
  • Analytical thinking, problem-solving skills and high standards of quality and innovation
  • Very good communication and presentation skills in German and good knowledge of English

Certifications in the area of public cloud or IT architectures (e.g. Azure Solutions Architect Expert / Enterprise Administrator Expert) or Togaf round off your profile. Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees

We maintain an open you culture – so feel free to send us your application directly in the you form.
