Full description

As part of our Digital Manufacturing team, you will drive the transformation of SFS Group’s global production sites into a smart factory. Together we develop innovative solutions that make production more efficient, transparent and data-driven. Whether it’s Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), paperless manufacturing or Big Data & Analytics, you bring your know-how where digital innovation creates real added value. Your Tasks:

  • Development of innovative digitalization solutions for our production
  • Integration of production into the exciting world of the Smart Factory
  • Implementation of backend services and advance technological advances
  • Ensuring the basis for data-driven production processes and analytics
  • Promoting the Smart Factory by introducing new technological standards
  • Design of modern digitalization architectures and AI-supported production processes Your profile:
  • Completed studies in software development or a related field (FH or higher) as well as several years of professional experience in software engineering
  • Interest in Smart Factory and data-driven production processes
  • Familiarity with cloud technologies, data-driven architectures (e.g. Kubernetes, Kafka) and AI architectures to support production processes
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Enthusiasm for continuous development and motivation to achieve outstanding results
  • Responsible and solution-oriented personality with team spirit and hands-on mentality We offer:
  • Space for personal responsibility and initiative
  • Varied tasks
  • Six weeks of holidays as well as the possibility to purchase additional holidays
  • Own, attractive pension fund with individual savings plans
