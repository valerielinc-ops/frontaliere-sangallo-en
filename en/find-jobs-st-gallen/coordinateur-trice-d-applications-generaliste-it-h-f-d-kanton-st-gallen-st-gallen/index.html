Full description

The Road Transport and Shipping Office is an ISO-certified company with around 160 employees in various professional fields and a modern infrastructure with 20 different specialist applications. Are you an IT all-rounder with good process understanding who wants to actively shape the digitalization of our core business? Are you looking for a versatile interface role in which you not only provide support, but also want to participate in the development of our specialist application and the associated processes? If you want to build the bridge between our specialist departments and the central service providers in order to noticeably improve our service provision, you are at the right place.

What to expect

  • Management and support of projects as well as support of specialist departments in the analysis, design and optimization of business processes
  • Coordination, planning and support in the implementation of new extensions and updates of the specialist application in close cooperation with the specialists
  • First and second level support for challenging user questions and participation in internal and external IT working groups
  • Use and use of modern tools (such as Microsoft 365, SharePoint or tablet solutions)
  • Preparation and maintenance of documentation and instructions

What sets it apart

  • Completed training as a computer scientist EFZ (e.g. company informatics) or a completed vocational apprenticeship in the commercial or technical area with IT training (e.g. business informatics HF, ICT-Advanced-Supporter)
  • Experience in support and support of specialist applications
  • Analytical and networked thinking
  • Conceptual understanding of modern IT environments (M365, Client Management)
  • Service-oriented, reliable and communicative personality

What we offer

  • Varied and responsible work
  • Modern infrastructure and contemporary employment conditions
  • Attractive workplace in the center of the city of St.Gallen
