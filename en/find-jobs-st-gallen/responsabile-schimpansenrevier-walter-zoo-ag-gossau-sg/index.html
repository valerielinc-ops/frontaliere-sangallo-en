Full description

District manager: in chimpanzees with a focus on training & research (m/f/d | 80-100%) The Walter Zoo in Gossau SG is a scientifically managed zoo that cares for 1,000 animals from around 100 different species. It inspires more than 275,000 visitors annually for the wildlife and awakens interest and understanding for their needs and the protection of their habitats. The zoo is involved in various conservation projects and manages two care stations for native wildlife. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality with enthusiasm for primate care and research. Their tasks

  • The professional, organizational and personnel management of the district incl. Training of learners in the field of animal care
  • Responsibility for the further development of animal husbandry and ensuring a high quality of care, employment and animal welfare
  • 50% of the workforce is allocated to the implementation of scientific projects and the implementation of a scientific training and employment program for chimpanzees
  • Care of other animal species of the South American rainforest
  • Cooperation in daily animal care (feed preparation, cleaning and plant design)
  • Carrying out commentary feedings, animal lectures, communicating information to visitors

What you bring

  • Completed training as an animal keeper: in (preferred to wild animals) or a degree in a scientific study with corresponding practical experience
  • Leadership experience or the motivation and willingness to assume leadership responsibility, as well as enjoyment of cooperation and promotion of employees
  • Experience in care and handling apes is beneficial
  • Structured, solution-oriented and independent working methods as well as the ability to set priorities and actively drive change
  • Readiness for weekend and holiday services
  • Good knowledge of English in words and writing (at least level B2) for scientific exchange and professional communication

What we offer you

  • A versatile and responsible job with a balanced combination of animal care and scientific work
  • A position with a 50% share of research, which offers the opportunity to participate in innovative research projects together with a renowned university
  • A motivated, interdisciplinary environment with a passion for animals and species conservation
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development
