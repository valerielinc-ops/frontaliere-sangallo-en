Full description

MAIN TASKS

For six months from January 2027, you will be supporting our Products (PLP) & Geberit Production System (GPS) Engineering department in projects related to new products, processes and the further development of our manual assembly. One focus of your internship is the capacity expansion of a production line. You will analyze existing assembly processes and capacities, develop suitable optimization solutions together with the specialist areas and also examine possible automation potential. You take responsibility for your own project and proactively drive it forward. In addition, the following tasks are part of your internship:

  • Analysis and optimization of production and assembly processes
  • Implementation and evaluation of process, time and data analyses
  • Identification of optimization and automation potential
  • Support for master data maintenance as well as technical and organizational project tasks

PROFILE

You study mechanical engineering, mechanical engineering, industrial engineering, production engineering or a comparable technical degree program at a high school or university of applied sciences. Ideally, you are interested in production processes and automation.

  • Good technical and analytical understanding
  • Independent, structured and proactive working method
  • Willingness to take responsibility for your own project and drive it forward consistently
  • Team-oriented, reliable personality with pleasure in tackling practical tasks directly in the production environment

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
