Full description

Our focus is on selected and high-growth end applications. At the Heerbrugg site, important precision molded parts as well as technical fasteners and assemblies for the automotive industry are produced. With the cold mass forming technology, we produce precision molded parts on the most modern multi-stage press machines. Do you want to actively contribute your expertise in this area? We look forward to your application! Your Tasks:

  • Adjustment, operation and monitoring of multistage press machines on which complex cold-formed parts are produced
  • Responsible for correcting process disruptions
  • Introduction of process optimizations
  • Responsible for the quality of the manufactured products
  • Input of measured values into the quality system Your profile:
  • Completed basic mechanical formation
  • Good technical understanding
  • High performance, teamwork and reliability
  • Positive attitude towards flexible two-shift operation
  • Self-employment and initiative
  • German skills in word and writing We offer:
  • Employment in future-oriented & innovative company
  • Varied tasks
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual participation in success
  • Staff restaurant with attractive prices
