Full description

In the team "Records Management Systems", we are responsible for the technical handling of the entire lifecycle of electronic documents – from creation to archiving and legally compliant destruction.

For the management of our team we are looking for an experienced and convincing leader at the location in St. Gallen by immediately or by agreement. You take responsibility for people, applications and the strategic development of this team. Tasks: • Personnel and professional management of the Group Records Management Systems with 8 employees • Responsible for planning, controlling and controlling the strategic measures within the organizational unit • Overall responsibility of applications electronic archive, document management system as well as the scanning and capturing solution • Ensuring active and economical application management for the responsible applications with regard to quality, availability, performance, security and risks • Coach and develop your team as part of "Performance & Development" • Central contact person for business partners, project managers and suppliers Requirements • A completed study of computer science or a comparable education or further education. • Several years of experience in leading IT teams. • Experience in application management of complex business applications. • Good knowledge of Java/JEE development and the use of appropriate tools. • Practical experience with agile working methods such as Scrum, Kanban or SAFe • Good knowledge of Jira and Confluence for planning, controlling and transparency of requirements, projects and operational tasks • Strong communication and leadership skills. • A structured, solution-oriented way of working and entrepreneurial thinking. • Very good knowledge of German and English We offer: The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.
