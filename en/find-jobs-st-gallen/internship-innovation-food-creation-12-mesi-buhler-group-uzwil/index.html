Full description

Do you want to help shape the food of tomorrow and develop new product ideas together with customers? In the Food Creation Center you work on trend-oriented product concepts for the areas of bars, waffles, biscuits and bread. You connect current global food and nutrition trends with the needs of different regions and markets. In addition, you will develop a practical guide for innovation workshops. In this way, you create a structured basis for developing and developing new ideas together with customers and our international teams.

Tasks

  • You develop and optimize recipes and product concepts for bars, wafers, biscuits and bread – oriented towards current market and nutrition trends, such as protein enrichment, fiber enrichment or sugar reduction
  • You will research and evaluate global and regional food trends, raw materials and consumer needs and develop product ideas that combine global food trends with the peculiarities of selected regional markets and consumer groups.
  • You plan and run test series, purchase them and evaluate relevant product and process properties
  • You develop a guide with a practice-oriented concept, suitable creative and development methods, processes and work steps for innovation workshops with customers and participate in the preparation and implementation of customer workshops and product demonstrations
  • You prepare the results for internal teams and customers, for example in reports and presentations

Requirements

  • Completed studies in food technology, nutritional sciences, process engineering or a related field – alternatively completed training as a food technology specialist with further qualification as a master or technician: in the food sector
  • Basic understanding of the functionality of ingredients and important process parameters in food production as well as interest in food and nutrition trends, regional eating habits and consumer needs
  • Secure handling of MS Office (experience with data evaluation, for example in Excel, is an advantage) as well as very good English skills in word and writing
  • Structured and careful working methods as well as organisational capacity and networked thinking
  • Communication, creativity, initiative and curiosity

With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of reconciling the needs of the economy, people and nature. In this way, we develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries.
