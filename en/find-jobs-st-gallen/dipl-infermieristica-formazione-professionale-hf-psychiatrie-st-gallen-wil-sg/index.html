Full description

Your tasks

  • With your professional competence, you strengthen our team of reception and emergency station 60+ in the field of gerontopsychiatry and actively participate in the day-to-day work of the station.
  • As a caregiver, you assume the nursing responsibility for the individual care and care of our patients and accompany them safely through the nursing and therapeutic process.
  • Even in complex situations, you can negotiate safely, carry out medical and technical prescriptions and perform administrative tasks as well as carefully documenting and reporting your observations.
  • An important focus of your work is the support and support of our HF students, whose learning process you specifically promote and support with planned learning support in a practical manner.
  • You ensure the training assignment in everyday station life and carry out location interviews, learning interviews and qualification interviews.
  • In exchange with the training managers, you contribute to a reliable training quality and, in the absence of the responsible vocational training managers, also take over the support of our FaGe learners. Your profile
  • You will also bring a completed training as a nurse HF or FH as well as the required SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck at a foreign diploma.
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in the psychiatric field and enjoy working with mentally ill people as well as in a multi-professional team.
  • With your social skills, your flexibility and resilience, you convince as well as with an independent, self-responsible and committed way of working.
  • Very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level and good IT skills are part of your profile and support you in your administrative tasks.
  • For the accompaniment of our HF students, you bring professional competence in care, supervision and medical technology and have the course SVEB 1 or are ready to complete it with generous support from us. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
