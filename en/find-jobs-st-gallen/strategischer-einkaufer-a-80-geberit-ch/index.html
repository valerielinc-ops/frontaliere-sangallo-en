Full description

MAIN TASKS

Geberit Produktions AG (almost) never stands still. In order for this to remain the case, purchasing is needed that is forward-looking, recognises risks and builds the right partnerships. You take responsibility for defined product groups and ensure that the required materials are optimally available in terms of cost, quality and security of supply. In close cooperation with suppliers and internal stakeholders, you actively participate in the further development of our procurement and supply chain processes. Your responsibilities include in particular:

  • Responsibility for the strategic procurement of the allocated product groups, taking into account costs, quality, delivery performance and security of supply
  • Evaluation, selection, qualification and further development of suppliers including contract and price negotiations as well as implementation of supplier audits
  • Identification, evaluation and minimization of supplier and supply chain risks
  • Close cooperation with internal stakeholders as well as implementation of Group-wide procurement and optimization projects
  • Maintenance of relevant master data as well as preparation of reporting, analyses and key figures for controlling the product group portfolio responsible
  • Conducting cost analysis and realizing sustainable savings taking into account total cost-of-ownership aspects

PROFILE

You will bring a completed commercial or technical basic education with further training in purchasing, supply chain management or business administration. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • 3 to 5 years of professional experience in purchasing, ideally in an industrial environment
  • Negotiation skills and enjoyment of building sustainable relationships with suppliers and internal contacts
  • Analytical, structured and independent working method combined with a pragmatic hands-on mentality
  • Very good knowledge of German and English at level B2

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
