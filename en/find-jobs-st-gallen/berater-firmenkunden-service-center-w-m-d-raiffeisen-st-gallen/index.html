01.10.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The Corporate Customer Service Center actively supports Raiffeisen Switzerland, Raiffeisen banks and group companies in achieving their service goals by receiving and answering telephone and written end customer inquiries. To strengthen our team in St. Gallen, we are looking for a fast-learning, service-oriented and communication-strong personality. We offer you a modern and flexible working environment as well as interesting further development opportunities. Support us in the further development of our ever-growing corporate customer service center and play your part in becoming the number one customer and jointly break new ground in digital banking.

What awaits you?

  • Solution-oriented, telephone support for corporate customers and corporate customer consultants from all over Switzerland
  • Independent processing and answering of written customer inquiries in various electronic channels
  • Management and maintenance of customer data
  • Implementation of individual outbound campaigns for the development of the corporate client portfolio
  • Active cooperation with local Raiffeisen banks and internal specialist bodies

What do you bring?

  • Basic commercial training with a banking orientation
  • Experience in the contact center area is advantageous
  • Passion to develop into a sustainable banking career
  • Strong customer and service orientation with sales flair
  • Excellent knowledge of German (in word + writing)
