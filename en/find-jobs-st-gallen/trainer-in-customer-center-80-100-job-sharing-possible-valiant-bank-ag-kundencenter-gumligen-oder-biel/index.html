Full description

Simplify together. Because life is full of possibilities. At the same time, it has become complex. That is why we are looking for employees who work with us to promote simplicity. You will

  • Planning, developing and implementing introductory, specialist and sales training
  • accompany new employees during their training and ensure a successful start
  • Coach employees in everyday work and support them in their professional and personal development
  • Developing and continuously optimizing modern training and learning concepts as well as training materials
  • Analyze quality and performance indicators and derive targeted development and training measures from them
  • accompany the introduction of new products, services and processes with suitable training concepts

You bring

  • Several years of experience in customer service, ideally in the financial industry
  • Experience in training, coaching or employee development
  • adult education training (SVEB) or equivalent qualification
  • Negotiation-proof knowledge of German and French in words and writing as well as the ability to conduct training and coaching in both languages
  • Joy in getting people excited, motivated and sustainably developed
  • a convincing communication ability and a secure appearance
  • an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • the willingness to commute regularly between the Gümligen and Biel sites

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1200 people – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
