21.10.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

With our dedicated Design System team, we are developing the central Raiffeisen Design Library, the basis for a consistent, modern and accessible digital customer experience. To strengthen our team at the St. Gallen site, we are looking for an independent and communicative personality as an Angular Developer – Design System.

What awaits you?

  • Further development and maintenance of the design system based on Angular
  • Implementation of reusable, performant UI components according to modern front-end standards
  • Working closely with UX designers and product teams to ensure consistent user experiences
  • Technical advice and support of other teams in the integration of the design system
  • Co-creation of development guidelines, architectural decisions and best practices in the front-end area

What do you bring?

  • Completed studies (FH/uni) in computer science or a related field of study
  • Several years of experience in development with Angular and Typescript
  • Technical flair for design systems and component-based development
  • Experience in accessibility is beneficial
  • Passion for quality, clean code and continuous improvement
  • Independent, structured working method and pleasure in cross-team communication
