Full description

You love it when administrative processes run like clockwork and want to be the first positive impression for our new team members? Then you are exactly right with us! From January 2027, our motivated HR team is looking for temporary support for 6 months in a workload of 60-80%. In this role, you take on tasks from HR administration with a clear focus on the onboarding process. If you work structured and feel comfortable in a dynamic environment, we definitely want to get to know you! Your role

  • Onboarding administration: Complete administrative handling of new entries from A to Z
  • Contracting: drawing up employment contracts, agreements and entry documents
  • Work permits: obtaining and coordinating work permits for foreign employees
  • System Capture: Capture and maintain all entry-relevant data in SAP HCM and SuccessFactors
  • Master data maintenance: Ongoing update and mutation of HR master data
  • File maintenance: Digital archiving and maintenance of HR personnel dossiers
  • Special projects: Participation and administrative support in current HR projects

Your profile

  • Completed further training as HR assistant, preferably further training as HR specialist
  • sound professional experience in HR administration
  • profound and proven experience with SAP HCM and SuccessFactors
  • You work structured, reliable and keep track of several tasks
  • With your open, helpful nature, you like to approach people and also bring a good dose of humor with you.
  • You communicate stylishly in German, knowledge of French is an advantage

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% pension
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
