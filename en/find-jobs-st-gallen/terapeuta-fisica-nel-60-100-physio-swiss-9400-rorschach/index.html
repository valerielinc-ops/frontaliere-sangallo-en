Since 1988, Physiotherapie Derksen AG has stood for personal and high-quality care. With the acquisition in the new generation, we are continuing on this path – with respect, appreciation and the aspiration to accompany our patients in the best possible way. That is why we are not simply looking for another physiotherapist. We are looking for someone who shares our values and wants to shape the future of our practice together with us. It is important to us to create a working environment in which you feel comfortable, support each other and communicate openly with each other. We appreciate the professional exchange as well as an uncomplicated cooperation. With us, you should have time to respond to your patients, be able to contribute your ideas and develop yourself both professionally and personally. Quality is more important to us than quantity. Whether you already have professional experience or are just starting your career is not crucial for us. More important is that you enjoy working with people, enjoy your profession and fit human well into our team.
