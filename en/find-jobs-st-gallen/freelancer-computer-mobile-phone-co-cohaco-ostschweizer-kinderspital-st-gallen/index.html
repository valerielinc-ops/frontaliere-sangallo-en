Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a freelancer for the offer "Computer, Handy + Co." (CoHaCo) to strengthen the child protection center.

Their tasks

  • Organisation of workshops and training assignments in schools
  • Providing responsible handling of digital media in the topics:
  • Social media
  • Gaming
  • Cyberbullying
  • Sexting
  • Pornography
  • Data protection and legal aspects

Your profile

  • Experience working with children and young people
  • Professional background e.g. in pedagogy, social work, media pedagogy or a related field
  • Joy of communicating and a safe presence in front of groups
  • Interest in digital living environments and child and youth protection

We offer them

  • Cooperation on a freelance basis
  • Approximately 12 assignments per calendar year or by arrangement
  • Area of application: especially the cantons of St.Gallen, Appenzell Ausserrhoden and Appenzell Innerrhoden
  • Annual update training and exchange with other freelancers
  • Working with CoHaCo documentation and materials provided by the Child Protection Center

Your work area Children and adolescents affected by violence and their environment receive advice and support at the Child Protection Centre (KSZ). Great emphasis is placed on support for finding your own solutions. Professionals and third parties can also contact the KSZ if it is reasonable to assume that children or adolescents are affected by violence. Through the child and youth emergency call, help seekers receive telephone or personal support on site – anonymously, confidentially and free of charge. The Parents Hotline “Effective” supports parents of younger children if they are strongly challenged with parenting tasks in the short term and it is no longer possible to solve disputes without violence. The interdisciplinary interaction at the OKS helps in situations with suspicion or certainty of violence against children and adolescents. Networking and cooperation are central to child protection. In addition to the advisory services, the KSZ trains and sensitizes various target groups with a variety of training and prevention services.
