Assistant psychologist/assistant psychologist — Psychiatrie St.Gallen

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

This position is aimed at psychologists as well as assistant physicians who are interested in further training in the field of forensic psychiatry and psychotherapy and would like to further develop their professional competences in a specialized forensic environment. Your tasks

  • You actively accompany patients in the treatment process, carry out psychiatric and psychotherapeutic assessments and therapies under guidance and support the diagnosis and individual treatment planning.
  • In the interdisciplinary exchange with psychology, medicine, nursing and social work, you contribute to a holistic and milieu-therapeutic care of patients.
  • With a resource-oriented and individual treatment approach, you take personal needs and tort-specific aspects into account and support the development of safe and everyday strategies.
  • You lead group therapies and actively contribute your ideas and professional impulses to the further development of the therapeutic offer.
  • As part of the forensic treatment and the associated safety requirements, you contribute to risk assessments and make an important contribution to the safety of patients and the treatment team. Your profile
  • You have a degree in psychology and have already gained professional experience in forensics.
  • Your psychotherapy training is already advanced and you want to deepen your professional knowledge in the psychiatric psychotherapeutic environment.
  • You already meet the requirements for a professional training permit as an assistant psychologist or assistant psychologist of the Canton of St.Gallen or can fulfill them before starting your job.
  • A respectful, unprejudiced and professional treatment of patients as well as a reliable cooperation with internal and external specialists characterize your way of working.
  • With the special structures and framework conditions of the implementation of measures you deal safely and maintain a professional and reflective attitude even in demanding situations.
  • High commitment, self-initiative and very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least level C1 round off your profile and allow you secure communication in clinical everyday life. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
