Apprenticeship as a specialist Cleaning Technology EFZ Training centres Place of work St. Gallen Job listing Your tasks and perspectives

  • You are responsible for the maintenance, intermediate and basic cleaning as well as hygiene of the buildings and thus indirectly for the safety of patients
  • For example, you disinfect the operating room, clean facades or perform professional pest control
  • Even demanding cleaning tasks with special equipment and means can be carried out independently
  • Proper operation of high-pressure cleaners, scrubbers and sweepers as well as their maintenance as well as carrying out minor repairs are part of your tasks
  • You know about the use of cleaning, care and disinfectants and learn to use and dispose of them properly What you bring for this job
  • You have successfully completed the compulsory schooling until the start of training
  • You are physically fit and active because you are constantly in motion during work
  • Cleanliness and order are important to you
  • In order to do this training, you must have no fear of heights and no allergies to cleaning products
  • You like to work in a team and with different people Our offer to you
  • During your training period, you can work in different departments and teams
  • You complete your training in a motivating company in which almost all learners successfully complete
  • For the cost of your school supplies you will receive financial support
  • You are allowed to attend internal training courses and information events
  • If you do your best during your apprenticeship, we may be able to offer you further employment after your apprenticeship. Your application
