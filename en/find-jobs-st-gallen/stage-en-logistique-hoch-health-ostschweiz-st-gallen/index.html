Internship in logistics

Training centres

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • You take goods, learn the right unloading and carry out the first inspection. You also pack and load different goods
  • You are responsible for the correct stock and learn to register on the computer
  • Cooperation with suppliers is also part of your field of activity

What you bring for this job

  • You are looking for a bridging year after the compulsory schooling period and would like to get a comprehensive insight into the warehouse management and the logistics of a hospital
  • The variety at work with very small but also very large goods gives you pleasure
  • You like to move, are resilient and have endurance
  • They can rely on you and you have a talent for organizing
  • It is important to you to offer an optimal service for other departments

Our offer to you

  • You can gain your first professional experience in an advanced environment and benefit from a well-trained, qualified and experienced team.
  • During your internship, you will work with various medicines, chemicals and hospital supplies
  • You have the opportunity to apply for an apprenticeship as a logistician EFZ or EBA in a simplified selection procedure

Your application

  • Entry immediately or by arrangement. You can find out more about this exciting internship on our website or from Lea Bischofberger, HR Education specialist, telephone +41 71 494 70 59
  • The traineeship is limited to 31. July 2027.
  • Take your chance and complete your internship at HOCH Health Eastern Switzerland

About us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a versatile working environment. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to the common future. We focus on team spirit, interdisciplinary cooperation and innovative solutions.
