Full description

10.11.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

As a reinforcement for the Digital Channel Security team at the St. Gallen location with the opportunity to work in the home office, we are looking for an interested and independent personality as an Application Security Engineer (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Develop, co-design and review solutions for secure applications, security monitoring and fraud detection
  • Accompanying projects with responsibility for application security, security monitoring and fraud detection
  • Coordination of projects in the field of digital customer channels
  • Track up and accompany security audits
  • Collaboration in the analysis of security incidents
  • Advising the business side and cooperating with other security agencies on topics related to application security, security monitoring and fraud detection

What do you bring?

  • university degree in computer science/business informatics (HF, FH, Uni) or comparable education or Training courses
  • IT security training
  • Experience in the field of application security, at best also in the field of mobile security
  • In-depth knowledge of standards, architectures and methods for secure applications
  • Experience in Application Security and Monitoring Systems (e.g. Splunk, QRadar, etc.)
  • Experience in software development
  • Good knowledge of German and English

With secure applications and a multi-layered fraud detection, Raiffeisen optimally protects its customers and thus actively supports digitalization. You can help shape the solutions to these challenges in this extremely exciting environment with creative ideas. Simon Züst Group Leader Digital Channel Security Why Raiffeisen?
