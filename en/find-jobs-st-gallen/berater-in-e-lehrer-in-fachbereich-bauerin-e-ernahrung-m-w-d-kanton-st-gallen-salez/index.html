Full description

The Agrarliche Zentrum St.Gallen (LZSG) is part of the Department of Agriculture and fulfils a versatile task in education and training as well as in agricultural consulting.

What to expect

  • Advising farmers on issues of farming and food
  • Organisation and implementation of courses and meetings in product processing and self-sufficiency
  • Education in basic agricultural education and higher vocational training of the bzbs at the locations Salez and Flawil (5-10%), in particular in the course “Farmer with a professional card” (module Product Processing)
  • Working as an examiner

What sets it apart

  • Completed studies as a teacher household and further education in the field of nutrition or gastronomy
  • In-depth knowledge of food processing with a focus on nutrition, health and farming
  • Professional experience in consulting and teaching
  • Understanding the multifaceted role of the farmer, enjoyment of dealing with farming families, course participants and young people in training

What we offer

  • High level of independence and personal responsibility
  • Positive working environment in a committed and collegial team
  • Different working time models, moving working hours
  • Catering facilities in the company’s own canteen in Salez
