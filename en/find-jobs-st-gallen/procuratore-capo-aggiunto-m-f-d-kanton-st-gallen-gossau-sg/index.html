Full description

The public prosecutor’s office serves the population of the canton of St. Gallen and is committed to a fair criminal justice system. As an independent investigative and prosecuting authority, we conduct criminal proceedings in accordance with the principles of the rule of law. At the Gossau site, more than 30 employees are committed to the enforcement of the law and thus make an important contribution to the peace of law in our society.

What to expect

  • Deputy Prosecutor
  • Possibilities for participation in the investigation office and the entire public prosecutor’s office
  • Conducting and concluding criminal investigations
  • Indictment and representation of the indictment before first and second instance
  • Pictet service

What distinguishes you

  • Empathy, strong communication skills and inclusive personality
  • Pronounced solution orientation, analytical and networked thinking, decision-making and tactical skills
  • Willingness to continuously develop leadership skills
  • Legally perfect, programmatic and role-faithful investigation What you bring with you
  • Proven leadership experience, preferably in a public prosecutor’s office
  • Leadership training
  • Completed legal studies, attorney's patent desired
  • Several years of successful work in law enforcement
  • Category B driving licence (passenger car)

What we offer

  • Young dynamic environment and collaborative cooperation in a team
  • Modern working environment and contemporary employment conditions with flexible working hours
  • Demanding and meaningful work in law enforcement and leadership
  • Place of work in walking distance to Gossau station
