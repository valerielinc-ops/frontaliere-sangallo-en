Full description

Specialist neurophysiological diagnostics 40-60 %

Clinic for Neurology

Place of work St. Gallen

Dipl. Nursing professional / specialist assistant / neurophysiological diagnosis, MTA-F 40-60 %

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Performation of electroencephalograms (routine, intensive and long-term EEGs)
  • Implementation of evoked potentials (SEP medianus, ulnaris, tibialis, MEP of the extremities, VEPs and AEPs) in inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Administrative and general laboratory work and autonomous testing
  • Neuromonitoring in neurosurgical, orthopaedic and vascular surgery (with diploma FND or MTA-F)
  • Preliminary evaluation of long-term EEG studies after appropriate incorporation

What you bring for this job

  • Training as a dipl. nurse HF / dipl. Nursing specialist HF or specialist health EFZ / specialist health EFZ
  • Further training as a specialist for neurophysiological diagnostics (FND) or medical-technical assistance for functional diagnostics (MTA-F) is advantageous
  • Interest in modern, computer-aided measuring and diagnostic systems
  • Readiness to take over on-call services at the weekend
  • Teamwork, independence and flexibility

Our offer to you

  • Mostly regulated working hours from Monday to Friday, supplemented by on-call service on around one weekend per month
  • Varied challenges in a dynamic working environment
  • Constructive cooperation in a permanent and collegial team
  • The opportunity to make an impact and develop
  • Various employee offers in the area of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
