Full description

We produce favorite products that inspire – in Switzerland and for Switzerland. Do you love working with high-quality raw materials and want to further develop your passion for baking? With us, you have the unique opportunity to grow step-by-step into a leadership role. We are looking for you for our in-house bakery – to shape the future of baking together with us.

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the production of high-quality baked goods in compliance with our quality standards
  • Leadership and development of a committed team
  • Ensuring efficient workflows and optimal use of resources
  • Creative design of new products and implementation of innovative ideas
  • Monitoring and compliance with hygiene and safety regulations

Requirements

  • Professional experience: Several years of professional experience, initial leadership experience, or the desire to develop into a leadership role
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): Bakery
  • Passion for the craft and joy in working with high-quality raw materials
  • Creativity, organizational talent, and a structured way of working
  • Team player with strong communication skills and assertiveness
  • Willingness for professional development through "on-the-job" experience
  • Use of internal training and development offers
  • German (fluent)

What we offer

  • Employee offers: Cumulus employee points, discounted mobile subscriptions, higher interest rates at Migros Bank, contributions to Klubschule/fitness, and more.
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensive coverage in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sickness benefit at full net salary.
  • Migros Bank: Higher interest rates on staff investment accounts at Migros Bank.
  • Education and training: We actively train employees through internal programs and support external training with time and financial contributions if needed.
  • Vacation: 5 weeks of vacation for everyone, 6 weeks from age 50, 7 weeks from age 60, possibility to purchase additional vacation days.
