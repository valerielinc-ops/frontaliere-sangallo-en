Main responsibilities

  • In the daily clinical setting, you take over the psychotherapeutic treatment of patients in individual and group therapies.
  • Case-related documentation, reports and performance recording in the clinic information system are part of your everyday life with the support of our secretariat.
  • In a manageable team of ten people, you will experience interprofessional cooperation at eye level and short decision-making paths.
  • A modern, evidence-based and versatile treatment offer you actively shape.
  • You are dedicated to high-quality group psychotherapy, including an interactively open therapy group, psychoeducation, acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), social competence training (SKT) and analytic-mentalization-based groups.
  • External supervision, internal case discussions and individual professional support by an experienced psychotherapeutic and psychiatric management regularly accompany you.
  • For every ten percent of your employment, you take on individual therapeutic case management for a patient, which keeps your case portfolio manageable. Requirements
  • You have successfully completed your psychology studies and already gained experience in inpatient adult psychiatry.
  • You have completed your Swiss-approved psychotherapy training or will complete it in the foreseeable future.
  • Daily clinical, interdisciplinary treatment oriented to the needs of patients and their relatives arouses your interest.

Key requirements

  • You have successfully completed your psychology studies and already gained experience in inpatient adult psychiatry.
  • You have completed your Swiss-approved psychotherapy training or will complete it in the foreseeable future.
  • Daily clinical, interdisciplinary treatment oriented to the needs of patients and their relatives arouses your interest.
  • High-quality group psychotherapy such as interactively open therapy group, psychoeducation, acceptance and commitment therapy or social competence training brings you pleasure.
  • Interprofessional teamwork, short decision-making paths and flat hierarchies are highly appreciated.
  • For this position, German language skills at C1 level are required to communicate professionally with patients, colleagues and external partners.
  • With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
