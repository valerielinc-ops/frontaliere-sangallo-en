Full description

The investigation office Uznach conducts all criminal proceedings in the area Gaster/See, Obertoggenburg, Neutoggenburg and Sarganserland. The team of around 30 people comprises ten prosecutors, as well as case handlers with prosecutorial powers, procedural assistants and trainees. It currently has two locations in Uznach and a branch office in Flums.

What to expect

  • Cooperation in criminal investigations under the direction and direction of a prosecutor
  • Assisting a prosecutor in the preparation of hearings, coercive measures and final orders
  • Possibility of accompanying a prosecutor in the pikettdienst

What sets it apart

  • Interest in prosecution
  • Completed Master's degree in Law
  • Freight forwarding and thorough operation
  • Flexibility, teamwork skills and social skills

What we offer

  • Auditorium as preparation for the bar examination
  • Modernly equipped work and training place
  • Additional information can be found at anwaltschaft.sg.ch
