Customer Relationship Manager — Bühler Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a Customer Relations Manager, it is your task to build a trusting and reliable partnership with our customers, establish Bühler as a service provider and offer our customers our diverse range of service offerings. You support customers in increasing the productivity and service life of their machines and systems and thus in becoming even more successful. Are you a proactive, business-oriented and empathetic personality with a very good technical and commercial understanding? Are you extremely successful in building and maintaining close customer relationships? Then become part of our Customer Service Team!

Responsibilities

  • You will be responsible for managing and developing existing customer relationships through customer visits, consultations and ensuring the agreed services, as well as through a keen interest in customer challenges and an understanding of decision-making processes and criteria
  • You are responsible for the active sale of sophisticated service products such as service contracts, spare parts, conversions and service projects, communicating the customer benefits in a targeted and convincing manner
  • You will systematically develop the market in your sales territory, further develop existing customers, identify and acquire new customers and contribute to winning back former customers
  • You are responsible for achieving sales and revenue targets through the sustainable expansion and further development of the service business, as well as for the professional control and management of the entire sales process in the CRM system
  • You will work closely with back-office teams and competence centers on customer inquiries and actively contribute to defining and implementing the Customer Service strategy in the sales territory, as well as the corporate strategy

Requirements

  • Technical training with further commercial education, as well as experience in selling sophisticated technical products and service solutions
  • Strong customer orientation, pronounced negotiating skills, as well as an interest in customer needs and sustainable customer relationships
  • Very good communication skills in German and English, ideally complemented by knowledge of French
  • Good understanding of sales processes, as well as of coordinating and collaborating with various stakeholders
  • Motivated, independent, team-oriented and entrepreneurial personality with a high willingness to travel of 60-80 % in the sales territory

About Bühler

Two billion people eat food every day that was produced on Bühler equipment. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts were produced using Bühler technologies.

With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of bringing the needs of business, people and nature into harmony. In this way, we develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries.

We want to create innovations for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions. That is why we work with customers, start-ups, multinational corporations and scientists to achieve a faster impact together.
