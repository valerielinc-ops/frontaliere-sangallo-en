Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Regio Uzwil – a successful, locally anchored bank – we are looking for an entrepreneurial and customer-oriented personality as a corporate client consultant. Help us to further expand our position as the preferred bank for companies in the Uzwil growth region.

Tasks

  • Ensuring comprehensive, needs-based advice to customers in corporate banking
  • Preparation of individual solution proposals for the entire Raiffeisenbank consulting and service offering in corporate banking, taking into account the specific needs of corporate customers
  • Maintaining existing customers as well as recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support of your own customer portfolio
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market processing measures and performance of representative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed banking training, ideally with further training in banking or finance (business administration, corporate finance, trust, etc.)
  • Several years of support and acquisition experience - especially in the SME segment
  • Team-capable and entrepreneurial personality with a pronounced customer orientation, negotiating skills and a secure appearance
  • High degree of independence and flexibility
  • Analytical ability and an exact and reliable way of working
  • Regional anchorage beneficial
