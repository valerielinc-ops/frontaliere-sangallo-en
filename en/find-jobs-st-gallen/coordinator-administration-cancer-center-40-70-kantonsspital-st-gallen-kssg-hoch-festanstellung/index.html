Coordinator/Administration Cancer Center 40-70 %

Medical Oncology/Hematology

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Organization and coordination of processes in the DKG-certified center as well as planning and recording of meetings and quality conferences
  • Supporting the management of the Quality Management CCC in certifications, monitoring audits and recertifications
  • Maintenance and updating of certification-related documents and evidence documents
  • Ensuring compliance with valid DKG survey forms and technical requirements
  • Central contact and interface between the CCC HOCH and the partners involved

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training in medical, nursing, health science, commercial or similar fields
  • Ideally, professional experience in health care or in a certified center
  • Experience in quality management and/or project management desirable
  • Secure handling of MS Office, especially Word, Excel and PowerPoint
  • Organizational strength, communication and coordination skills as well as an independent and structured working method

Our offer to you

  • A responsible and versatile job in a committed, dynamic team
  • Active design possibilities in the construction and further development of the centre
  • Structured familiarisation and continuous technical support
  • Diverse internal training opportunities
  • Attractive employment conditions with a contemporary pension solution and flexible working time models to promote work-life balance
