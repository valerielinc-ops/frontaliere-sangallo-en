Instructor baby swimming 10%

Course system G&G

Place of work Wil

Job advertisement

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Independent planning and varied design of five consecutive course units
  • Professional baby swimming courses are held on around 15 Saturday mornings per year, each with four lessons in a row
  • Playful promotion of getting used to water and the movement development of babies and toddlers
  • Competent support and advice for parents during the course units
  • Shared responsibility for a safe, warm and appealing course atmosphere

What you bring to this position

  • Experience in leading baby swimming courses and enjoyment of working with babies and their companions
  • Completed aqua-baby training from swimsports is a prerequisite for this job
  • Empathetic, open-minded and responsible personality with high social skills
  • Independent, flexible and reliable way of working
  • Strong service orientation and enjoyment of contact with families

Our offer to you

  • A varied and interesting job in a family environment
  • Attractive employee benefits
  • Hourly wage employment conditions in accordance with cantonal guidelines
  • Wide range of professional and personal training and further education opportunities
