Full description

Stationary psychiatry means for you: taking responsibility where people need it most. In Wil in the canton of St. Gallen, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior adult psychiatry in the range of 60-100%, who will take over the professional management of an interdisciplinary team in dual partnership management. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They are focused on stationary care and take over the professional management of the interdisciplinary team in partnership dual management with the nursing station management.
  • They provide therapeutic and medical care together with assistant doctors and psychologists
  • They accompany and support the assistant doctors and assistant psychologists assigned to them through structured further education and regular supervision.
  • Together with the senior physicians team and the nursing management, they constantly develop concepts and the orientation of the station.

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy or shortly before completion of this training
  • Valid Professional Permit (BAB) of the Canton of St. Gallen or willingness to acquire it before entering the job
  • Knowledge of German at C1 level for professional communication with patients, colleagues and external partners

That sets them apart.

  • Enjoyment of inpatient work with mentally ill patients as well as close interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • Recovery-oriented attitude that you represent not only theoretically, but in lived practice
  • Flexibility, team spirit, communication strength and continuous development
  • Working independently, assuming responsibility and secure decision-making

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Gross wage at 100% is expected to be between CHF 151'000.– and CHF 181'000.– annually, according to cantonal wage guidelines
  • Generous financial participation in training and paid working time for internal and external training
  • Individual career and career planning, support for doctoral theses, cross-location networking
  • Flexible working time models: flexible time, bandwidth models, part-time, job/top-sharing, maternity and paternity leave
  • Reduced childcare, catering and eco-bonus and company parking
  • Performance, loyalty and mediation rewards and personal gifts
  • Services for well-being and health (yoga, fitness room, flu and tick vaccination)

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
