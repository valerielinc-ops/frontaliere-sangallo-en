Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Zürcher Oberland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Uster, Gossau, Pfäffikon, Wetzikon and in Hinwil, we are looking for a personality as a customer advisor (m/f/d) in Gossau ZH.

What awaits you?

  • Ensure smooth and customer-oriented operation at the counter as well as efficient and needs-based operation and advice in the customer zone
  • Responsible for a customer portfolio in the team and contributes to the identification and exploitation of potential through effective and efficient sales activities
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer support
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education or equivalent with banking experience
  • Good knowledge of banking products in the areas of saving, paying, investing, provisioning
  • Enthusiastic and sales-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Self-employed, precise and responsible work
  • Teamwork, flexibility and high commitment

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
