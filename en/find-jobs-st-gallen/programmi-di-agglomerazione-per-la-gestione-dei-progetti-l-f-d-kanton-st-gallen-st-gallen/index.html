Role overview

The Office for Spatial Development and Geoinformation (AREG) is the cantonal department for spatial development, official surveying and geoinformation.

Within the framework of the Agglomeration Policy, the federal government is looking forward to contributions to the investments in agglomeration traffic.

It demands the creation of agglomeration programs in the area of settlement and transport.
