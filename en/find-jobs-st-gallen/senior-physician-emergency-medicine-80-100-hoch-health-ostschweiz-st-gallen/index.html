Full description

Emergency medical practitioner 80-100 %

Emergency Centre

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Care and management of emergencies as well as shock room guidance in the largest emergency and HSM trauma center in Eastern Switzerland with a wide range of internistic/surgical disciplines
  • Implementation of highly specialized emergency medical interventions such as NIV/HFNC, analgosedations, thoracic drapes, system of invasive blood pressure measurement and artery catheter
  • Further education and support of our experienced senior physicians in all areas of emergency medicine
  • Structured teaching and mentoring for senior physicians in further training for ISP Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR
  • Very varied work in an exceptionally wide range of emergency medicine

What you bring for this job

  • Specialist title with interdisciplinary focus title Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR
  • Several years of experience as a senior physician in interdisciplinary emergency medicine
  • Experience in instructor work (sonography, ACLS, ATLS, simulation) as well as a tutor/supervisor status SGUM of advantage
  • Team-capable, responsible and resilient personality with motivation to work together in an interdisciplinary team

Our offer to you

  • Recognized, strengthened emergency medicine with a lot of competence and responsibility since realignment in 2025 as well as participation in the further development to NFZ 2.0
  • Collegial collaboration in the team of 15 emergency medical professionals in a city with charm, culture and a high quality of life
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • In case of interest and suitability – management of the emergency practice embedded in the emergency centre and implementation and management of research projects
  • Lecturer within the scope of the diploma program Emergency Care HF and the medical master program of the HSG/UZH (Joint Medical Master St.Gallen)

Your application

  • Contact us and ask us – you will be surprised how comprehensive and specialized emergency medicine is understood in St. Gallen

About us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a versatile working environment. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to the common future. We focus on team spirit, interdisciplinary cooperation and innovative solutions.
