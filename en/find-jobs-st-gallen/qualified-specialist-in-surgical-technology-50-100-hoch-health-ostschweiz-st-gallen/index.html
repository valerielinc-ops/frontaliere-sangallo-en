Your tasks and perspectives

  • Instrumentation and assistance in transurethral and endourological procedures

  • Resilience, flexibility and openness to change
  • Commitment to interdisciplinary and constructive collaboration

  • Varied area of ​​work and comprehensive introductory concept

  • Working in an innovative environment with modern surgical methods

About Us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a diverse working environment. Three operating departments, 22 operating rooms and a working environment in which precision, teamwork and modern surgical techniques come together. In the OR department at HOCH, you will work in an interdisciplinary manner, at a university level and in a team that consciously combines experience and new impulses. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to our shared future. We rely on team spirit, interdisciplinary collaboration and innovative solutions.
