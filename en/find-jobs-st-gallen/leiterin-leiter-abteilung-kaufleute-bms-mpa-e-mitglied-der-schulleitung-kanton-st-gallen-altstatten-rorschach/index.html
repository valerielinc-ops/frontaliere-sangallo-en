Full description

The vocational and further education centre Rorschach-Rheintal BZR is an important commercial and industrial cantonal competence centre in Eastern Switzerland. At the Rorschach and Altstätten locations, around 1,900 learners from different cantons and nations are accompanied in their basic vocational training. The BZR combines sound vocational training, contemporary forms of learning, high practical relevance and close cooperation with teaching companies, professional associations and other educational partners. Vocational training (WZR) completes our educational offer. In a variety of courses and courses, more than 1,000 professionals expand their qualifications every year.

What to expect

At our location in Altstätten, you lead the department «Maufleute / Berufsmaturitätsschule / Medizin Praxisassistenz» and are responsible for the following topics:

  • Operational management of the department with around 30 employees
  • School development at department level (implementation of Vision BZR30+)
  • Implementation of resource and personnel planning
  • Implementation of quality management
  • Representation of the department outside
  • Teaching from mind. As a member of the school management, you are also involved in comprehensive school projects and in cantonal specialist committees.

What sets it apart

Are you a person with high social skills, successful professional experience in leadership and teaching and have the following characteristics:

  • strategic thinking, innovation, goal orientation and teamwork
  • Strong communication and organisational skills
  • independent, structured and efficient working method
  • Joy of working with young people
  • proven experience in project management
  • very good knowledge of vocational training, especially the commercial education landscape
  • university degree with an elective qualification as a teacher for upper secondary education and completed school management training, or The willingness to make them up

What we offer

They take on a versatile and meaningful leadership role in a well-positioned vocational and further education center with committed employees, strong educational partners and high regional importance. In addition to contemporary cantonal employment conditions, you will receive

  • the opportunity to actively shape the future of the department in an important development phase
  • A competent and experienced department team
  • an organisation with short distances, a high degree of personal responsibility and scope for design
  • An exciting environment between education, business and society
  • a task with a direct impact on the professional future of young people
