Full description

The Office for Military and Civil Protection is the cantonal service provider in the field of military, civil protection and civil protection and employs around 65 people in various professional fields.

What to expect

  • Ensuring lodge, telephone, postal and information services
  • Miscellaneous financial/informatics/staff support work
  • Organisation of the Periodic Protection Room Control (PSK) for the Canton of St.Gallen
  • Operation and resource planning for the PSC (civil protection personnel)
  • Miscellaneous support work in the field of compulsory protection

What sets it apart

  • Completed teaching as Eidg. Kauffmann/Kauffrau or equivalent education
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Broad IT knowledge
  • High level of service orientation
  • Good organizational skills
  • Basic technical interest

What we offer

  • Versatile and varied activity with a wide range of tasks
  • Professional and motivated working environment
  • Very independent work with a lot of personal responsibility and design room
  • Modern work equipment and well-equipped infrastructure
