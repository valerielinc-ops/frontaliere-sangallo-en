Full description

As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a medical secretary to reinforce our secretarial team of the Group of Paediatrics and Adolescent Medicine.

Their tasks

  • Telephone contact with patients and their relatives as well as other internal and external contacts
  • Organization and coordination of demanding appointments and dispatch of orders
  • Management of general administrative tasks in the secretarial area
  • Processing and coordination of the inbox (letter post and e-mails)
  • Management of patient records (electronic and paper)
  • Creating, editing and sending demanding medical correspondence

Your profile

  • Completion of training as medical practice assistant or commercial basic training in the medical field and further training as medical secretary
  • Professional experience in a similar capacity
  • Very good knowledge of medical terminology as well as a stylish handling of the German language in communication and correspondence
  • Structured, exact and independent working method
  • Empathetic, team-oriented personality with quick comprehension

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together

Your work area Secretarial staff in the various departments are responsible for day-to-day secretarial work, such as contingency, general correspondence and coverage of services. They support medical specialists in the preparation of office hours and ensure professional interdisciplinary cooperation. The secretariats are divided into 6 groups by department with a responsible group management: Adolescent Medicine & Pediatric Psychosomatics, Pediatric & Adolescent Medicine, Pediatric Surgery & Day Clinic, Orthopaedics, KER Centre and Stations. The interdisciplinary medical secretariat additionally supports the individual groups in the administrative area, mainly in reporting.
