10.09.2026 St. Gallen Member of the Board of Directors of Raiffeisenbank St.Gallen with banking profile

Become part of our team!

As the second-largest banking group in Switzerland, Raiffeisen is a leader in the retail business with its 208 independent Raiffeisen banks organised as cooperatives. Raiffeisenbank St.Gallen stands for closeness, trust and responsibility. As a locally anchored cooperative bank, we combine professional banking with a strong regional connection. We are a successful and locally oriented bank with 90 employees and around 16,000 cooperatives. As part of the succession arrangement, we are looking for a committed, independent and entrepreneurial personality who, together with the Board of Directors and in cooperation with the bank management, will continue to write the success story of our bank. The request for election will be submitted at the ballot in May 2027.

What do you expect?

As a member of the Board of Directors, you are jointly responsible for

  • the appropriate and effective supervision of the management of the Bank;
  • the financial situation and development of the Bank;
  • the development and definition of the strategy of Raiffeisenbank St.Gallen and the review of its implementation
  • Compliance with laws and regulations and risk management

Within the Board of Directors, you contribute your banking background. These include:

  • the assessment, analysis and discussion of credit transactions within the competence of the Board of Directors
  • the assessment of the credit transaction processes and controls to ensure credit settlement in accordance with the regulations
  • the periodic review of the development and structure of the credit portfolio on the basis of reporting provided;

What do you bring?

  • They identify with the cooperative values and the leadership principles of Raiffeisen and represent them convincingly.
  • They have deep experience in the financial services sector and have an in-depth understanding of financial products, business models and organizational structures. In addition, you have solid business and economic knowledge as well as proven experience in banking, especially in private and / or corporate banking.
  • They identify key opportunities and risks at an early stage, assess them in a differentiated manner and support the Board of Directors in determining suitable measures. You have a good understanding of the market, liquidity, credit, personnel and operational risks of a bank.
  • They are familiar with the regulatory and normative requirements of the Swiss financial market and are able to assess their impact on the strategic and operational orientation of a Raiffeisenbank in a well-founded manner.
  • They have knowledge of accounting, financial reporting and the control and monitoring processes of a bank. You also understand the requirements of an internal and external audit.
  • They are willing to spend the time required for the mandate and have the necessary flexibility to take on additional responsibility in the event of extraordinary events or special challenges.
  • They may hold office for at least two consecutive terms. The statutory age limit for members of the Board of Directors is 70 years.

Please note that the curriculum vitae (CV) must be signed for the application as a member of the Board of Directors in accordance with the current banking regulation. With a committed Board of Directors, a strong bank management and a competent team, we continue to develop Raiffeisenbank St.Gallen in a sustainable manner. for our members.
