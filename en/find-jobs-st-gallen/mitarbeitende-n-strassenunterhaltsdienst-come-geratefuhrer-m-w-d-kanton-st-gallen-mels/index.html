Full description

The National Roads Division VI of the Civil Engineering Office of the Canton of St.Gallen makes a significant contribution to the safety of the national roads in the cantons of Glarus, Thurgau and St.Gallen with its maintenance work. For this purpose, the territorial unit has four workshops in Thal, Mels, Biäsche and Oberbüren as well as two bases in St.Gallen and Müllheim.

What to expect

  • Signalling work for project construction sites
  • Care of greenery and forestry
  • Road and civil engineering work
  • Operation of vehicles and equipment
  • Winter service and accidents

What sets it apart

  • Craftsman with completed vocational training in one of the fields of horticulture, business maintenance, forestry or main construction
  • Team-capable, independent and reliable personality
  • Readiness for piquetting and assignments outside normal working hours
  • Enjoyment in all work of road maintenance and winter service
  • Driving licence Cat C/CE
  • Multitasking for operating a special equipment (Unimog) in green care
  • Accessibility of the workshop within 20 minutes

What we offer

  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
  • Exciting and interesting activities in a motivated team
  • Careful training
