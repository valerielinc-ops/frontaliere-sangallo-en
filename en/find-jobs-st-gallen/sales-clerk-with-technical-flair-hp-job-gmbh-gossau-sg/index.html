Full description

For our customer in 9200 Gossau SG we are looking for you for a permanent job with 80% time in technical order processing. The company manufactures electrostatic powder coating equipment and equipment. Your tasks

  • Advise customers and sales partners and clarify technical questions with the relevant specialist departments.
  • Record customer orders, process them and track their progress.
  • Make offers and confirm orders.
  • Coordinate deliveries with sales, purchasing, production and logistics and monitor the dates.
  • Process returns and organize subsequent deliveries.
  • Keep customer and master data up-to-date in the ERP.

You bring that with you

  • Commercial degree or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of practice in technical order processing, for example in mechanical or plant engineering
  • Good feeling for technical connections
  • Very good communication in German and English
  • Routine with ERP and MS Office, especially with Excel
  • You work independently and carefully, set priorities and keep track even with high utilization.
  • You enjoy working with different departments.

That is why it is worth it.

  • You benefit from attractive employment conditions and wage benefits.
  • You can contribute your own ideas and benefit from short decision-making paths.
  • You work in an international company with a good working atmosphere.
