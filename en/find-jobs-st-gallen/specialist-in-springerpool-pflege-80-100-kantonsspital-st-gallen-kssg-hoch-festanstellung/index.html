Specialist/in Springerpool Pflege 80-100 %

Springerpool

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • You coordinate and plan the assignments of the Springerpool in close cooperation with the management and make a significant contribution to a secure and needs-based personnel assignment planning
  • You are the contact person for our temporary employees and take on administrative tasks such as working time control, invoice control and the maintenance of personnel data
  • You support the management of Springerpool in interviews, quality development and cross-divisional projects
  • You actively contribute to the further development of the cross-location Springerpool and promote transparent cooperation with the various stakeholders

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH and professional experience in acute care
  • Experience in a coordinative function, for example as day manager, group management or in assignment planning, combined with an independent and structured working method
  • High social and communication skills as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Responsibility, discretion and the ability to keep track even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office applications as well as a high affinity for digital planning systems

Our offer to you

  • Responsible and versatile work with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and regulated working hours from Monday to Friday with flexibility
  • The opportunity to actively shape the further development of a cross-location springer pool
  • Close cooperation with management as well as insight into strategic and operational topics of resource management
  • Structured induction as well as attractive internal and external training opportunities
  • A motivated team with short decision-making paths and an open, appreciative cooperation
