Full description

Property valuer:in 40 - 60% ◆ Vaduz or Uznach ◆ m/f/d

Their role

Ideally, you start with a workload of 80-100% and take on a maternity representation for about half a year. Afterwards, you continue the function in job sharing with a workload of 40-60%.

  • Independent preparation and assessment of real estate valuations (single-family house, yield objects, commercial and industrial object to special object)
  • Cooperation and exchange with external evaluators
  • Professional support for credit management (and market organisation) in complex real estate financing
  • Assistance in the further development and optimization of processes, instructions and guidelines in accordance with regulatory requirements and current market standards

Your education and experience

  • Ideally, industry-standard further specialist training in real estate valuation (specialist ID, CAS, MAS Real Estate Management, real estate trustees, etc.)
  • Building knowledge and experience beneficial

Their way of working

  • They like to take responsibility and assert themselves with sensitivity
  • They show their own initiative, are resilient and flexible
  • They bring their own ideas and actively shape the further development of the Group Real Estate team.

Your team

The small and dedicated team works in partnership and is happy to tackle upcoming challenges with you

Your perspective with us
