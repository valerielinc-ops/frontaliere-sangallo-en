Put to the Point

Heavy vehicles and engines fascinate you? With your mechanical abilities, you ensure the functionality of the tanks in this extraordinary function and thus make a valuable contribution to the security of our country.

You can make this contribution

  • Repair and revise main and auxiliary equipment, running gear, steering and brakes
  • Install and remove units such as motors, transmissions, side drives, clutches, etc.
  • Carry out inspection and adjustment work on all vehicle components as well as deadline work on hull and chassis
  • Advise and accompany customers on the occasion of the versions and the returns regarding driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and check the vehicle inventory
  • Provide technical support for maintenance operations at decentralised shooting and weapon sites of up to 10 weeks per year

  • Cat B driving licence (cat C, C1E and SUVA-approved forklift of advantage)
  • Frequent and conscientious work and organisational skills to support a smooth operation
  • Service-oriented behaviour in dealing with customers
  • Cooperative and reliable behavior in the team
