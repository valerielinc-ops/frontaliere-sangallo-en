Full description

Give your job a new look FISBA is one of the world’s leading suppliers in the optics industry and stands for outstanding performance from optical design and system engineering to high-precision series production and coating. FISBA operates from its headquarters in Switzerland and branches in Germany, the USA and China. The company is privately owned. We employ over 360 employees from 30 nations worldwide. In the area of technology innovation, we are looking for an This is your future activity As an operation mechanic with a focus on maintenance, you ensure that our production facilities run reliably. They are there when a machine has a fault, detect technical weaknesses and prevent unnecessary downtimes with predictive maintenance.

In particular, its tasks include

  • Analyze mechanical faults on production machines and correct them professionally
  • Carry out maintenance, repair and repair work independently
  • Planning, implementing and documenting preventive maintenance measures
  • Assess machine states and initiate necessary measures at an early stage
  • Implement technical improvements to increase plant availability
  • Manufacture, procurement and management of replacement and wear parts
  • Participating in machine installations, commissioning and conversions
  • Coordinate and accompany external service technicians

We make these requirements They are mechanics by passion and do not let up until a cause of disruption is found and permanently fixed. Ideally, bring the following:

  • Completed training as a polymechanic, production mechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer, agricultural machinery mechanic or comparable basic technical training
  • Practical experience in the maintenance and repair of production machinery
  • Good knowledge of mechanics and pneumatics
  • Knowledge of hydraulics is an advantage
  • Safety in handling technical drawings, machine documentation and maintenance plans
  • An independent, careful and solution-oriented working method
  • Enjoying a versatile machine park and technical challenges
  • Team spirit, reliability and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as basic English and MS-Office knowledge
  • Willingness to cooperate in technical piketting
