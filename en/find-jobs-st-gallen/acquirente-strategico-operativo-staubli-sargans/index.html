Full description

At Stäubli, you work on technologies that are used in demanding industrial applications worldwide. For innovation to become possible, purchasing that combines security of supply, quality and cost-effectiveness is needed. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a personality that combines operational procurement and strategic purchasing. Your tasks

  • The focus is on the time, cost and quality-related procurement of electronic and electromechanical components and assemblies.
  • From receipt of the offer to delivery: You process offers, orders and order confirmations, monitor delivery dates and maintain relevant master and contract data in the ERP system.
  • With your purchasing expertise, you accompany development and industrialization projects; from prototype to series maturity.
  • In supplier management, you assess existing partners, qualify new suppliers and accompany quality and improvement measures.
  • Commercial negotiations and contract management are just as much a part of your responsibility as monitoring relevant procurement markets.
  • Depending on your experience, you will have the opportunity to help shape strategic purchasing and participate in cross-location cooperation within the division.
  • Basic technical or commercial training combined with several years of experience in purchasing.
  • Ideally, you already have experience in purchasing electronic components. Alternatively, you bring shopping experience from a manufacturing industrial company.
  • Experience in strategic purchasing and category management is welcome, but not a requirement.
  • Experience in project purchasing is an additional plus.
  • You have experience working with ERP systems; SAP knowledge is of great advantage.
  • You communicate safely in German and English.
  • Through negotiation skills, initiative and joy, you convince yourself to design a wide range of tasks independently.

You can look forward to that.

  • Broad range of tasks: From operational procurement to project purchasing and contract management to supplier evaluation, you can expect a varied purchasing function.
  • Design leeway: You get the opportunity to take on responsibility and develop your area of responsibility according to your experience and your strengths.
  • Flexible working: Flexible working hours and home office possibilities support a contemporary way of working.
  • Further development: You can develop professionally and personally and take on additional responsibility.
  • Attractive framework conditions: modern jobs, free parking, good public transport connections as well as attractive employment conditions and social benefits.

Interested? Are you looking for a shopping position that is not limited to a single task area? With us, you can assume operational responsibility, accompany projects, further develop suppliers and, depending on experience and interest, actively shape strategic purchasing. We look forward to meeting you.
