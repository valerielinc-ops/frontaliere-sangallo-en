Full description

The Office for Commercial Registry and Notaries maintains a public register in the area of commercial registers and performs notarizations in commercial register matters, matrimonial property and inheritance law. Furthermore, the notary is active in the area of administrative inheritance law and responsible for inheritance-related security measures as well as official inheritance distributions.

What to expect

  • Independent pre-examination and processing of new entries and changes at sole proprietorships and collective/common limited partnerships as well as branches of Swiss companies as well as independent processing of personnel mutations and changes of address in all legal forms
  • Independent processing and preliminary examination of registration transactions (consolidation, amendment of the articles of association, dissolution, etc.) of public limited companies, GmbH, cooperatives, associations, foundations and branches of domestic and foreign companies
  • Management of restructurings (merger, division, conversion and transfer of assets), legal matters relating to the registration of undertakings and opening of the procedure for obtaining registration
  • Management of cases in the field of official procedures and preparation of referral and opinions to the court
  • Advising companies, lawyers and trustees

What distinguishes you

  • Training as a legal agent, legal specialist, completed university of applied sciences, bachelor or master in law or comparable training
  • Ideally, professional experience in the fields of business, advocacy, notary or trust
  • Ability to respond to different points of view and arguments and develop viable solutions
  • Negotiation skills and pleasure in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Strong team skills
  • Precise, structured and efficient working methods and high service orientation

What we offer

  • A versatile activity in an exciting social environment
  • High independence and personal responsibility and at the same time an experienced, supportive team
  • Good training opportunities
  • Appealing workplace near St.Gallen Central Station with modern working conditions
