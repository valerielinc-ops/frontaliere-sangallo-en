Company overview

At McDonald’s, every visit is a fun and friendly experience. Our team loves welcoming customers and turning every moment into a memorable experience. If you enjoy interacting with people and are looking for a lively, dynamic job full of opportunities, you’re in the right place!

Job description

As a host, you are the face of the restaurant. You welcome guests with a smile, organize birthday parties and creative activities, and make sure everyone — especially the youngest — has a great time. You bring your energy, creativity and good humor to the team.

Main responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and create a relaxed and friendly atmosphere
  • Organize birthday parties and creative events for children
  • Give kitchen tours during guided visits
  • Support the team at the counter, in the McDrive or in the dining area during busy periods
  • Assist management with administrative tasks

Required qualifications

  • Open and positive personality
  • Enjoy interacting with people, especially children
  • Creative and full of ideas
  • Team player, reliable and motivated
  • Good communication skills (French required, other languages are a plus)

  • Compensation and employment conditions in accordance with the provisions of the CCNT
  • 50% off our products in restaurants in Switzerland
  • Discounted Sunrise subscriptions
  • Access to the Corporate Benefits platform
  • Additional vacation days offered to people over 50
  • Free access to the largest online training library
