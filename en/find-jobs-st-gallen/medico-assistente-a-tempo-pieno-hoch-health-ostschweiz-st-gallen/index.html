Assistant doctor / doctor 100%

General internal medicine, family medicine, emergency medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Care of complex patients under close supervision of the medical cadre

What you bring for this job

  • Enjoying constructive and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities

  • Employment possible in job sharing
