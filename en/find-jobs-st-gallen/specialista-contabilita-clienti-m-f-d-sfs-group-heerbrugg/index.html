Full description

As an Accounts Receivable Specialist, you combine your pay flair with your communication talent and take on responsibility in an international environment. You can expect a diverse and varied area of responsibility in which you can contribute your expertise and create real added value for our Group. With your know-how, you ensure an important part of our financial processes and thus make a decisive contribution to our corporate success. Your Tasks:

  • Checking, recording and recording of receipts
  • Account reconciliation Intercompany votes
  • Clarification and handling of differences and open positions

  • Establishment, maintenance and verification of customer master data
  • Monitoring risky customer claims and informing competent authorities Your profile:
  • Commercial training, ideally with further training as a clerk/accounting
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in accounts receivable
  • Experience in dunning
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of French and English and fluent knowledge of German
  • Analytical and networked thinking, numerical flair, conscientiousness and a structured and efficient working method We offer:
