Full description

Psycho-oncologist/in 40-80 %

Clinic of Internal Medicine, Palliative Care

Place of work Grabs

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Patient/internal support: crisis intervention, mental condition assessment, support in adaptation processes and disease management, resource activation and death support
  • Music Therapy: Complement the therapeutic conversation on a nonverbal, body-related level with the goals of relaxation, calming, distraction, pain relief and resource discovery
  • Family work and team support: crisis intervention, psychoeducation and stabilization in the illness and farewell process, short interventions and professional impulses for employees in stressful situations
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Close exchange in the weekly report with doctors, care, pastoral care, physiotherapy and social work; joint case management
  • Documentation: Daily, structured entries in the patient documentation system according to quality standards

What you bring for this job

  • Completed university degree in psychology (completed federally recognized psychotherapy education desired)
  • Further education and experience in psycho-oncology
  • Further education and experience in music therapy
  • Experience in palliative care and/or oncology desired
  • Engaged, resilient, flexible and team-capable personality with high social competence and initiative

Our offer to you

  • Meaningful task in the existential area of people
  • Engaged and interprofessional teams of palliative care and oncology
  • Regular intervision and internal training in palliative care
  • Responsible role with room for manoeuvre and confidence in your expertise
  • Various employee offers in the area of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
