Full description

Take quality to the next level together. Do you want to actively shape processes and make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of our quality management system? In this versatile function, you work closely with different departments, manage audits and support the further development of our business management system. Your Tasks:

  • Planning, implementation and follow-up of internal and external audits
  • Further development, maintenance and rollout of the Business Management System (BMS) of the Business Support Functions
  • Support process owners in documenting and optimizing processes
  • Processing and tracking of error messages as well as support in the implementation of corrective measures
  • Further development and maintenance of meaningful quality reporting as well as analysis of relevant quality indicators
  • Collaboration in Quality Improvement Projects (QIP) within the Business Support Functions Your profile:
  • Commercial vocational training or business studies completed
  • In-depth experience in quality management and knowledge of common systems and standards (e.g. ISO 9001, ISO 14001, IATF 16949, ISO 27001 or TISAX)
  • Many years of experience as an auditor
  • Very good knowledge of English (at least level B2)
  • Structured, independent and careful working methods as well as a strong quality awareness
  • Communication-strong, team-oriented personality with initiative and pleasure in continuous improvement We offer:
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual participation in success
  • Numerous sports offers & employee events
  • Staff restaurant with attractive prices
  • Varied tasks
