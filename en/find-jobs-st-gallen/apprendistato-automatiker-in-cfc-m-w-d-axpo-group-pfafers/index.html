Full description

As an EFZ automatist, you are a professional in the construction and development of electrical control and automation systems.

Start of teaching: 1 August 2027

Basic training: Landquart

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Wiring of control cabinets and switchgear
  • Control programming
  • Maintenance and repair of electrical equipment
  • Planning of small projects
  • Preparation of electrical diagrams, instructions and technical documents

Profile and skills

  • Completed elementary school at high level, good performance in mathematics and physics
  • Enjoying accurate and careful working methods
  • Interest in new technologies, electrical engineering and technical contexts
  • Craftsmanship, technical understanding and spatial imagination
  • Curiosity, perseverance and good concentration ability

About the team

As a learner, you will work with us in a well-established and future-oriented team.

You will complete the basic training in Landquart. There you will be optimally prepared for your teaching.

Start your apprenticeship with an eventful and practical introductory week together with many other learners. You can also expect 6 weeks of holidays, a contribution to your mobile subscription and a generous mobility contribution for your flexibility on the road. In addition, we reward good performance with great extras.

Look forward to a dynamic environment where you can get involved and grow beyond yourself.
