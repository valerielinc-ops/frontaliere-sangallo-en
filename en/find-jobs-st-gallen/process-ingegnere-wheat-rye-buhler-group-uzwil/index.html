Full description

As a Wheat & Rye Process Engineer, you will immerse yourself in the heart of the global grain and food business. You start up state-of-the-art milling plants worldwide, experience different countries and cultures, are in close contact with our customers, pass on your expertise in training and thus directly contribute to the fact that mills around the world produce efficiently, safely and in the best quality.

Tasks

  • They independently commission new machines and milling plants worldwide and ensure their successful transfer to our customers.
  • They train and advise our customers and their employees and impart technological and process know-how in a practical manner
  • They carry out mill inspections and other services at existing plants and identify targeted potential for improving process-related key figures
  • They develop technological processes and process diagrams based on individual customer requirements and accompany their implementation
  • You support sales, offering, project management, engineering, automation, R&D and product management in technological and process-related issues

Requirements

  • Completed training as a grain miller:in or process technologist/technologist in the mill and feed industry, supplemented by further training as a master:in, technician:in or a comparable qualification, ideally at the German Müllerschule Braunschweig or the Swiss Müllereifachschule St. Gallen
  • Several years of experience in the operation and maintenance of grain mills, combined with basic knowledge of electrical engineering and a strong affinity for automation
  • Good knowledge of German and English (minimum B1) as well as convincing communication skills in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial
  • Pronounced customer and service orientation, high personal responsibility and a routine handling of modern digital work equipment, in particular Microsoft 365
  • High flexibility and willingness to travel worldwide (>60%) as well as openness and joy in working with people from different cultures
