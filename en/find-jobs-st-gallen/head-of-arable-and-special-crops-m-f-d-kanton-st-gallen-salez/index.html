Full description

The Agricultural Centre of St.Gallen (LZSG) is part of the Agricultural Office. It fulfils a versatile mission in the agricultural consulting as well as in the education and training. What you're expecting:

  • Personal and professional management of the department with the specialist departments of agriculture, bees, vegetable, berry, fruit and viticulture and plant health
  • Active cooperation in the management of the LZSG
  • Self-employed management of the specialist department of vegetable farming with practical consulting activities
  • Management or support of cross-disciplinary projects of quality production, sustainable use of resources and innovations along the value chain
  • Management of practical experiments
  • Teaching in basic agricultural education and in higher vocational training of the Vocational and continuing training centre (BZBS) at the locations Buchs and Flawil What makes you stand out:
  • Master's degree in Agronomist/in ETH/FH
  • Perennial professional and management experience in a comparable position
  • Experience in the management and implementation of projects
  • Methodological and didactic knowledge desired
