Full description

The GBS St.Gallen is an important vocational and further education institution in Eastern Switzerland. In addition to commercial-industrial basic education with 4 500 learners in over 45 professions, we also train 600 students and course participants in the field of higher vocational training and further education (HBB/WB). Our mission statement of people-education-success is based on the principle of shared leadership responsibility, where cooperation and collaboration are at the heart of creating a dynamic learning and working environment based on diversity, innovation and continuous development. Creativity is more to you than good design? Do you enjoy promoting young people, advancing ideas and developing an entrepreneurial studio? Then a special task awaits you in the middle of the old town of St.Gallen: you combine the training of around 30 learners with the creative and economic development of our couture apprenticeship.

What to expect

  • Management of the apprenticeship according to educational and business criteria, including acquisition of customer projects
  • Implementation of the operational part of the Education Ordinance Clothing Designer:in EFZ
  • Leadership and further development of the team of three employees
  • Initiate, implement and accompany practice-oriented projects
  • Representation of the teaching studio vis-à-vis various stakeholders

What distinguishes you

  • Sectoral vocational training in the field of clothing design or production completed with a higher vocational examination, higher specialist examination or professional training in the field of professional practice
  • Practical professional experience of several years
  • You recognize creative scope, use it and can simultaneously deal with binding specifications and processes
  • Business thinking
  • Training as a vocational trainer: in or willing to obtain it
  • Communicative personality with strong social skills
  • Exemplary appearance and joy in dealing with learners

What we offer

With us, exciting challenges await you and a lot of space to help shape. The employment takes place according to cantonal guidelines. We look forward to your complete application documents. Send us your dossier until 29 November 2026 via the button below.
