Full description

Good job appointments start for us long before the contract is signed. They arise where people and corporate culture fit together. This is where you come into play: you inspire talent for SGKB, advise our managers on an equal footing and continuously develop our recruiting with new ideas and modern approaches. If you can assess people well, do not want to recruit according to Scheme F and like to move something, you are in the right place with us.

This is what you will do.

  • You will be responsible for the entire recruitment process, from tendering to selection and interviews to successful employment.
  • Together with our HR Business Partners, you will find the right solution for filling positions in all areas of the SGKB.
  • You accompany the leaders competently in the acquisition of new talents.
  • With your personal and appreciative nature, you inspire candidates for the SGKB and ensure an outstanding candidate experience from first contact to employment.
  • You keep an eye on current recruiting trends, bring fresh ideas and continuously develop processes.

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training and higher further education in the HR area at at least the specialist card level form your professional basis. These include, for example, the Swiss Federal Professional Card as HR specialist or a comparable training at HF or FH level.
  • Several years of experience in operational recruiting as well as very good knowledge in dealing with LinkedIn recruiters. Experience in the financial or banking environment is an advantage.
  • With your strong consulting and communication skills as well as your good feeling for people and different target groups, you convince executives as well as candidates.
  • New recruiting methods, trends and technologies awaken your curiosity and you enjoy developing existing approaches and putting new ideas into practice.
  • An open, solution-oriented and team-oriented personality as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders sets you apart.

Information Martin Knechtle, HR Business Partner, telephone 071 231 31 06, is happy to answer your questions.

Application www.sgkb.ch/karriere, Martin Knechtle, HR Business Partner For this position, no applications from external personnel service providers are considered.
