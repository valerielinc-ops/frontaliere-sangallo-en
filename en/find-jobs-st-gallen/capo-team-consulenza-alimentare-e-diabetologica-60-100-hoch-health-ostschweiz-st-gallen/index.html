Department of Endocrinology, Clinic of Internal Medicine

Place of work Uznach

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Team management and thus contact person of the nutrition and diabetes consultancy
  • Diverse outpatient and stationary consulting activities with the possibility of individual specialization
  • Assistance of students in practical training
  • Administrative and organizational activities such as assignment planning, absence management, various discussions with employees
  • Representation in the team in various specialist committees (e.g. nutrition commission), inter-hospital cooperation with team leaders from the other locations St. Gallen, Wil, Grabs

What you bring for this job

  • Completed studies in nutrition and dietetics (HF/BSc/MSc) with the professional qualification of the Canton of St. Gallen and the SRC recognition for qualifications from abroad
  • Leadership training is beneficial
  • Engaged, resilient and team-oriented personality with good IT skills
  • An independent and flexible working method with a high sense of responsibility
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Organizational talent with an eye for the whole – They think in a networked and cross-divisional way

Our offer to you

  • Working in a future-oriented, innovative environment with modern technology, including BIA measuring device
  • Leading an open-minded, motivated and collegial team
  • Support for subject-specific training
  • Responsible work in a work environment characterized by quality, respect and appreciation
  • Diverse employee benefits around health, work and family – including attractive perks and more
