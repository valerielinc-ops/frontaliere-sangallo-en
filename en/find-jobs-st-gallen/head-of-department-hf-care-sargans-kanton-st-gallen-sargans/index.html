Full description

The Vocational and Further Training Center for Health and Social Professions St.Gallen - BZGS - trains around 3,000 apprentices and students in various basic and further training professions in the areas of health, social affairs and home economics. The BZGS is a dynamic and innovative competence center. For our dynamic HF nursing department in Sargans, we are looking for a committed leader as head of the HF nursing department. In this role, you will lead and shape the further development of the department and shape a modern and future-oriented learning environment. Our HF Nursing training program is characterized by problem-based learning (PBL) and a modern, digitally supported learning environment. This is where didactic innovation, practical relevance and professional leadership come together.

What to expect

▪ You lead and accompany the lecturers, promote their pedagogical competence and create an inspiring working atmosphere ▪ You work closely with the other department heads, the secretariat and the school management ▪ You design and evaluate the lessons and are close to the students and the learning processes ▪ You develop the department professionally, use the Learning Management System (LMS) specifically to increase quality and work closely with practical institutions This unique mix makes the function one varied and creative leadership tasks.

What sets you apart

▪ Specialization in the field of nursing (Bachelor / NDS / HFP) ▪ Pedagogical qualification (certified vocational school teacher / lecturer HF or adequate qualifications) ▪ Enjoyment of innovation, new forms of learning and the further development of digital learning environments ▪ Leadership, communication skills and joy in taking responsibility together in the management team of further training ▪ Experience in project work as well as in collaboration with educational partners and practical institutions ▪ High Motivation to accompany a professional team and at the same time to participate effectively in the classroom
