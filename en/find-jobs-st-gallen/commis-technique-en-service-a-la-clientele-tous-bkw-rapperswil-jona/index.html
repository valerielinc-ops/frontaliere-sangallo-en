Full description

Their tasks

  • You take over the first-level support in customer service.
  • You process telephone and written inquiries from customers and business partners competently and service-oriented.
  • They maintain, manage and update our customer database.
  • They handle the general correspondence independently and reliably.
  • They create customer service offers and assist in tracking them.
  • They provide technical support on behalf of the management.
  • They organize and coordinate internal and external events.
  • You plan and schedule the assignments of our service technicians.
  • They assist in the preparation and processing of accounts payable and accounts receivable.
  • They perform a wide range of administrative in-house tasks, in particular in the areas of tool, warehouse and equipment management.
  • They contribute to the smooth running of daily business processes and work closely with the various divisions of the company.

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training as an electrical installer or assembly electrician
  • Professional experience in a comparable position is beneficial
  • Team-capable, committed and service-oriented personality
  • Strong social skills and pleasure in dealing with customers and employees
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Secure handling of MS Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio)
  • Knowledge of the industry software Volta and Abacus is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational skill as well as high customer and service orientation
  • Willingness to take responsibility and actively contribute to team success
