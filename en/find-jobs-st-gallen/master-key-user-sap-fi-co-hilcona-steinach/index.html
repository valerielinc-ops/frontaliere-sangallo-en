Full description

Interested in a key role in which you actively shape financial processes and act as the link between Finance, Business and IT? At Hügli in Steinach, which is part of Bell Food Group, a versatile role awaits you, bringing together solid SAP FI/CO expertise, a strong understanding of numbers and strong project management skills. As Master Key User, you are the linchpin for further developing and sustainably harmonizing our financial processes across national borders.

We can only consider applicants who are authorized to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We consider direct applications only.

Job information

  • Contract type: Permanent
  • Workload: 80% - 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible working hours
  • Start date: Immediately
  • Language: German
  • Work location: Hügli Nährmittel AG, Bleichestrasse 31 9323 Steinach

Tasks

  • Process architecture: You design and standardize the SAP FI/CO modules across the Group and ensure that our systems are optimally aligned with the needs of the country subsidiaries
  • Standardization: The development of global guidelines and best practices is in your hands to ensure maximum transparency and efficiency throughout the Group
  • Business partnering: As the central contact, you bridge the gap between specialist departments, IT and the local finance teams
  • Support & Training: You empower the local key users through targeted training (DE/EN) and act as a competent 1st-level support contact for complex questions, additionally acting as the interface between 1st- and 2nd-level support
  • Project management: Exciting SAP Fi/Co roll-outs in countries such as Italy, the Netherlands or the UK, as well as the development of modern controlling and analysis tools (e.g. BI, -profit center accounting etc.) will shape your agenda for the coming years
  • Consolidation: You support Group-wide consolidation processes, ensure a consistent data foundation and assist with the further development of the corresponding processes and system solutions, for example in Jedox

Requirements

  • SAP FI/CO expertise: You have extensive and several years of experience with SAP FI/CO – ideally as a key user, master key user or in a comparable business-related role. Your focus is on processes and their further development; in-depth programming or customizing knowledge is not required
  • Affinity for numbers: You have a strong understanding of financial interrelationships, analyses and key figures. You do not have to be a traditional controller – what matters is that you are comfortable in the world of numbers and understand finance processes from a professional perspective
  • Consolidation & Reporting: You are familiar with consolidation, planning and reporting processes and ideally have already worked with corresponding BI or performance management solutions such as Jedox.
  • Analytical acumen: You enjoy understanding complex business processes and translating them into smart system solutions
  • International spirit: Since we operate globally, you have excellent English skills for negotiations and enjoy intercultural collaboration as a matter of course
  • Can-do attitude: You have the necessary willingness to embrace change to not only introduce new standards but also embed them sustainably within the company
  • Project experience: You have sound project management experience and are accustomed to coordinating complex projects and different stakeholders in a structured manner

What we offer

  • Work-life balance: At our company, you benefit from a 40-hour week, at least 25 vacation days and a flexitime system. In addition, you can arrange your working days between the office and mobile office.
  • Catering: We attach great importance to culinary offerings; freshly prepared meals in our staff restaurant bring this to life for our employees.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient access to public transport, ample parking options and reimbursement of your travel expenses for business trips – travel sustainably and worry-free!
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive training opportunities, high-quality training at the Coop Education Center and diverse personal development opportunities such as mentoring and coaching.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health-supporting services and exclusive employee discounts thanks to the Coop employee discount card!
  • Fair wages and social benefits: Secure your future with our above-average pension provision, receive anniversary bonuses and attractive bonuses for employee referrals. In addition, you receive the Coop employee discount card and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Arrange your working hours flexibly with part-time arrangements and experience an inclusive working environment in which diversity is valued and promoted!
  • Corporate events: Experience unforgettable moments at our corporate events and strengthen team spirit at the company run—celebrate, get active, and make connections together!
