Senior Laboratory Specialist — CSL Behring

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are currently looking for a Senior Laboratory Specialist to join our team in St. Gallen. Your main responsibilities will include the independent execution and documentation of syntheses and analytical tests; process development and production support for existing and new products; qualification and maintenance of laboratory equipment; compliance with technical, organizational and staff safety measures etc.

Key Tasks

  • Independent synthesis, characterization and documentation of synthetic compounds (eg. complex iron compounds, ..) in lab and pilot scale
  • Performing analytical method development
  • Feedback to the laboratory manager in the event of unexpected observations during execution of experiments
  • Performing qualification of laboratory equipment (execution and documentation)
  • Carrying out work in accordance with cGMP regulations
  • Maintaining laboratory quality systems and records of synthetic protocols and analytical results
  • Apprenticeship education
  • Following measures to protect health and the environment such as: prescribed in the safety organization INS020046SI
  • Regular controlling of the status and utilization of staff protective gear support and usage of safety devices and protective equipment
  • Inducting of new employees in the laboratory organization
  • Organizing routine maintenance of laboratory equipment
  • Requesting quotes for routine maintenance and new equipment
  • Maintaining laboratory operations (eg. raw material and consumables, …)
  • Subject matter expert for enquiries from external partners and for on-site inspections
  • Creation and revision of laboratory test procedures

Your Profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as a lab technician or comparable education with ≥ 3 years professional experience or
  • Bachelor’s degree in chemistry or comparable education
  • English and German oral and written
  • Professional experience in scale-up of chemical processes
  • Excellent computer skills (eg. MS Office, especially Excel; tiamoTM, PerkinElmer Signals Notebook)
  • Creative thinking, curious about scientific and technical innovation
  • Quick learner, adapting thinking to complex environments
  • Experience in a cGMP environment

Are you interested? We are looking forward to receiving your online application.
