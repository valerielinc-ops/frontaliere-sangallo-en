Full description

The gastronomy of the Migros Ostschweiz cooperative is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether it's a Migros restaurant, Migros Take away, a community catering restaurant or catering services – we offer every customer and every kundin a tasteful experience.

Mansioni

  • You present the dishes in the salad and cake buffet creatively and matching the time of day and the season to offer the guests an optical and culinary experience.
  • You take care of, advise and serve our customers in the restaurant and at the cash desk with professionalism and cordiality.
  • You make sure that the hygiene regulations are adhered to and consistently implemented according to food safety and carry out various cleaning work (also dishes) to guarantee an all-round well-kept operation.
  • In case of bottlenecks you are an important support in the kitchen area and thus provide for full moments with our guests. - The management of the in-house kiosk and ensuring an appealing product presentation prepares you fun.

Requisiti

  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience: mandatory in cold and warm kitchen
  • Completed basic or further education: Advantage
  • You love being in contact with people and enjoy inspiring our guests.
  • You like working in a team, are reliable, flexible and support your colleagues.
  • Self-employed and accurate work are self-evident for you.
  • You are ready to continuously train yourself and learn new things.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Corporate parking: free parking directly at the workplace
  • L-GAV: Attractive national employment contract
  • Social benefits: Very good social benefits in the areas of child allowances, sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Cum-Sensual-Points: Multiples of the Cum-Sensual-Points on Food- and Nonfood-Articles
  • Leisure and Culture-offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
