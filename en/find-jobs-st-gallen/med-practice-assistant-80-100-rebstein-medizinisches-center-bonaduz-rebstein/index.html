Full description

The Medical Center Praxis Rebstein is a practice of the MCM Group, which offers outpatient primary medical care. For our medical practice we are looking for a medical practice assistant (MPA) / a med. Practical assistants (MPA).

Their tasks include

  • Reception and care of our patients
  • Scheduling and telephone triage
  • Work in the laboratory and digital X-ray and practice pharmacy
  • Practice administration (ordering, performance recording, billing and insurance clearance)
  • Consultation hours assistance

They bring with them

  • Completed apprenticeship as MPA (EFZ) with laboratory and X-ray knowledge
  • Joy and enthusiasm for your profession
  • friendly attitude and high service orientation in dealing with patients
  • independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Good computer skills
  • Emergency preparedness

We offer you

  • An appreciative atmosphere in a motivated family team
  • Pleasant working atmosphere
  • An attractive, modern and versatile workplace
  • Regular working hours

Interested? We look forward to your application! Please send your documents by e-mail to Sina Rüesch: operativeleitung@mcm-group.ch or via our application form.
