Full description

The opening of the Age Psychiatric Day Clinic is planned for April 1, 2027. You will already be part of the team in the preparatory introductory phase and actively involved in the development of the day clinic until its opening. Your tasks

  • In the daily clinical setting, you take over the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and psychoeducational treatment of patients aged 60 and over in group setting and lead preliminary discussions on the indication for the daily clinical program.
  • The focus of your work lies in the group therapy area with indication-specific and cross-disorder group offers such as psychoeducation, ACT and interactional discussion groups.
  • Case-related documentation, reports and performance recording in the clinic information system are part of your everyday life and are supported by the medical management and the secretariat.
  • In the team, you will experience an interprofessional cooperation at eye level with short decision paths and will be actively involved in the further development of the daily clinical concept.
  • Regular external supervision, internal case discussions and individual professional support by an experienced psychotherapeutic and psychiatric management accompany you in your professional development.
  • The close cooperation with the Old Age Psychiatric Ambulatorium at the St. Gallen location, the outpatient case management and, if you are interested, the participation in the neuropsychological investigation team complement your versatile area of responsibility. Your profile
  • With your completed psychology studies and initial experience in inpatient adult psychiatry, you will have a sound basis for this activity.
  • Your started or already advanced training in a federally recognized psychotherapeutic procedure forms an important basis for your further professional development.
  • For our team, we are looking for a personality who understands group therapy not only as a method, but as an important component of modern patient care and wants to actively contribute.
  • Your interest is in day-to-day clinical and interdisciplinary treatment, in which the individual needs of patients and relatives are consistently the focus.
  • Interprofessional cooperation, short decision-making paths and flat hierarchies are just as much appreciated as the opportunity to actively participate in everyday work with your own initiative and sense of responsibility.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least C1 level as well as your motivation for continuous professional development round off your profile. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
