Officer: in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Officer:in St. Gallen, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

At 15 locations in Switzerland, the SBB Fire Brigade - Intervention with 350 dedicated employees and modern emergency vehicles ensures fast and safe event management. At the same time, we are railway specialists, train drivers and firefighters and take care of the safety of our customers around the clock. Through your cooperation, you help ensure that rail traffic runs smoothly again as quickly as possible. As part of disruption management, Intervention SBB supports public emergency organisations and is actively involved in event prevention. You can move that.

  • In this varied position, you will lead the 24-hour shift at the intervention location in St. Gallen.
  • In the event of incidents, you assume responsibility for the fire department or the role of “Operation Manager: in SBB” and are thus responsible for the entire event management on site as well as the quickest possible return of the operation to an orderly state.
  • You are jointly responsible for the protection of people, animals, the environment and railway infrastructure.
  • You assess the situation on site, discuss the operational measures to be taken with the operations centre and ensure the security of the external event services and customers. During the entire deployment phase, you are responsible for creating the necessary documents, reports, journals, tracking the situational picture, etc. and, if necessary, ensure debriefing with the intervention team.
  • In the event of an incident, you save travelers, fight fires, provide oil defense and technical assistance.
  • You train the various workflows and techniques on a regular basis to ensure smooth event management at all times.

You bring that with you.

  • Experience as an active firefighting officer: with strong leadership and social skills as well as respiratory protection suitability form the basis for this exciting activity.
  • You have physical and psychological resilience as well as high performance and commitment and enjoy working in a team.
  • Readiness for shift service 5 x 24h model on site as well as Pikett at the weekend. To do this, you can reach the location within the given time of 15 minutes after alerting or are ready to stay at the location. Furthermore, you are ready to take over detachments at other locations.
  • You bring at least 3 years of experience in a similar function at SBB or an external intervention organization (firefighting, police, medical, etc.).
  • You have completed basic training (EFZ) or equivalent training, have the category B driving licence and ideally also have the C1-118 driving licence.
  • You have an oral and written knowledge of German (level C2).
