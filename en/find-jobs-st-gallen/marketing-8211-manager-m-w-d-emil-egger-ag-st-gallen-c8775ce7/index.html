Full description

Emil Egger AG is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies and offers its customers a comprehensive range of services in the areas of general cargo, heavy cargo and warehouse logistics. At 11 locations, around 800 employees currently work for well-known clients from industry, trade and trade. In order for our services, our people and our brand to become visible, we are looking for a versatile and initiative personality who develops our marketing with creativity, personal responsibility and energy. You work at our headquarters in St.Gallen. With us, you do not expect a narrow area of responsibility, but a wide range of marketing and communication tasks. You bring your own ideas, implement them independently and ensure that good approaches become convincing results.

Tasks

  • Marketing from A to Z: You plan and realize versatile marketing measures and develop our market presence continuously. From the website to customer information to internal communications.
  • Social Media & Content: You manage our social media channels, develop suitable content and create posts, pictures, videos and stories from our everyday business.
  • Website & digital channels: You maintain our website, keep content up to date and recognize opportunities to further develop our digital presence.
  • Creative implementation: You design presentations, flyers, brochures and other marketing documents or coordinate external partners where it makes sense.
  • Projects & Events: You organize and accompany marketing projects, trade fair appearances and events and ensure that everything fits together.
  • Employees: You approach our employees and departments, recognize exciting stories and make visible what distinguishes us.

We expect

  • A training or further education in marketing, communication or a similar field of expertise
  • Several years of professional experience in marketing with a broad, practice-oriented understanding of tasks and enthusiasm for the topic of transport and logistics
  • Practical experience in dealing with AI (ChatGPT, claude.ai etc.)
  • Experience in supporting social media as well as in creating and preparing content
  • A good feeling for texts, pictures, design and the right approach to different target groups
  • Safety in dealing with digital communication channels and common marketing tools
  • An independent, structured way of working and the ability to manage various tasks and projects
  • Creativity, initiative and joy in not only developing ideas, but also implementing them yourself
  • An open, uncomplicated way and joy of working with people from different fields

We offer

  • An interesting and varied activity in a dynamic company
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Free parking
  • Good working atmosphere, set up small team
  • Family culture / Du culture
