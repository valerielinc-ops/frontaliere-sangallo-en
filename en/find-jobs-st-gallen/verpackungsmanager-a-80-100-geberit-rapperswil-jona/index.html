MAIN TASKS

As a packaging manager, you play a key role at Geberit: You control and take responsibility for all packaging topics, set central impulses at the headquarters and shape the strategic orientation in the Group – at the same time you develop the packaging managers in the plants:

  • Implementation and further development of packaging strategy and processes
  • Definition and implementation of the further steps of the PPWR
  • Packaging consultancy for development teams and production teams
  • Optimization and standardization together with purchasing and production
  • management of projects. (e.g. redesign, material changes)
  • Close cooperation with: Product Management, Purchasing, Logistics and Production

PROFILE

You have an education as a packaging engineer or an equivalent qualification or many years of experience in the packaging industry as well as a further education HF/FH and more than 10 years of professional experience and convinces by the following strengths:

  • Focus on simple and economical solutions in the sense of the company
  • Solution-oriented, practical team player with assertiveness and organizational talent
  • Convincing and addressable communication style and a skillful hand in dealing with stakeholders
  • Business knowledge and experience in project management
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • User knowledge of MS Office, SAP in turn is an advantage
