You want a job where each of your handles counts? At SBB, you have the chance to apply your skills and at the same time acquire new skills that enrich and advance you professionally. You have completed a vocational apprenticeship in the mechanical and craft sector and would like to make a difference in an exciting and innovative working environment that makes sense? Then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for support at these locations of German-speaking Switzerland: Zurich, Sargans, Brugg, Rapperswil, Winterthur and Gossau SG. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. For this, you can train during the week in the fitness center, enjoy the sun in the Badi, shop without queuing – simply make your free time more flexible. Pikett, night and weekend work are paid for with us. Depending on the stakes, we pay between 300 and 700 CHF per month. You also get 10-30% time surcharges. This means you can compensate for these time surcharges with free time. Our other benefits such as children's allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under " Benefits".   • You have completed an EBA or EFZ basic mechanical training (production mechanic:in, polymechanic:in, agricultural machinery mechanic:in, general mechanic:in, locksmith:in in the construction industry or similar professions, e.g. landscaper:in).
