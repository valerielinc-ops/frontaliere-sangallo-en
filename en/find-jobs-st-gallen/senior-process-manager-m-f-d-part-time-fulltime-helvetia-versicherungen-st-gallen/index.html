Business Analysis / Requirements Engineering Pensum: 50 - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • Identification and implementation of process improvements, standardization and automation as well as active co-creation of new processes in the context of AI solutions
  • Development and maintenance of process models, process maps, business processes and workflows
  • Determination, implementation and evaluation of process measurement variables and key figures
  • Coordination and support in the management of risks and controls along business processes
  • Ensuring process quality and supporting management with regard to continuous process optimization
  • Conducting process analyses, workshops and stakeholder management at all hierarchical levels
  • Management and support of process optimization and transformation initiatives in close cooperation with departments and IT
  • Assuming process-related responsibility for entire topics

What you bring

  • Studies in business administration (FH or university) or comparable training with specific further training in process management
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the field of quality and process management and sound methodological competence in the field of process management (knowledge in the Lean Six Sigma methodology is advantageous)
  • Experience from the insurance industry is an advantage
  • High affinity for AI and analytical skills
  • Communicative and sociable personality with high customer orientation
  • Style-confident knowledge of German, English and French desirable
