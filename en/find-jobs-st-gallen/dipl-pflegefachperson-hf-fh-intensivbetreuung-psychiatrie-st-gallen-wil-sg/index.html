Role overview

The three admission and emergency departments and the forensic department at the Wil site treat mentally ill adult patients with disorders from the entire psychiatric diagnosis spectrum.

You will be available as a guard and to support intensive care for patients in exceptional psychiatric situations.

The assignments as a guard are mainly in the evening (18:00 to 03:00) or at night (21:00 to 07:00) in 24/7 operation.
