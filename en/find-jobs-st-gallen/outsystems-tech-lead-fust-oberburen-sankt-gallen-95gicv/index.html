Switzerland buys at Fust! We are proud to be a leader in electrical household appliances, multimedia/entertainment electronics and kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and with our comprehensive range of services and unique services we create real added value! Become part of our team and help us continue to create customer experiences! A job at Fust, and it’s running!

Tasks

  • Ownership for the Outsystems platform, from planning to further development to stable operation
  • Ensuring the required quality by controlling CI/CD and deployments, production releases, code and architecture reviews, refactoring, monitoring, tests, performance analyses, bug fixing and documentation of applications and interfaces
  • Planning and development of business applications in close cooperation with Solution Architects, Enterprise Architects and Developers, including preparation of user stories, maintenance of reusable templates and active participation in software development
  • Professional management of internal and external developers including selection and training, supplemented by sprint and resource planning, implementation of sprint demos and responsibility for cost estimates

Requirements

  • Completed studies in the commercial field or in the field of business informatics
  • Many years in the development of enterprise applications with outsystems as well as proven leadership experience in development management with hands-on mentality
  • Experience in the development and implementation of integrations with enterprise applications, preferably SAP, as well as in the implementation of business requirements in scalable technical solutions
  • In-depth knowledge in technical project management, development coordination, software architecture, APIs, integrations, ALM as well as agile process models, CI/CD, deployment and release management
  • Structured, implementation-oriented and communicative personality with a high level of initiative, good knowledge of German and English
  • Retail or omnichannel experience is beneficial
