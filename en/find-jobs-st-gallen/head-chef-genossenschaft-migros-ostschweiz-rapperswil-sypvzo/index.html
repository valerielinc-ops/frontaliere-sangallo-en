Full description

Want to become our key figure? Directly on Lake Zurich, we operate the gastronomy of a university of applied sciences with around 600 guests every day. In addition, there are various caterings for about six months a year, from exclusive 5-course dinners to large events with 1,500 people. For this varied management task, we are looking for a committed personality as a chef.

Luogo di lavoro: Community gastronomy, GG OST Rapperswil, Oberseestrasse 10, 8640 Rapperswil

Important information

Operations Monday to Friday and occasionally on Saturday. 4 weeks of company holidays per year.

Mansioni

  • You take over the operational and personnel management of a four-member kitchen team and ensure a smooth flow in everyday kitchen life. With your commitment, you create an environment in which guests and employees feel equally comfortable
  • You ensure a consistently high food quality and are responsible for purchasing, goods management and calculation
  • You plan, organize and realize caterings of different sizes
  • You promote cooperation in the team and support the professional and personal development of our employees and learners
  • You ensure compliance with all hygiene, quality and safety standards and monitor their consistent implementation
  • You bring ideas to the further development of our offer and our processes and implement them together with your team.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience and proven management experience: in the gastronomy / hotel industry
  • Completed basic or further education: in gastronomy (cookchef or system gastronomy specialistwoman)
  • You attach great importance to quality and freshness, cook with passion and inspire our customers with your positive and committed manner
  • The system catering fascinates you and you appreciate a dynamic environment with different guest needs and changing frequencies
  • You bring your first leadership experience from a comparable company and want to further develop your leadership and expertise in a targeted manner
  • You promote an appreciative cooperation in the team and move forward as an example
  • Thanks to your networked way of thinking, your quick comprehension and your hands-on mentality, you can keep track even in hectic situations.
  • You have in-depth knowledge in calculation, goods management and in dealing with appropriate systems
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Working hours without room hour: 41h week and regular working hours without room hour
  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Handy subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
