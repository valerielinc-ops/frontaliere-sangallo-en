Full description

Become part of our team at Lüchinger + Schmid in Flawil! Are you ready to take responsibility and cook, dye and pack around 180,000 eggs of the highest quality every day? Then we are looking for you as a plant operator in production (100%) in our dyeing plant!

Luogo di lavoro: Lüchinger+Schmid, Toggenburgerstrasse 23, 9230 Flawil

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You serve our cooking and dyeing plant and ensure that the eggs are perfectly cooked, coloured and packaged.
  • You monitor and optimize production quality and make sure everything runs smoothly.
  • Minor disturbances at the plants are repaired independently.
  • You implement the production plans while maintaining the highest quality and hygiene standards.
  • You actively work on the line and take it where it is needed.
  • At the daily shop floor you are actively involved and bring your ideas.

Requirements

  • A few years of experience in a manufacturing company – ideally in the food industry.: Initial leadership experience is beneficial, but not a must.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the food industry or a technical profession.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing (minor level B1)
  • Experience in handling food plants and packaging machines.
  • Passion for food production and a high sense of responsibility for quality.
  • Willingness to work in 2-shift operation (05:00 to 14:30 & 14:30 to 23:30) and to jump in on a Saturday before Easter and Christmas.
  • A good physical condition – you like to take part.
  • A truck ID (R2 and S2) as well as experience with SAP and the common office tools.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Contatto
