Full description

Social affairs and culture, municipal supervision, civil rights and civil status, commercial registers, notaries and bankruptcy: as versatile as the activities of the Department of the Interior is the work in the legal service of the Department, which is part of the General Secretariat.

What to expect

  • You will deal independently with appeals in the department’s area of competence.
  • You prepare legal opinions and conduct legal clarifications on behalf of the department management, offices and other departments of the administration.
  • You provide legal information to various stakeholders.
  • You participate in strategic projects and working groups, such as legislative procedures.

What distinguishes you

  • You have completed university studies (MLaw or lic.iur.).
  • Preferably, you bring professional experience in the processing of appeals.
  • You are interested in state and administrative law, in particular in the areas of social welfare law, municipal law and civil law.
  • You like to work independently, stay decision-making even in complex and unclear questions, think purposefully and are used to participating in project organizations.
  • You have strong analytical skills, a quick comprehension and precise expression in words and writing.
  • You enjoy exchanging your knowledge and experiences in discussions.

What we offer

  • You contribute to ensuring and further developing the application of the law in important, politically sensitive areas of our canton
  • Demanding questions from a wide range of fields, references to current developments and participation in the department’s central office
  • Attractive employment conditions with the possibility for home office and a working environment in the historic government building in the middle of the old town of St.Gallen
  • Individual development opportunities in the field of legal remedies processing as well as in broader areas of strategic administrative activity - informally as well as in the context of further training

Contact: Department of the Interior, General Secretariat
