Full description

MAIN TASKS

This function includes the management (approx. 30% of the work) of the employees and an operational function (approx. 70%) in the daily business of the conveyor technology. In addition to the planning and prioritization of the work, you are also the contact person for the customers in the area of intralogistics. The following activities are among your main tasks:

  • Ensure the readiness to use: responsibility for electrical maintenance, maintenance and reinstallation of conveyor technology in compliance with safety and environmental requirements. - Efficient maintenance & fault minimisation : Cost-conscious maintenance with the aim of reducing production interruptions to a minimum. - Programming & technical competence : Programming and support of the control systems of conveyor systems as well as driverless transport systems (FTS).
  • Continuous improvement & projects : Active introduction of suggestions for improvement, cooperation in projects and support for data transmission and implementation. - Support & flexibility : Technical support for logistics and central services, piket service, special assignments as well as application for spare parts and further training.

PROFIL

You will be awarded an electro-installator/automatist EFZ and have a further training in control technology as well as initial management experience. Optimally, you already have an operating electrician permit according to NIV Art. 13 or are ready to acquire it. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities are distinguished:

  • Good PLC programming skills (Siemens and B&R)
  • Connected thinking and project implementation experience to increase plant availability
  • Well-founded knowledge in Microsoft Office and SAP
  • Team capability
  • willingness to shift working hours (06:00
  • 15:00 / 08:30 – ca. 17:00), individual special assignments (night/weekend) and piket service for about one week per month

ADDITIONAL NOTES

We only take direct applications into account for this position.
