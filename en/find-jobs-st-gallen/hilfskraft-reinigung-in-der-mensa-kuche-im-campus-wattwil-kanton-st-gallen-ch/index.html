Full description

Together, the Wattwil Cantonal School (KSW) and the Vocational and Continuing Education Centre Toggenburg (BWZT) form the Wattwil Campus, which is visited by around 1,200 students and learners every day. Around 250 employees full-time or part-time for the campus.

What to expect

The cafeteria is the heart of the new Wattwil campus, which includes the Kanti Wattwil and the Vocational and Further Education Centre Toggenburg. The cafeteria focuses on a regional, seasonal and modern offer of food and is frequented by around 300 students and employees every day.

What sets it apart

  • Joy and interest in working with young people and in a school environment
  • Good constitution and willingness to work physically
  • Team-oriented, communicative work method For our kitchen team we are looking for a motivated and independent personality. Your duties include:
  • Assistance in washing dishes
  • Assistance in cleaning the kitchen and the guest room
  • Compliance with hygiene, order and food guidelines (HACCP)
  • Assistance in special events and school events

What we offer

We offer a responsible and varied job in a lively school organization with regular working hours and little evening and weekend work. It expects you a school climate characterized by appreciation and a respectful and well-functioning team. The work is divided into three days per week from 11.00 to 15.30. During school holidays, the canteen is closed and there is no work.
