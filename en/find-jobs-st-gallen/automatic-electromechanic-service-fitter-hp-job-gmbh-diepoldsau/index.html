Full description

For our customers, innovative and internationally active industrial companies, we are looking for motivated specialists in the fields of electrical engineering, automation and mechanical engineering. Depending on your interests and strengths, there are exciting opportunities in internal assembly or technical field service. Activities

  • Mechanical and electrical assembly of machinery and equipment
  • Switchgear cabinet construction and electrical wiring according to the diagram
  • Testing and commissioning of machinery and equipment
  • Performance of functional tests and quality controls
  • Analysis and correction of technical failures
  • Maintenance, repair and inspection work
  • Installation and commissioning of equipment at the customer's site
  • Technical support and remote maintenance
  • Cooperation in the optimization and further development of machines and plants

  • Good knowledge of electrical engineering and automation
  • Experience in switchgear cabinet construction or in industrial assembly is advantageous

  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Teamwork and high quality awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Willingness to travel and driving licence Cat. B if interested in the field service
