Full description

MAIN TASKS

As an Electronics Engineer, you expand and optimize the electronic assemblies of Geberit products, always focusing on quality, function, conformity and cost-effectiveness. The range extends from small corrections to complicated, multi-year change projects. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Management and coordination of projects for the implementation of changes and optimizations of electronic assemblies (project management)
  • Further development and revision of electronic circuits (electronic design and layout)
  • Further development of embedded software for our products (embedded firmware)
  • Carry out systematic analyses and troubleshooting to ensure product quality (debugging and error analysis)
  • Responsibility for planning, controlling and optimizing the supply chain (supply chain management)
  • Close cooperation with internal and external partners from development, design, quality management, purchasing and production to achieve joint success

PROFILE

You have a degree in electrical engineering at level BSc/MSc or a comparable degree program and electronics is your great passion. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • Strong interdisciplinary skills, high communication skills and willingness to travel occasionally
  • Independent error analysis (HW and FW) and bug fixing of defective electronic assemblies
  • Knowledge of the design of analog and digital circuits with microcontrollers
  • Programming skills, especially in C and practical experience with RTOS
  • Knowledge of Bluetooth technologies and STM32 processors
  • Comprehensible documentation of your own work
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented working method
  • Interest in continuous further education and personal development
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Stylistic German in word and writing as well as good English skills
