Full description

At Pfister, we have a vision: We want to bring our products into every Swiss home. To achieve this, we are looking for dedicated support. Help us bring the approximately 40'000 products in our range into our customers' homes. Whether at one of the 18 branches or at the head office in Suhr: You can look forward to a unique environment, working with appealing products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your efforts. Around 1'300 people already work at Pfister - will you be joining them soon?

For the start of the apprenticeship in 2027, we are looking for a

Specialization: Styling - 4-year apprenticeship

Your tasks

  • During the 4-year apprenticeship as a 3D polydesigner, you will learn the craft of three-dimensional design from trained professionals
  • You will learn how inspiring and sales-promoting presentations are implemented, from design, through planning and organization, to execution
  • For this purpose, countless accessories, materials and lighting options are available to you, which you will learn about in depth during your apprenticeship

What you bring

  • You have a flair for colors and shapes
  • In addition, you enjoy designing harmonious spaces
  • You enjoy hands-on work and can put your skills to full use when creating and presenting decorative objects
