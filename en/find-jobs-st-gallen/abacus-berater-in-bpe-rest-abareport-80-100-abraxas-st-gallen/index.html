Full description

Are you thrilled when systems talk to each other, data is used intelligently and solutions arise from requirements? Do you like to move at the interface between department and technology and would like to help companies exploit the full potential of Abacus? Then join us, with us you take responsibility for modern integrations and digital processes – as a personality who actively shapes and creates real added value. Your role

  • Design and implementation of integrations via the Abacus REST interfaces
  • Analysis of customer requirements and development of suitable technical solution concepts
  • Creation and further development of reports, evaluations and dashboards with AbaReport
  • Advising customers on the digitalization and automation of their business processes BPE (Business Process Engine)
  • Participation in exciting implementation, optimization and integration projects
  • Testing, documentation and quality assurance of the developed solutions
  • Support our customers as a competent contact person for technical issues

Your profile

  • Experience with Abacus required, especially in BPE, REST and/or AbaReport
  • Understanding data structures, interfaces and system integrations
  • Analytical thinking and the joy of translating complex requirements into practical solutions
  • An independent and structured working method
  • Strength of communication and joy in the exchange with customers and colleagues
  • Very good knowledge of German

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% pension
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
