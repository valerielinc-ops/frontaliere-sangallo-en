Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain. Research engineer in mechatronics for biomedical sensor systems (a) In the research area Digital Health Solutions, we develop technologies for the continuous monitoring of body signals, which are flexibly tailored to different applications – from medical patient monitoring to occupational safety in demanding environments to top-level sports. In the Biomimetic Membranes and Textiles laboratory, an interdisciplinary team is working on innovative, multimodal sensor systems, especially in combination with textile solutions. Our sensor concepts are developed, examined and validated under realistic conditions. In doing so, we analyse measurement accuracy, robustness and long-term stability. In addition to wearing tests, we use standardized measuring setups that enable controlled and reproducible tests. We are also currently developing a platform for the realistic simulation of (patho-)physiological body signals. Their tasks

  • They develop and implement a mechatronic system for simulating various body signals (e.g. ECG; blood pressure, pulse wave, oxygen saturation, respiration), including the integration of hardware and software components.
  • They plan and perform experimental validation and verification measurements.
  • They create technical documentation (e.g. standard operating procedures) and ensure the quality of the developed systems.
  • They support the research team in the evaluation of sensor systems for detecting body signals.
  • They take over the management of sub-projects and coordinate development tasks as well as cooperation with internal and external partners.
  • They participate in the technical development of project proposals as well as in the presentation and further development of system solutions in laboratory operations. Your profile
  • Master’s or PhD degree in Biomedical Engineering, Robotics, or a related discipline with relevant practical experience.
  • In-depth technical knowledge in mechanical and electrical systems as well as in the development and application of control units.
  • Experience in simulation, data acquisition, processing and signal quality analysis.
  • Basic understanding of physiological signals and interest in biomedical applications.
  • Experience in (sub)project management as well as structured, independent working methods.
  • Proactive, solution-oriented personality with quick comprehension and very good knowledge of German and English.
