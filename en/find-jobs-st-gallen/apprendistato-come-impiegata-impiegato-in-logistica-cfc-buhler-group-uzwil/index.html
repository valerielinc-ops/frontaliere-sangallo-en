You like to keep track, get involved and are interested in how goods end up in the right place at the right time? Then the apprenticeship as a logistician: in EFZ at Bühler is exactly the right thing for you!


Apply now with your complete application documents (motivation letter, CV, certificates) and start your future with us! Attractive mobility concept We offer an OSTWIND ZVV / Z-Pass + Half-Tax for 365 CHF + 400CHF public transport credit as well as an attractive first-class subscription or free parking. Staff restaurant VIVA We offer our employees a daily fitness menu from 9.00 CHF. Mobile subscriptions We offer special conditions for mobile subscriptions via Mobilezone at Sunrise and Swisscom. Health port We are committed to health prevention through laboratory analyses, massages, nutritional advice, physiotherapy, group courses and a company doctor. Sports activities We promote sport through self-organized circuit training and running groups as well as vouchers for update and active fitness. Two billion people eat food produced at Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts were produced using Bühler technologies. With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of reconciling the needs of the economy, people and nature. We want to innovate for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions.
