Full description

As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a specialist HRM to strengthen our team.

Their tasks In this versatile function, you are part of our HR team and together with us provide professional HR services along the Employee Lifecycle:

  • They ensure smooth HR administration from entry to exit, create personnel documents and keep personnel and contract data up-to-date.
  • In recruiting, you coordinate the administrative steps from the job advertisement to entry and support the managers if necessary.
  • Around education, training and further education, you will take on a wide range of administrative and coordinative tasks and participate in the supervision of our learning management system easyLearn.
  • For employees and managers, you are a competent contact person for administrative HR issues.
  • With your ideas, you contribute to the optimization and further development of our HR processes.

Your profile

  • A basic commercial training and further training in human resources, for example as an HR assistant or HR specialist, form your professional basis.
  • They bring several years of experience in HR administration, preferably in hospital or healthcare, and know the processes along the employee lifecycle.
  • In dealing with digital work equipment and HR systems, you are well-versed and quickly find your way into new applications.
  • They work independently, structured and carefully and keep track of several tasks.
  • A high level of service orientation and enjoyment of working with different stakeholders round off your profile.

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in your competence
  • Teamwork, because together we achieve more, support each other and cultivate an appreciative cooperation
  • Perspectives, because we promote your professional and personal development
  • Flexibility because we enable contemporary forms of work and home office
  • Variety because you expect a versatile area of responsibility in a dynamic HR environment

Your work area In our 10-member HR team, we accompany employees and managers along the entire employee lifecycle and jointly ensure professional HR services. Our day-to-day work is characterized by a wide range of tasks, different HR systems and close cooperation in the team.

She expects a varied HR environment in which you can contribute your expertise, work independently and actively contribute to the team.
