Underwriting / Product Management Workload: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With work experience FlexOffice: on-site Anyone taking out insurance wants to feel secure. You can assist our customers here – through risk transfer and capital optimization in numerous risk classes. Based on our Swiss values, our vision is to be a strong reinsurance partner throughout the world, both in life and in non-life insurance. Are you on board – and ready to be part of our dynamic, sustainable growth?

What you'll be doing

  • Take ownership of varied administrative tasks relating to business partners, reinsurance contracts and renewal submission data
  • Support our underwriters in nurturing existing partnerships and helping develop new business relationships
  • Prepare insightful figures and analyses that contribute to well-founded decisions on reinsurance contracts
  • Create and maintain meaningful reports that support portfolio management and effective control
  • Communicate professionally with internal stakeholders as well as external clients and brokers
  • Collaborate closely with colleagues across underwriting, actuarial and accounting teams within reinsurance

What you'll bring to the role

  • A higher education background combined with relevant experience in the insurance or reinsurance environment
  • A strong sense of commitment, curiosity and willingness to learn, paired with excellent interpersonal, organizational and communication skills
  • A proactive, independent and well-structured working style
  • Confidence in using Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel
  • Resilience and the ability to stay focused and organized during busy periods, especially renewal season
  • Excellent written and spoken English skills
  • German and any additional languages are a plus
