28.08.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The Risk Data & Reporting Solutions department of the Risk & Compliance department is responsible for the Group-wide risk management database. Our mission is to provide reliable data and meaningful analytics that enable informed decisions and meet regulatory requirements. In order to strengthen the Risk Reporting & Insights team based in St. Gallen, we are looking for a competent personality as a business intelligence specialist (m/f/d) in risk reporting.

What do you expect?

  • Analysis of professional requirements and development of modern reporting and BI solutions
  • Development and optimization of dashboards and visualizations for internal stakeholders
  • Identification of new use cases together with specialist areas and translation of technical questions into innovative data solutions
  • Modeling and preparation of data for reporting, analysis and self-service BI applications as well as carrying out data analyses directly on the data mart if required
  • Continuous development of the reporting landscape and BI platform
  • Advising and supporting users in the use of Power BI or MicroStrategy as well as promoting the expansion of self-service BI
  • Close cooperation with risk experts, data engineers and other stakeholders

What do you bring?

  • Completed university degree (FH/UNI) in business engineering, business informatics, data science or a comparable quantitative discipline
  • Several years of experience in the field of Business Intelligence or Data Analytics
  • Very good knowledge of Power BI, especially in data modeling, visualization and DAX. Experience with MicroStrategy is an advantage
  • Sound SQL knowledge and experience in dealing with complex data structures
  • Strong analytical and communication skills as well as a structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Ideally, experience in the banking, risk or financial environment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

"You are at home in the business intelligence environment and want to create more than just reports?" In the Risk Reporting & Insights group, we develop reporting and analytics solutions that directly contribute to risk monitoring and management. You can expect a versatile field of activity, modern BI technologies and a dedicated team with open and collaborative cooperation.
