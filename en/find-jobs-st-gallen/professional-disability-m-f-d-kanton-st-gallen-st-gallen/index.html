Full description

The Office for Social Affairs monitors and initiates developments in the social sector for the benefit of children and adolescents, families, people with social problems, elderly people, people with a migrant background and people with disabilities, as well as organisations and institutions working for these target groups. The Department of Age and Disability is responsible for shaping the age policy involving the municipalities and other central actors in the Canton of St.Gallen. We ensure the monitoring of offers and promote integrated approaches. In addition, we are responsible for needs-based housing, employment and workshop offers, their approval and supervision as well as financing for people with disabilities. In addition, we are the interface for disability policy measures and work in a cross-sectional function for accessibility and participation for people with disabilities.

What to expect

  • Together with other specialists, you will take on central tasks in the area of performance and contribution management of facilities for people with disabilities (examination and coordination of applications for recognition, preparation of individual performance agreements and monitoring of performance performance).
  • You create operating permits and together with other team members take over the supervision of stationary residential offers and daily structures
  • You conduct recognition procedures of ambulatory care providers and ensure effective controlling
  • Together with other team members, you will design different change projects in the area of disability policy

What distinguishes you

  • University or university of applied sciences degree, preferably in the areas of business administration, business administration, health care management or a similar discipline
  • Proven experience in project and change management
  • Good negotiating and consulting skills
  • Knowledge and practical experience of the disability supply landscape (e.g. at facility level)
  • Very good oral and written expression skills
  • Very good MS-Office user knowledge
  • High service orientation and good organisational skills

What we offer

  • Varied activities in a motivating and collegial environment
  • Work models with flexible working hours
  • Excellent opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Attractive workplace in the center of the city of St.Gallen
