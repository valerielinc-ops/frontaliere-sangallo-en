For our customers, an established company in the field of industrial automation and control solutions, we are looking for an experienced specialist in electrical planning. In this function, you are responsible for the creation and further development of electrical engineering planning documents for demanding systems.

02 Your task

  • Creation and processing of electrical engineering plans and schemes for automated plants

  • Further development and maintenance of CAD templates and planning standards
  • Detailed planning of systems and their components in the industrial environment
  • Close cooperation with project management, software engineering and other specialist departments
  • Cooperation with commissioning and technical adaptations in the course of the project

  • Understanding of electrical systems and systems in the industrial environment
