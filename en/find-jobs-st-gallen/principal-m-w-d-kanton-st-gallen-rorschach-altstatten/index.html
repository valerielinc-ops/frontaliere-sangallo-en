The Rorschach-Rheintal BZR vocational and further education centre is an important commercial and industrial cantonal centre of competence in eastern Switzerland. At the Rorschach and Altstätten sites, around 1,900 students from different cantons and nations are accompanied in their basic vocational education. The BZR combines sound vocational training, contemporary learning forms, high practical proximity and close cooperation with teaching companies, professional associations and other educational partners. The professional further education (WZR) completes our educational offer.

In a variety of courses and courses, around 2,000 professionals expand their qualifications annually.
