Full description

The Office for Economic Affairs and Labour provides a wide range of services for companies, employees and job seekers. "Quick & Smart", "Ready to go" and "Education & Coaching" are offers of the logistics labor market measures. They promote the employability of job seekers and support integration into the labour market.

What to expect

  • Planning and conducting individual coaching sessions to provide targeted support to job seekers
  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of courses for job seekers
  • Ensuring the quality of offers and active cooperation with interface partners
  • Collaboration in projects and further development of offers

What sets it apart

  • Completed coaching training and/or trainer with federal law. Specialist card
  • Professional experience in coaching, adult education or work integration
  • Solution-oriented working method, high personal responsibility and assertiveness
  • Winning, motivating and enthusiastic personality with a safe appearance
  • Practical handling of MS 365 applications

What we offer

  • Motivated, innovative team with an open and supportive working culture
  • Diverse opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Modern employment conditions and attractive working time models
