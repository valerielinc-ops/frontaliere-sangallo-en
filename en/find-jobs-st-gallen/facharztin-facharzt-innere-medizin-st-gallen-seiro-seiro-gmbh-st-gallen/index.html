Full description

Holistic medicine at the interface of body and psyche: you take care of the somatic health of psychiatric patients. In St. Gallen, we are looking for a specialist for general internal medicine in the amount of 20 – 50% by appointment. In a recovery-oriented clinic with university connections, you play an important role in the interdisciplinary team at the interface between somatic and psychiatric care. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take over the internistic and general medical care of the inpatients
  • They carry out medical examinations, provide indications and diagnoses and initiate appropriate treatments
  • They assess and treat somatic concomitant diseases in a psychiatric context and optimize somatic medication
  • They work closely with psychiatrists, psychologists and the nursing and therapist team
  • They assist in the interpretation of ECG and laboratory findings
  • They carefully document and actively participate in interdisciplinary case meetings

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for general internal medicine or equivalent foreign specialist title with federal recognition
  • Interest in working in the psychiatric environment and enjoyment of a holistic treatment approach
  • Independent, responsible working methods and enjoyment of working in an interprofessional team
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • High social competence and appreciative treatment of patients
  • Overview and sound medical decisions even in demanding situations
  • Joy of interdisciplinary collaboration at the interface of somatics and psychiatry

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Generous financial participation in training and working time for internal and external training
  • Attractive internal training offer with supervision and individual career planning
  • Flexible working models with flexible time, part-time, job sharing and top-sharing as well as benefits in childcare
  • Food, eco-bonus, company parking and staff rooms
  • Benefit, loyalty and placement rewards and well-being and health services such as yoga, gym, flu and tick vaccination

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
