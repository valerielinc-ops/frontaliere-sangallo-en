Full description

Pharmaceutical assistant or drugist EFZ, Clinical Pharmacy 40-50 %

Hospital pharmacy

Place of work St. Gallen and Wil

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • You will process pharmaceutical and logistical inquiries about medicines at the Geriatric Clinic St. Gallen
  • You manage the station pharmacies in the hospital Wil and in the geriatric clinic, from ordering to storing the medicines
  • You support the clinical pharmacy team with audits of station pharmacies and document management
  • You work on two to three days of the week, with Wednesday being a fixed working day

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a pharmaceutical assistant, specialist pharmacy EFZ, or drugist EFZ
  • Self-employed, exact and responsible work with a high level of service orientation
  • In-depth knowledge of pharmaceuticals, experience in goods management and flexibility for use in other locations
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer to you

  • Interesting and versatile work in hospital pharmacy in a collegial, motivated team
  • Cooperation in a safe drug therapy in an interdisciplinary environment between clinic and hospital pharmacy
  • Attractive training opportunities
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
