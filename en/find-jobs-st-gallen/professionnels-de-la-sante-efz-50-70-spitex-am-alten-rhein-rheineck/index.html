Full description

Spitex Am Alten Rhein currently employs 22 people in the field of care at home. Our services are available to all residents of the political communities of Lutzenberg, Rheineck, St. Margrethen and Thal who are dependent on support due to illness, accident, disability, disability, maternity or the like. We are looking for an immediately or by agreement Health professional EFZ 50-70 % Her education in the field of health EFZ forms the basis for missions in the outpatient area of Spitex. What is part of the remit?

  • An individual, person-centered care and support of our clients according to care planning
  • Cooperation with the case-leading nurses HF/FH and the team management
  • Professional expertise in current medical technology (injections, dressing therapy)
  • Understanding of chronically ill people and empathy for care situations in the home, private environment
  • Cooperation with carers
  • The electronic documentation of processes, directly on site on the company's mobile phone
  • Independent assumption of day trips with the company car, along the previously created planning by the team management

We offer

  • A responsible job in an appreciative environment and the opportunity to contribute your valuable knowledge and specific experience
  • Support by a competent and independent team, competent experts and committed team leaders
  • Modern infrastructure, proximity to public transport, free parking

For further information, the Managing Director, Rico Fach, is at your disposal on Tel 071 888 25 77. Please send your complete application documents to info@spitexaar.ch .

Portrait Spitex Am Alten Rhein serves the 4 municipalities of Rheineck, St. Margrethen, Thal and Lutzenberg according to the public service mandate in the canton of St. Gallen.
