Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Seed analysis, seed preparation and cryopreservation
  • Performing IVF and ICSI treatments
  • Cultivation of embryos and cryopreservation of gametes and embryos
  • Assistance in follicular punctures and embryo transfers
  • Cooperation in laboratory transport as well as responsibility for equipment management and ordering

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Clinical Embryologist (MSc Embryology), Bachelor in Biology, Dipl. Biomedical analyst HF or equivalent qualification
  • Ideally practical experience in andrology and/or in the IVF laboratory
  • Flexible, reliable and team-oriented personality with a precise, independent and responsible way of working
  • Joy in accompanying patients empathetically on their way to the desired child, as well as readiness for weekend services
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing and good knowledge of English

Our offer to you

  • A varied and meaningful activity with personal responsibility in a committed team
  • Carefully planned and individually accompanied onboarding for an optimal start
  • An open, appreciative team culture with respectful cooperation at eye level
  • Diverse opportunities for professional and personal development through a broad range of further education and training
  • Attractive employee benefits around health, work and family – including various perks
