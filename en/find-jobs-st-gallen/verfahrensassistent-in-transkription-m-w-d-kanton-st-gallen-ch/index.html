Full description

The public prosecutor's office with around 250 employees consists of four regional investigative offices, a cantonal investigative office responsible for the entire cantonal territory, the staff services and the youth prosecutor's office. The Public Prosecutor's Office is an independent investigative and prosecuting authority of the Canton of St.Gallen, which is responsible for the equal enforcement of the state criminal claim and conducts criminal proceedings in the event of crimes, offences and transgressions.
