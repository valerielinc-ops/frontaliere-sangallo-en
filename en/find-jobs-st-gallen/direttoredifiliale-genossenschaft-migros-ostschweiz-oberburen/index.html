Full description

Do you enjoy retailing and are looking forward to successfully running a branch? With passion for the job 🧠 you motivate your team, create a shopping experience that excites 🛒✨, and keep the numbers under control 📊. Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, M Oberbüren, Sandackerstrasse 4, 9245 Oberbüren

Mansioni

  • You are the head of our branch. With your leadership style, you motivate your team and take on the professional and personnel responsibility so that everything runs smoothly.
  • You turn customers into fans 🛒✨. Through your passion for sales and consulting, you provide a shopping experience that inspires – and implements targeted sales promotion measures.
  • You have the numbers under control 📊.Whether sales, costs, inventory or warehouse management – you keep the overview and control the branch successfully.
  • You are the linchpin 🔄. From ordering to incoming goods inspections to presentation and dispatch – you make sure that everything is perfectly organized.

Requirements

  • Many years of professional experience: in the retail trade
  • Eidg. Professional ID: as a retail specialist
  • You already have leadership experience in the food sector and know exactly how to motivate and lead your team successfully.
  • Your personality makes the difference. With your convincing appearance, your situationally adapted behavior and your high level of commitment, you are a role model for your team.
  • You are a professional in dealing with customers 🛍️. Friendly, expert and customer-centered, you manage to make every customer happy.
  • You think connected and act entrepreneurial 🔗. Your quick comprehension, your analytical abilities and your physical resilience help you to master even challenging situations.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
