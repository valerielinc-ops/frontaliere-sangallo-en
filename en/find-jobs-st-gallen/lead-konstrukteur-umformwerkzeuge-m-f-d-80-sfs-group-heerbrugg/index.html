Full description

Combining technology, precision and aerospace: As a lead designer, you will be responsible for challenging design and development tasks related to the cold mass forming of Aerospace Fasteners. With your know-how in forming technology and tool manufacturing, you design processes, develop innovative tool solutions and accompanying projects from design to series production. Your Tasks:

  • Responsibility for the design and calculation of stage sequences for cold mass forming
  • Development and construction of forming tools for multi-stage presses with CAD
  • Preparation and assurance of complete and correct production documents
  • Support and coordination of tool and process implementation on the forming machine
  • Implementation of process reviews after samples and test runs as well as monitoring of series start-up
  • Cooperation and responsibility in process optimization Your profile:
  • Completed training as a constructor with corresponding professional experience, further training as a technician HF or study FH advantageous
  • Several years of experience in forming technology as well as in-depth knowledge in toolmaking, ideally in cold mass forming and multi-stage pressing technology
  • Excellent CAD knowledge and MS Office knowledge as well as experience with PLM/PDM and FEM tools
  • Flowing knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Technical understanding, solution-oriented, precise and conscientious working method
  • Strong team and communication skills We offer:
  • Varied tasks
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual participation in success
  • Own healthy pension fund with individual savings plans
  • Internal training courses & support for external training
  • Other benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
