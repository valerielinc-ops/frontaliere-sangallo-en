Senior physician Gynecology and Obstetrics 80-100 %

Clinic for Gynecology and Obstetrics

Place of work Uznach

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Care of the birth room and the ward together with the assistant doctors
  • Conducting a general gynecological and obstetric consultation
  • Operational activity by level of training, with the possibility of further development
  • Participation in the education and training of assistant doctors
  • Ensuring a coorperative, interdisciplinary cooperation

What you bring for this job

  • Completed specialist training in gynecology and obstetrics or shortly before graduation
  • Interest in obstetric ultrasound
  • Flexible, capable of teamwork, independent, committed and responsible
  • Enjoyment of the further education of assistant doctors, students and nurses
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. C1)

Our offer to you

  • Possibility to achieve the focus Operational Gynecology and further development to / to the senior physician mbF
  • Collaboration in a motivated and collegial team
  • Varied challenges in a dynamic working environment
  • Responsible activity, a versatile area of responsibility and design possibilities to contribute your ideas
