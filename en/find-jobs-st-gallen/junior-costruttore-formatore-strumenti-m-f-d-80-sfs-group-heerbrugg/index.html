Full description

Do you want to expand your career path in a dynamic engineering environment and take on responsibility in tool design? As a junior designer of forming tools, you actively shape innovative processes in cold mass forming and successfully implement precise solutions. Your Tasks:

  • Design and modification of forming tools using modern CAD system (MSTK, MSTG)
  • Design of the sequence of stages according to specified specifications
  • Cooperation in process development as well as in the contract review for the field of cold forming
  • Creation and responsibility of precise production documents for tools
  • Support and optimization over the entire product lifetime Your profile:
  • Completed technical training and ideally first professional experience as a constructor
  • Knowledge in the application of CAD programs as well as MS-Office. PLM/PDM knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience in general toolmaking as well as in forming technology and multi-stage pressing technology is desirable
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Solution-oriented working method with high quality and responsibility We offer:

  • Staff restaurant with attractive prices
