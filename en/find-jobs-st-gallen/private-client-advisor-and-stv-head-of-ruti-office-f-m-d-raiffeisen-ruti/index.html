Full description

For <b>Raiffeisenbank Rapperswil-Jona</b>, a successful, innovative and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona SG and offices in Rüti ZH, Hombrechtikon ZH and Egg ZH, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented and independent personality as a private client advisor and Stv for our modern advisor bank in Rüti ZH. Head of office Rüti ZH (f/m/d).

Tasks

  • Development and management of the own private customer portfolio
  • Deputy Head of Rüti Office
  • Ensuring competent advice on financing issues (housing) as well as standardised advice on investing and provisioning
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and life situations of the customers as well as within the segment-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

Requirements

  • Banking and/or financial education completed
  • Experience in the financial services sector in particular in mortgage consulting
  • Strong customer and sales-oriented thinking
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as enjoying intensive customer contact
  • Teamwork and flexibility

We offer

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.
