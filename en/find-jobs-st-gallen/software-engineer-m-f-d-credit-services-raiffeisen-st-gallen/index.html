Full description

03.11.2026 St. Gallen Zurich

Become part of our team!

The Credit Services team develops, tests and operates services and applications throughout the credit process. As part of the Solution Delivery Living & Financing department in the area of Development Client Solutions, we work on business-critical applications and modern service-oriented architectures for Raiffeisen Switzerland. In order to strengthen our team at the St. Gallen and/or Zurich Circle sites, we are looking for a committed, reliable and motivated personality as a software engineer (m/f/d).

What do you expect?

  • Responsibility for the further development, maintenance and reliable operation of our credit solutions and the associated microservices based on Java & Spring Boot.
  • Analysis and implementation of professional and technical requirements with a focus on robust, scalable and maintainable software solutions in close cooperation with product owners, business analysts, developers and architects.
  • Ensure high quality standards through code reviews, automated testing and the exchange of best practices within the team.
  • Gradually assume responsibility for further services based on Kotlin, Spring Boot and Kafka streams as well as their continuous development.

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in the development of backend applications with Java and Spring Boot.
  • In-depth knowledge in the design and implementation of microservice architectures, REST APIs and ideally event-based architectures (e.g. Kafka).
  • Experience with automated tests, code reviews and the development of high-quality, maintainable software.
  • Secure use of CI/CD pipelines, OpenShift and container technologies to efficiently deploy and operate applications.
  • Team-oriented, responsible personality with a high level of initiative, enjoyment of knowledge exchange and very good knowledge of German.
