Full description

Tasks

As a room service employee you ensure a smooth process in the room service, this also includes

  • Acceptance of orders on the phone and simple advice for guests
  • Service of food and drinks in the hotel room
  • Preparation of Mise en place for the daily process
  • Preparation of simple drinks for the service
  • Disclosure of guest feedback and assistance with concerns
  • Compliance with cleanliness, standards and daily tasks according to specifications

Requirements

You are interested in working in the service and are happy to be in contact with guests. In addition, you bring with you

  • First experience in the service or hotel industry is an advantage
  • Willingness to perform simple service tasks reliably
  • Careful and structured working methods
  • Teamwork and a friendly way of dealing with guests
  • Good knowledge of German or English

We offer

  • Tasting evenings and perks at Thermal Spa
  • Free Tickets Tamina Gorge
  • Test sleep after passing the test period
  • Up to 50% discount in restaurants
  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origins in the healing thermal water of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place stands for regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis, we continue to develop the resort consistently – with the aim of becoming the leading Wellbeing & Healthy Living Resort in Europe and creating a place of strength for generations.

Around 800 employees shape the experience of our guests every day. Different talents, cultures and personalities work together on one goal: to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common aspiration to make this special place a bit better every day – One Resort – One Team.
