Full description

Do you have a feeling for excellent service and are enthusiastic about offering our customers a positive shopping experience at the checkout and customer service? You are sales-strong 💪 and like to communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Mansioni

  • With customer service and cashing at the Scanningkasse 💳 you are always attentive and reliable. With your smile, you make every purchase a positive experience! ✨
  • You make sure that everything in the checkout area is filled and tidy and take care of the order and cleanliness ✨🧹
  • You support our customers with Subito Self-Scanning. With your help, shopping becomes even easier.
  • You take on administrative tasks 📋 with care, are the friendly voice at our telephone exchange 📞 and support our customers competent customer service 💶

Requirements

  • Professional experience is advantageous: preferably in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: preferably in the retail trade
  • You already have experience working with a scanning cash register 🛒📲
  • You convince with your well-kept appearance, your reliability and your flexibility. Loyalty is a matter of course for you.
  • With your exact working method and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • You bring good PC skills 💻🖱️ and with digital tools and systems you are safe.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
