Full description

Age psychiatry with perspective: inpatient responsibility, outpatient leadership option and building a focus with Memory Clinic. In the St. Gallen area, we are looking for a senior physician with a special function or a senior physician with a special function for age and neuropsychiatry in the amount of 50 – 100%. In a clinic with several locations, you can expect an interdisciplinary team with room for manoeuvre between inpatient and outpatient care. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Your focus is on stationary care, where you take over the professional management of the interdisciplinary team in partnership dual management with the nursing station management.
  • You will lead the focus age and neuropsychiatry with outpatient area, day clinic and memory clinic
  • They independently and on a case-by-case basis carry out treatments of outpatient patients in individual, group and family settings, including documentation, correspondence and performance recording.
  • They provide therapeutic and medical care together with assistant doctors and psychologists
  • They promote networking with surrounding retirement and nursing homes and take over visits as well as consiliary and supervisory inquiries
  • You participate in the clinic-wide background service, with the option to work either in different locations or exclusively in one location

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or shortly before completion of this training, with interest in the focus on old-age psychiatry, the acquisition of which is gladly supported
  • Experience in the treatment of psychiatric patients or in geriatrics or neurology
  • Professional Permit of the Canton of St. Gallen or the prerequisites for obtaining it before taking up the job
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, at least level C1

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centred, trusting attitude with recovery-oriented thinking
  • Enjoying responsibility, independent decision-making and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • Engagement in a multi-professional team

D as offers you your future employer

  • Senior medical position with room for manoeuvre between inpatient and outpatient care and management of its own centre of gravity
  • Support for the acquisition of the focus title Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • Academic teaching hospital of the University of Zurich with high competence in education, training and further education
  • Interdisciplinary, appreciative working environment with Memory Clinic and Day Clinic
  • Workplace in a scenic region between Lake Constance and Lake Zurich with a high quality of life

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
