Full description

Platforms at Scale. Hilti’s Future Powered.

As a Head of Architecture, Security & Quality, you will set and drive the enterprise-wide architectural direction for our digital platforms, translating business strategy into a coherent, secure, scalable, and future-ready IT landscape while leading the Architecture, Security & Quality function within our Subscription Business and Development organization. You will translate business and technology strategy into clear architectural principles, standards, and target architectures, providing strategic guidance for complex decisions across products and platforms. You will ensure secure, compliant, and high-quality development and operations while driving technology enablement and shaping the organization’s readiness for emerging capabilities, including agentic AI.

What You’ll Bring

To help you succeed, we’ll provide a strong global network, committed teams and space to shape the future. Here’s what you will need to bring:

  • Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related discipline, combined with 10+ years of progressive experience in IT, enterprise architecture, and technology leadership.
  • Proven track record of leading complex architecture initiatives across cloud, integration, and enterprise platforms, supported by strong expertise in cybersecurity, resilience, scalability, and modern software engineering practices.
  • Demonstrated ability to define and evolve target architectures, technology standards, and strategic roadmaps, translating business and technology priorities into well-informed architectural decisions and pragmatic implementation paths.
  • Proven experience leading and developing international teams or architecture communities, with the ability to build capabilities, foster collaboration, and influence outcomes across organizational boundaries without relying on formal authority.
  • Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills in English, with the ability to translate complex technology topics into clear business implications and influence senior business leaders, IT executives, and strategic external partners.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
