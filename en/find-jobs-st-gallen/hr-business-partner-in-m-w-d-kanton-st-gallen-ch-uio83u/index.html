Full description

The Department of Economic Affairs keeps pace with the economy and currently employs 650 people. They work in the General Secretariat, Office for Public Transport, Cantonal Forestry Office, Office for Agriculture, Office for Nature, Hunting and Fisheries, Office for Economic Affairs and Labour, and Office for Data and Statistics. The General Secretariat provides central services for the entire Department of Economic Affairs, including in the areas of law, HR management, finance and IT.

  • Cooperation with the Central Personnel Office (HR Services and Support, HR Competence Center, HR IT)

What sets it apart

What we offer

  • Different working time models
