Full description

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. To strengthen the Regionalcenter East in Wil SG, we are looking for a service manager in the field of gas.

As an innovative person, you are responsible for the dynamic handling of service processes and contribute ideas for the further development of our service processes. Together with your broad-based service team, you will contribute to achieving the goals of the East region and the company as a whole.

Your tasks – here you take responsibility

  • Operational responsibility for the intensive service area in the East Region
  • Professional and personnel management of a team of specialists in the service and repair environment comprising 10 - 15 people
  • Regular contact with private and large customers, partial accompaniment of experts in daily use, sporadic front-line use in the event of bottlenecks and emergencies in disposition
  • Structured planning of resources and tasks, implementation of internal meetings, coordination of all activities related to the specific company key figures (quality, quantity, addition/replacement sales, etc.)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with other units (sales, logistics, technology, etc.) as well as close exchange with the aftersales management
  • Deputy of other leaders at the same level

We offer you – an environment that strengthens you

  • An exciting challenge in a successful, sustainably growing company
  • Collegial cooperation in a family environment shaped by a du-culture
  • Performance-oriented company with a lot of design possibilities
  • Business cars for private use
  • Multi-week implementation programme

Your profile – that makes you our ideal match

  • In-depth technical training in the field of building technology/HLKE, further training both technical and business management
  • Several years of experience in dealing with direct customer contact in the aftersales environment of a company with technical products
  • Proof of performance with leadership experience in the area of customer care, technical service, disposition, customer service or comparable
  • German as a mother tongue
  • Mastery of the most common IT applications (Windows, Excel, Word, SAP)

Are you our new, highly motivated team leader and want to become part of the Hoval culture? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
