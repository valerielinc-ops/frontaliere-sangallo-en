Full description

The teaching supervision department of the Office for Vocational Education and Training is responsible for the operational part of the basic vocational qualification. She works closely with the training companies. If you want to help shape and promote vocational training, you are in the right place with us!

What to expect

As a training consultant, you supervise all teaching conditions in a group of professions. See also:

  • Supervise teaching and training
  • Advise the Contracting Parties on all matters relating to the teaching contract and the training
  • mediate in case of inconsistencies in the teaching relationship
  • examine new training places
  • work in OC and technical commissions
  • contribute to the training, advice and support of vocational trainers

What sets it apart

In this versatile and demanding area of responsibility, you are as open to young people as you are to the concerns of training companies. A solid basic vocational training, continuous further education, several years of professional experience as a vocational trainer resp. Vocational trainee, a broad general education, very good skills in written and oral expression as well as good knowledge in dealing with computer tools. In addition to negotiating skills in dealing with learners, teaching companies, associations and authorities, you also have an interest in basic vocational training and enjoyment in dealing with young people.

What we offer

  • varied and exciting tasks in a motivated team
  • Personal scope for design
  • Flexible working hours
  • Attractive development and training opportunities
  • Participation in Ostwind company subscription
