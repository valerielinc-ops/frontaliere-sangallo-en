Employee/patient transport 100%

Department of Real Estate & Operation, Supply Chain Management

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Accompanying and transporting patients within the Cantonal Hospital St. Gallen as well as within the Geriatric Clinic, the Clinic Valens and Wohnen am Singenberg
  • Transport execution with the electromobile in the underground canal system

What you bring for this job

  • High willingness to work in shift work (3 shift operation) and on weekends
  • Health and physically resilient
  • Good knowledge of German level B1 (in word and writing)
