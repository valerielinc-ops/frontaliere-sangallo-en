Full description

The legal service of the Building and Environmental Department, which is organised in the Office for the Environment, supports the Office for the Environment, the Office for Water and Energy, the Civil Engineering Office and the Office for Spatial Development and Geoinformation in the legally compliant enforcement. The legal service deals with the wide range of areas of law that fall within the scope of the said offices.

What to expect

  • Coordination of water engineering planning procedures (flood protection, revitalization) in municipal and cantonal waters, including language processing in cantonal waters
  • Coordination of special use permits for hydraulic engineering (construction of buildings and installations in the area of public waters)
  • Water detections
  • Preliminary checks and approvals of municipal and partial road plans
  • Legal support for projects related to road legislation
  • In the areas of hydraulic engineering, natural hazards and road law:
  • Advising municipalities and private individuals
  • Participation in appeal proceedings
  • Advising the Water and Energy Office and the Civil Engineering Office
  • Participation in consultations on bills by the Confederation and the Canton
  • Participation in the treatment of parliamentary initiatives and participation in projects

What sets it apart

  • Graduate degree as lic.iur. or MLaw
  • In-depth knowledge of state and administrative law
  • Enjoying complex issues and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Initiative Personality with a secure appearance and editorial skill
  • Careful, purposeful and independent working method
  • Teamwork and social competence

What we offer

  • Versatile and legally demanding work with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Collegial working atmosphere in a supportive team
  • Flexible working time models and individual training offers
