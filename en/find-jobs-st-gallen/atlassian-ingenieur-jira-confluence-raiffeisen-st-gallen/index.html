29.09.2026 St. Gallen Zurich Atlassian Engineer (Jira & Confluence) 80-100 %

Become part of our team!

The Quality Assurance and Test Coordination team is also responsible for the operation, further development and optimization of Raiffeisen Switzerland’s Atlassian cloud platform. In order to strengthen our team at the St. Gallen and Zurich Airport (The Circle) locations and with the possibility of a home office, we are looking for an analytical personality with technical know-how and process understanding as Atlassian Engineer (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Operation and development of Jira and Confluence in our Atlassian cloud platform
  • Configuration, optimization and further development of workflows, permissions, screens and schemes
  • Implementation of automations, integrations and standardized Jira solutions
  • Analysis and implementation of business and IT processes, advising our departments and supporting projects
  • Ensuring governance, compliance and quality requirements

What do you bring?

  • Higher education in computer science (FH, HF or comparable)
  • Several years of experience as a software, system or application engineer
  • In-depth knowledge of Jira and Confluence administration and configuration
  • Experience in process analysis, requirements management and stakeholder management

You have technical Atlassian know-how and understand the integrated processes and would like to further develop our platform? Then strengthen our team and inspire us with your commitment, your creativity and your team spirit! Philipp Weibel Group Leader Quality Assurance & Test Coordination Why Raiffeisen?
