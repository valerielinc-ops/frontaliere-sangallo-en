Full description

As a senior physician or senior physician for psychiatry and psychotherapy, you shape the professional orientation of age and neuropsychiatry and set up an age psychiatric day clinic. In St. Gallen we are looking for you immediately or by agreement in the bustle by agreement. They steer the psychiatric services through outpatient, daily clinical and consiliary settings and bear the cadre responsibility for patients aged 60 and over. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • As medical director, you control the professional orientation and further development of aging and neuropsychiatry via outpatient, daily clinical and consiliary settings
  • They lead the senior physicians, assistant physicians and psychologists professionally and personnel and are responsible for their further education and supervision
  • Together with the interprofessional team, you ensure the medical and therapeutic care of patients aged 60 and over.
  • You will take over the development of the age psychiatric day clinic conceptually, organizationally and professionally and develop a cross-positional treatment path
  • They actively network with homes, Spitex and other hospital partners and promote a treatment culture based on the principles of recovery and shared decision making.

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or equivalent foreign specialist title
  • Ideally, the main topic is age and neuropsychiatry or an already started training course.
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in a psychiatric institution offering outpatient treatment
  • Enjoyment in the further education of psychological employees and a strong willingness for interprofessional cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least C1)

That sets them apart.

  • Integrative, solution-oriented personality with entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Efficient, forward-looking working methods
  • High social skills, teamwork and personal commitment

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Senior medical management function with room for manoeuvre in setting up an age psychiatric day clinic
  • Opportunity to redevelop a cross-positional treatment pathway
  • Interdisciplinary working environment at a psychiatric company with around 1,400 employees and nine locations
  • Structured introduction plan and onboarding in the new team
  • Supply contract for the cantons of St. Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as the Principality of Liechtenstein

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
