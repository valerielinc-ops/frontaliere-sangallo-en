Full description

«We are the first bank for our customers». For us, the first bank means constantly inspiring our customers by focusing on their needs. We need you for that! Do you want to run our office in Altstätten as our new colleague? Then come to us!

This is what you will do.

  • You are responsible for the management and results for the locally strongly rooted Altstätten branch and the subordinate branch in Oberriet. You implement the business strategy in the region through goal-oriented market development activities.
  • You have overall responsibility for 28 employees and directly lead the business customer team in Altstätten with 5 employees.
  • You manage your own business customer book in the areas of financing, investing and pensions, offer your customers first-class advice and attentive support.
  • The expansion of existing and the acquisition of new customer relationships are also part of your tasks.
  • You actively participate in the economic and social life in the market area (public events, events, etc.) and represent the bank in public.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher banking or business education.
  • You have many years of leadership experience and thanks to your high social skills, you enjoy leading a powerful team.
  • You have several years of experience in advising customers, especially from the business customer segment, as well as in-depth knowledge in the financing, investment and pension business.
  • You enjoy taking on and shaping responsibility, are strong in communication, think entrepreneurially, act independently and distinguish yourself through your negotiating skills as well as your sales and closing strength.
  • You have good knowledge of the regional and local market conditions and are accordingly well networked.

Information Raphael Wenk, Head of Private and Business Customers East Region, telephone 071 231 35 40, will be happy to answer your questions.
