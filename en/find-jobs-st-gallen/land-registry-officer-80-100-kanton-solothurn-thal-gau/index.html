Full description

The Land Registry of the Thal-Gäu Registry carries out tasks in the preparation and implementation of public deeds relating to legal transactions over land and land accounting. For the Land Registry Thal-Gäu, we are looking for a case handler, 80-100%, at the Balsthal site as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They are responsible for preparing public documents on legal transactions in immovable property such as purchase, easement and pledge contracts as well as for examining external land register applications and enter them in the land register.
  • All administrative activities in connection with the creation of public documents as well as the examination of external land register applications are carried out by you.
  • They advise our customers at the counter, by telephone and electronically and provide information.

Your profile

  • They have a commercial apprenticeship or equivalent education. Ideally, you will have several years of professional experience and have knowledge of property, land registry and bond law.
  • Exact and independent work, a forward-looking and structured working method as well as stylish German in word and writing are a matter of course for you.
  • They work customer- and service-oriented and have very good IT knowledge. Your strong social skills and communication strength distinguishes you.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
