Full description

The Office for Spatial Development and Geoinformation (AREG) is responsible for spatial development policy, official surveying and the provision of geoinformation. The Building outside Construction Zones department coordinates and assesses construction projects outside the construction zone in the area of spatial planning, construction and planning law and is active in advising building authorities and building owners as well as their planners.

What to expect

  • Application and deepening of legal knowledge in spatial planning, construction and planning law as well as in other areas of public law
  • Processing of construction projects outside the construction zone
  • Advising the building owners, planners and building authorities
  • Conduct of inspections and meetings
  • Preparation of decisions

What sets it apart

  • Completed legal studies
  • Enjoyment of building, planning and spatial planning law as well as other areas of public law
  • Motivated and interested personality
  • Precise and reliable operation

What we offer

  • Administrative internship as preparation for the bar examination
  • Collegial and motivated team
  • Attractive workplace with modern infrastructure
