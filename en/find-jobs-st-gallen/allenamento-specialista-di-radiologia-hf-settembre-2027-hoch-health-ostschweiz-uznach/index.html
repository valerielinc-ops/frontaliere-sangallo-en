Training centres

Place of work Uznach

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Responsibility in the fields of Diagnostic Radiology, Radio-Oncology and Nuclear Medicine
  • Presentation of various medical images by means of X-rays, magnetic fields, ultrasonic waves and radioactive substances, verification of organ functions
  • Close contact with patients
  • Performance of therapeutic treatments
  • Working independently and independently to ensure optimal care for patients
  • Numerous further education offers due to the rapid technical development in radiology

What you bring for this job

  • 3-year basic training with a federal certificate of competence or a specialised secondary school diploma or graduate school
  • Interest in science and technology
  • Advanced knowledge in mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology
  • Basic knowledge of English and computer science
  • Empathy and understanding for patients

Our offer to you

  • The training enables applications in various fields of radiology
  • Working is possible with state-of-the-art radiological equipment
  • Insight into the full spectrum of radiology (including neuroradiology), radiation oncology and nuclear medicine
  • Training takes place in an advanced environment with a well-trained, qualified and experienced team
  • Learning is supported by the latest learning infrastructure

Your application

  • Admission as of 17.09.2027. You can find out more about this exciting training on our website or from Elina Boos, HR Education specialist, telephone +41 71 494 27 36
  • Take your chance and complete your education at HOCH Health Eastern Switzerland
