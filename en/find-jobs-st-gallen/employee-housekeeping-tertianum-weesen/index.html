Do you care deeply about older people? Is working in housekeeping your passion? Do you see opportunities to use your skills and experience with us in a meaningful way and have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

For Wismetpark in the St. Gallen / Linth region, we are looking for someone from 23 November 2026 or by agreement

Housekeeping employee 40%

Your responsibilities

  • Responsible for the proper cleaning of all assigned rooms and facilities
  • Ensuring the supply of textiles in the areas of workwear and professional clothing (ordering, coordinating and distributing) as well as guest laundry
  • Distributing and labeling guest laundry
  • Performing housekeeping tasks as instructed
  • Proper handling of materials and equipment in accordance with regulations
  • Compliance with hygiene regulations and personal hygiene standards in accordance with the company's instructions

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in housekeeping (care home, hospital or hotel)
  • Strong service orientation and commitment, with an eye for detail
  • Reliability, a sense of responsibility and physical stamina
  • Enjoyment of working with older people
  • Willingness to work on weekends and public holidays and, when needed, support other departments
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of varied tasks
  • German language skills, both spoken and written

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
