Full description

Together for a good life in old age Under the umbrella of RaJoVita, the Health and Old Age Foundation of the city of Rapperswil-Jona, we create living spaces where older people feel safe, secure and at home. In our two care centers Meienberg and Bühl, in the retirement and nursing home Bürgerspital as well as in the nursing apartment Porthof, we personally and heartily support 175 people in need of care. The day care center Grünfels and Spitex complement our versatile offer and provide comprehensive support – both stationary and outpatient. Around 355 employees work every day to offer our residents and our clients individual, needs-based and loving care. For us, the focus is always on people, their life story and their personal needs. Administration staff in Spitex, 60-80 % Your tasks

  • You are our first contact person on the phone and at the reception
  • You process telephone requests or forward them
  • You ensure the flow of information between customers, caregivers and team leaders
  • You process new registrations and collect master data
  • You support in operational planning

Your profile

  • You have completed a commercial education (KV), an education as a medical assistant (MPA) or specialist health (FaGe) with a pronounced IT affinity and you enjoy the telephone exchange with various professional groups
  • You think service-oriented, entrepreneurial and are a team player
  • You have a natural talent for networked thinking
  • You keep the peace and the overview even in hectic moments
  • You are flexible from Monday to Friday, during normal office hours

Our offer

  • An innovative working environment that meets you with appreciation and respect
  • A varied activity and opportunities for personal development
  • Attractive training opportunities and motivated team
  • Good social benefits and at least 5 weeks holidays
