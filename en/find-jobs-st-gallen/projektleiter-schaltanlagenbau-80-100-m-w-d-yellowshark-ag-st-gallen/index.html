01 Future employer

For an established company in the field of electrical engineering and automation, we are looking for a committed individual who enjoys managing switchgear projects holistically and leading a team both professionally and personally. You can expect a varied role with a high degree of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and exciting technical challenges.

02 Your task

  • You will independently manage several switchgear projects, from technical and commercial preparation through successful handover.
  • In doing so, you will coordinate material procurement, resources and staff deployment and ensure smooth project execution.
  • You will lead the employees assigned to you and support your team with technical and organizational issues.
  • You will keep costs, deadlines and quality under control at all times and initiate suitable measures at an early stage in the event of deviations.
  • You will maintain professional contact with customers and actively develop existing business relationships.
  • Thanks to your technical understanding, you will identify additional customer needs and thereby contribute to winning follow-up and additional orders.
  • You will work closely with internal departments and other project participants and ensure efficient collaboration.

04 Your profile

  • A completed vocational qualification as an automation technician EFZ, switchgear assembler EFZ, or in a comparable electrical engineering profession.
  • Further training in switchgear construction, project management, or electrical engineering is an advantage.
  • Professional experience in the planning, design, or implementation of switchgear and control systems.
  • Initial or extensive experience in managing employees, as well as enthusiasm for taking on responsibility for a team.
  • Good knowledge of using CAD applications and a strong affinity for digital tools.
  • An independent, solution-oriented, and structured way of working.
  • Strong organizational skills as well as a good technical and commercial understanding.
  • A communicative, proactive, and team-oriented personality who enjoys driving things forward.
