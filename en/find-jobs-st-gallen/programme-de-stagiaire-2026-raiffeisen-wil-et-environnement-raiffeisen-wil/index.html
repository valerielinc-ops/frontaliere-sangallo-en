Full description

For the program starting on October 1, 2026, we are looking for a communicative, willing to learn and enthusiastic trainee (f/m/d).

Tasks

The trainee program at Raiffeisenbank Wil and the surrounding area offers you, as a university graduate (f/m/d) with specialist and leadership potential, an ideal starting point for the start of your banking career. During 12 months, you will get to know different areas of day-to-day business on the job and actively support the bank management in strategy and project development. You will be specifically prepared for your future target function in the management office. Through internal and external training, you can continually expand your specialist knowledge and expand your network of contacts within the Raiffeisen Group through various activities.

Requirements

  • High social and self-competence
  • Conceptual and networked thinking skills
  • Willingness to work and learn
  • German language skills B2
