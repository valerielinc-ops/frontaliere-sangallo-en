Full description

The GBS St.Gallen is a leading vocational training institution in Eastern Switzerland. Around 4 500 vocational learners are trained in over 45 occupations of basic vocational training. We are known for innovative teaching methods and the continuous development of our offerings – always with the aim of optimally preparing our learners for the future.

What to expect

  • Guidance for learners in the development of creative concepts for interactive and digital applications of electronic devices
  • Providing basic knowledge for planning, creating and implementing projects with interactive and digital content
  • Fulfillment of the professional mandate for teachers at vocational colleges
  • Further education in the pedagogical-didactic field or the willingness to complete one

What sets it apart

  • Communicative personality with strong social skills, exemplary demeanor and pleasure in dealing with learners and teaching
  • Several years of professional experience in online marketing and digital marketing
  • Experience with social media, SEO/SEA, content marketing and analytics and enjoyment of digital trends and new technologies
  • Experience in campaign planning and digital communication as well as interest in visual design and interactive media
  • Ability to convey complex content in an understandable and practical manner, ideally experience in vocational training or adult education
  • Willingness to develop teaching materials, exercises and projects independently

What we offer

An interesting and challenging activity in a motivated environment. The recruitment takes place under the Supplementary Regulation on the employment of teachers at vocational and training centres. Interested and motivated? We look forward to your complete application documents. Please submit your dossier by 18 October 2026 via the link below.
