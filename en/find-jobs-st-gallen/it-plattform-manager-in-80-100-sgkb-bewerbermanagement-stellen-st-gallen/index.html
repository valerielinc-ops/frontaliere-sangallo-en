Full description

“We inspire our customers with stable, secure and sustainable IT platforms.” At the headquarters in St. Gallen, you take responsibility for the reliable operation and further development of our IT platforms. You understand the technical connections, keep an eye on the status and life cycle of the platforms and control necessary adjustments together with our external service providers.

This is what you will do.

  • For the IT platforms assigned to you, you are responsible for the entire life cycle, control their further development and ensure stable, secure and reliable operation.
  • You keep an eye on the technical status, availability and performance of the systems, recognize the need for action at an early stage and initiate the necessary measures.
  • You analyse and specify changes to the platforms, commission their implementation with our external service providers and ensure their professional implementation.
  • In the event of disruptions and technical problems, you take over the coordination, carry out root cause analyses together with the participating bodies and ensure a sustainable solution.
  • Lifecycle, maintenance, patch and upgrade topics you plan ahead and coordinate their implementation with internal specialist departments and external partners.
  • Relevant configurations and operational information are documented in a comprehensible manner and together with the participating agencies we continuously develop our operational processes and standards.

You bring that with you

  • A higher education in computer science (HF/FH) or a comparable qualification forms the basis of your profile.
  • Several years of experience in the operation and support of complex IT systems or IT platforms as well as experience in provider management distinguish you. You have a sound technical understanding and are familiar with the coordination of changes, incidents and technical operational tasks.
  • In modern platform landscapes, you move safely and understand the interaction of different technology components. Experience with Avaloq, databases such as Oracle, PostgreSQL, MS SQL or MongoDB, Kafka as well as container platforms such as Kubernetes or OpenShift are ideal.
  • You understand the concepts and connections of modern platforms and can holistically assess and connect topics such as CI/CD, monitoring and observability as well as identity & access management. Technologies such as GitLab, Argo CD, Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus or Keycloak are familiar to you.
  • You think analytically, work in a structured and responsible way and quickly capture complex technical contexts. You communicate clearly, bindingly and on an equal footing with internal and external stakeholders.
