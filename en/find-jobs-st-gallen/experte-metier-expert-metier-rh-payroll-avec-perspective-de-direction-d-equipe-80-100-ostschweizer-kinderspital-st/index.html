Full description

To strengthen our HRM team, we are looking for a committed personality who takes responsibility and actively shapes the further development of our HR processes. You are a payroll professional, have a distinct flair for numbers? Then we look forward to meeting you!

Their tasks

  • Together with the team, they ensure correct and timely wage processing for around 1,000 employees.
  • They deal with challenging questions about wages, social insurance and withholding taxes and are the contact person for internal and external offices.
  • You take responsibility for the annual work and the quality assurance in the payroll.
  • They support HR controlling in the creation and analysis of HR key figures, statistics and evaluations as well as in the further development of reporting.
  • Together with the team, you ensure the correct processing of time management data and support you with questions about time management.
  • They support and optimize our HR systems and processes, in particular SAP HR and Polypoint PEP, and are actively involved in digitalization projects.
  • Depending on your experience and interest, you will also be responsible for the technical and personnel management of the HR Payroll & Controlling team.

Your profile

  • They bring a basic commercial education and a completed further education in the payroll sector, ideally supplemented by a background in HR or social security.
  • They have several years of experience in wage processing and in-depth knowledge of Swiss social security law.
  • You have experience or interest in HR controlling and have a strong understanding of numbers.
  • You are well versed in dealing with SAP HR and Excel, ideally you also have experience with Polypoint PEP and Power BI.
  • They work independently, structured and solution-oriented and enjoy the optimization of processes.

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together
  • Design leeway, because you can actively shape the further development of our HR processes
  • Modern working environment, because you benefit from a modern infrastructure in the new Eastern Swiss Children's Hospital

Your work area Our HRM team looks after around 1,000 employees at the Eastern Swiss Children’s Hospital and is in an exciting phase of further development. In the course of a personnel change, we are looking for an experienced specialist for our HR Payroll & Controlling department. In addition to ensuring professional contract processing, the function offers the opportunity to actively shape and further develop our systems and processes.
