09.09.2026 Shame of Amden

Become part of our team!

We – Raiffeisenbank Schänis-Amden – are a locally anchored consulting bank and employ more than 30 employees at our two locations in Schänis and Amden, including three apprentices. As a modern employer, we continuously promote and challenge our employees. Their further development is just as important to us as a balanced work-life balance. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated and service-oriented personality as a customer consultant for our headquarters in Schänis and our office in Amden.

What do you expect?

  • Independent implementation of consulting services (basic advice with basic solutions, savings and figures as well as simple investment and pension issues) at the reception desk and during consultations
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the living situation of customers
  • Demand recognition and forwarding of customers to the relevant specialist consultants
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support
  • Cooperation in market development measures and performance of representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • A completed banking education
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact
  • Winning, competent and professional appearance
  • Good communication and social skills
  • Flexibility, reliability, trustworthiness and teamwork

Our focus is on the customer. Contribute to the family togetherness in our bank! We look forward to seeing you! Sarah Jöhl’ Head of Customer Services Why Raiffeisen?
