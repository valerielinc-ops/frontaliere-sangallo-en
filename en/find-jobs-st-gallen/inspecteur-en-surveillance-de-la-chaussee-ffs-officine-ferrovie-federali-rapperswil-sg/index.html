Inspector:in surveillance Roadway 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Inspector:under surveillance Roadway Rapperswil SG, 01.11.2026 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In the Infrastructure Division, you are shaping the backbone of Swiss mobility. You ensure that trains are safe, punctual and reliable throughout the country. In this varied and responsible function, you contribute directly to safety on the rail. A well-rehearsed, collegial team looks forward to welcoming you. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You carry out plant and InOffice inspections in the area of road systems and document your results with the available tools.
  • With your technical sense, you carry out standardized measurement work on track and switch installations, independently or in a team and ensure precise protocols in the systems.
  • You take the necessary immediate measures and report safety-related deficiencies immediately so that train traffic remains safe.
  • You will support the Technical Assistant in the planning and implementation of route inspections as well as in the evaluation and analysis of the results. Your observations are crucial for sustainable solutions.
  • Assignments as security manager: in and occasionally as security guard:in complement your varied area of responsibility.

You bring that with you.

  • You work independently, reliably and take responsibility. Safety is your top priority.
  • Organizational talent, initiative and flexibility set you apart.
  • You have several years of practical experience in the field of road systems and know the challenges on site.
  • When dealing with IT applications, you are well-versed and open to further expanding your knowledge.
  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship in traffic infrastructure construction / civil engineering as well as the driver’s license Cat. B. Professional training for foremen:in, polisher:in or chief fitter:in rounds off your profile.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in requirement level F, Regional Allowance Level 1.
