As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • You accompany and supervise patients with an increased need for care in a 1:1 setting, each from 22:30 to 7:00
  • You recognize changes in behavior or well-being at an early stage and inform the responsible nurse immediately
  • You prevent falls, self-endangerment and the removal of catheters or infusions
  • You support with simple everyday activities such as camping, drinking or giving orientation – after consultation with the care team
  • You provide a calm, trusting atmosphere and respond sensitively to the needs of patients.

Your profile

  • Experience in caring for people, ideally in hospital, long-term care or psychiatry
  • Advantages: Training as a nursing assistant / SRK, assistant / Health and Social EBA or an ongoing study in nursing or medicine
  • Rest, patience and empathy, even in challenging situations
  • Reliability, secrecy and an alert eye for change
  • Flexibility for short-term assignments, especially at night and on weekends
  • Good knowledge of German in Word (at least level B2); Other languages are welcome

Retired people are also welcome. Do you enjoy continuing to engage meaningfully? Then we look forward to you as a seat guard.

  • Cheap Reka checks and much more!
