Full description

MAIN TASKS

In your role, you are responsible for ensuring the quality of our products and components from development to series release. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Define quality requirements and ensure product quality in development projects and product changes
  • Creation of test concepts and test plans as well as coordination and evaluation of the necessary tests
  • Planning, accompanying and evaluating practical tests
  • Applying and moderating quality methods such as reviews and FMEA
  • Conducting supplier and process audits
  • Close cooperation with development, project and product management, procurement, production, suppliers and other interfaces

PROFILE

You bring a technical degree FH, a degree as a technician HF/TS or a comparable technical qualification. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • Several years of experience in quality management, preferably in the industrial environment
  • Further education in quality technology as well as knowledge in sanitary technology is advantageous
  • Experience in project work and in interdisciplinary problem solving
  • Analytical and holistic thinking skills
  • Independent, structured and self-responsible working method
  • Communication strength, negotiation skills and confident appearance
  • Good knowledge of SAP and Microsoft Office
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
