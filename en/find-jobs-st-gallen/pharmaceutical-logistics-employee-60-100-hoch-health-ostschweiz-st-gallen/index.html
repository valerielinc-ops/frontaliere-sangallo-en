Pharma Logistics Employee 60-100%

Hospital Pharmacy

Work location St. Gallen

Job advertisement

Your tasks and prospects

  • Responsibility for the receipt of medicines and medical devices in the hospital pharmacy
  • Provision of infusions and disinfectants for the ward pharmacies at all hospital locations of HOCH Health Ostschweiz
  • Involvement in the automated picking system from 2027
  • Support for all logistics processes through the ERP system SAP-MM

What you bring to this position

  • Completed vocational training with an EFZ in logistics or equivalent training, as well as an interest in and willingness to learn the processes of pharmaceutical logistics
  • Trustworthy person who enjoys working in a small team and has good physical stamina
  • Precise and independent way of working
  • Very good German language skills, both spoken and written, are required

What we offer you

  • Interesting work in a collegial and motivated team
  • Insight into the diverse processes of a hospital pharmacy
  • Friendly, open and motivated team
  • Employee benefits in the area of health promotion as well as various discounts

Your application

  • Start date 1. January 2027 or by agreement, fixed-term until 31. April - 2027
  • More about this exciting position can be learned from Michaela Portmann, Team Lead Pharma Logistics, telephone +41 71 494 26 51
  • For questions about the application process, please contact Sara Reisenhofer-Weber, HR Business Partner, telephone +41 71 494 38 61
