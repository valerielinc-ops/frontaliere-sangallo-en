Apprenticeship as an assistant in health and social affairs EBA

Get your EBA through nursing training. With us you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a health and social assistant you can expect a supportive working environment from day one in which you can help people and are accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For Haus Steinegg/Feldegg in Degersheim we are looking for you as of August 1, 2027 for the apprenticeship position as Assistant Health and Social EBA

You'll learn that with us

  • Care, support and support of guests in everyday situations
  • Support with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Accompanying the guests on a daily basis with games, crafts, singing, etc.
  • Domestic, administrative and logistical tasks
  • Compliance and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations
  • Electronic nursing documentation

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school B or C)
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

What benefits do we offer you?

You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get your birthday off
  • CHF 500 towards your compulsory school materials
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Each lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounted mobile phone subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts and offers relating to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • And much more...

You start straight away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your training completion must be celebrated properly, so we organize an unforgettable graduation party for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.
