Full description

Give your job a new look FISBA is one of the world's leading suppliers in the optics industry and stands for outstanding performance from optical design and system engineering to high-precision series production and coating. FISBA operates from its headquarters in Switzerland and branches in Germany, the USA and China. The company is privately owned. We employ over 360 employees from 30 nations worldwide. In the Operations department, Optical Coating Technology (OCT) department, we are looking for a This is your future activity

  • Operation, installation and monitoring of high-vacuum coating and cleaning plant
  • Implementation of quality controls and tests of optics and coatings by means of suitable measuring and testing equipment (e.g. microscope, spectrophotometer)
  • Documentation of ongoing production with corresponding quality certificates (booking in ERP, creation of measurement protocols)
  • Preparation, cleaning and pretreatment of optics, tools and plant components
  • Ensuring the availability of consumables (test glasses, tools, coating materials and aids)
  • Acquisition of data to determine overall plant effectiveness (BDE)
  • Support for various production areas as required and active participation in the continuous optimization of processes
  • Active feedback on production processes and active participation in the daily shopfloor meeting
