Full description

The Direct Payments Department is part of the Department of Agriculture and is responsible for the implementation of agricultural legislation and the settlement of direct payments to farmers in the Canton of St.Gallen.

What to expect

  • Independent spatial data management for direct execution
  • Editing GIS special topics such as orchards QII, PFAS maps, Alpine maps, biodiversity topics
  • Developing GIS solutions for the implementation of new processes and tools in the new agricultural data system GIS
  • Provides GIS specialist support for the department and for external stakeholder groups as well as training for municipal agriculture and farmers

What sets it apart

  • Completed studies ETH/FH in agricultural, environmental or natural sciences, geography or equivalent education
  • Experience in Area Data Management and in-depth GIS knowledge
  • Basic agricultural knowledge beneficial
  • Joy of dealing with farmers

What we offer

  • High level of independence and personal responsibility
  • Interesting and versatile activity with direct reference to agriculture
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated and collaborative team
  • Flexible working time models, possibility for one day of home office per week
  • Place of work at a central and well accessible location in St.Gallen
