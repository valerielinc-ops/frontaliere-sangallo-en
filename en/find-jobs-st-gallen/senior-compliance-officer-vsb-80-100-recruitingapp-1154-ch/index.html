Full description

«We inspire our customers with competence, attention and reliability» Do you want to actively shape these values in everyday work? As our new colleague, you support the compliance department at our headquarters in St. Gallen or at our headquarters in Zurich. You contribute your expertise, assess complex issues and contribute to the further development of our compliance requirements and processes. We look forward to welcoming you to SGKB.

This is what you will do.

  • You will advise and assist with more complex questions about the VSB and in connection with the Transparency Register and carry out legal clarifications.
  • With comprehensive compliance and risk analyses, you independently assess relevant questions and derive sound insights from them.
  • As part of the internal control system (IKS), you independently carry out controls of the 2nd Line and ensure their comprehensible documentation.
  • You create reports and training materials and participate in the implementation of training courses.
  • For instructions, processes and compliance applications, you take responsibility. You develop specifications, define technical requirements and submit your own suggestions for improvement. You also work in projects in the area of VSB and Transparency Register.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed degree, preferably in law, or a completed banking apprenticeship with appropriate further education.
  • Several years of banking experience in the compliance area and in-depth expertise in the field of VSB characterize your professional background.
  • Your analytical and conceptual skills help you to assess complex questions in a structured way and develop solutions.
  • A reliable and solution-oriented way of working distinguishes you as well as your teamwork, communication strength and discretion.
