Full description

The Civil Engineering Office of the Canton of St.Gallen employs around 300 people. Together we plan, build and maintain our cantonal road, so that the next generations can also draw on a good transport infrastructure and be mobile.

What to expect

  • Insight into almost the entire construction segment, with the focus of the training in the field of civil engineering (road construction, sewerage, settlement development) and engineering (supporting construction)
  • Project planning of construction projects from the preliminary project to the execution project together with the project management
  • Planning of projects up to maturity together with project management
  • Development of project plans using CAD under the guidance of the project management
  • Regular site visits and photographs on site (measurement, inventories) for interesting and instructive insights into practice
  • Support activities such as mass calculation or tender documentation

What distinguishes you

  • Higher education at secondary level
  • Joy of mathematics and a flair for technical drawing
  • Good spatial imagination
  • Careful and exact working methods
  • Interest in construction

What we offer

  • Various training in public administration
  • Active support for your school and professional development
  • Collegial and well-established and supportive team
  • A modern infrastructure
