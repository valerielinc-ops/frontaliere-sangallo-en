Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an employee for 40% - 50%. At the main reception, you act as a visiting card of the Rosenklinik and the first contact person for inquiries of all kinds from internal and external bodies. A customer-friendly representation of our special clinic is important to us. They are the central point of contact for customers, suppliers, business partners and service providers.

Their tasks are

  • First point of contact for patients, visitors, service providers and suppliers
  • Telephone system maintenance services
  • Distribution of incoming mail
  • Check-in and check-out of patients incl. Debt collection
  • Cooperation with internal gastronomy
  • Miscellaneous secretarial work
  • Experience in hospital reception or in similar customer-oriented reception points is an advantage
  • Happy, friendly and positive treatment of people
  • Maintained Appearance
  • Responsible, resilient and flexible personality
  • Readiness to work during shifts (early and late duty, no night duty) and on weekends
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous

Rosenklinik Rapperswil offers a wide range of services with a special focus on orthopaedics and prosthetics. In addition, they are the first hospital in Switzerland to work holistically according to agile+®, an established treatment concept for the rapid return to everyday life after orthopaedic procedures. Rosenklinik Rapperswil lives a family tradition and strives for innovation
