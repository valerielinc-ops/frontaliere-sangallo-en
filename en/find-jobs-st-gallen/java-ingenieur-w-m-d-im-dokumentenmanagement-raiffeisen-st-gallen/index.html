Full description

To strengthen our records management team at the St.Gallen and Zurich Airport (Circle) locations, we are looking for a committed personality as a Java Engineer (f/m/d) in document management, immediately or by arrangement.

Tasks

  • Collecting, analyzing and documenting technical requirements in collaboration with the departments
  • Translating technical requirements into technical specifications and implementation concepts
  • Ensuring regulatory requirements and auditable processes
  • Development and implementation of modern backend solutions based on Java, Kotlin and Spring Boot
  • Participation in the further development of microservice architectures and event-driven solutions with Kafka
  • Automation of DevSecOps processes and development of security monitoring and alerting
  • Close collaboration in an agile, interdisciplinary team with a high level of personal responsibility

Requirements

  • Completed studies (FH/University) or vocational training in computer science or comparable training or further education
  • Experience in agile functional and technical requirements engineering
  • Very good knowledge of modern Java development (Java, Kotlin, Spring Boot), Kafka, GitLab and Helm
  • Experience in developing and operating microservices
  • Knowledge of cloud technologies and container platforms such as OpenShift or Kubernetes
  • Solid experience with Doxis as well as automated testing and CI/CD pipelines
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as a structured, team-oriented and solution-oriented way of working

We offer
