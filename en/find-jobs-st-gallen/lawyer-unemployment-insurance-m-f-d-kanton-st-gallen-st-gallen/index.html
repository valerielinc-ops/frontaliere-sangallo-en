Full description

The cantonal office (KAST) is the legal service of the unemployment insurance and ensures a uniform implementation of the Unemployment Insurance Act (AVIG) at cantonal level. Notifications and transfers by the Regional Employment Centres (RAV) and the Unemployment Funds on entitlement issues are processed by KAST and corresponding orders and objections are issued.

What to expect

  • Addressing challenging legal issues regarding the mediation capacity and reasonableness of work and pre-declaration of short-time and bad weather compensation
  • Processing of complex objections against dispositions and decisions of unemployment insurance
  • Representation of unemployment insurance in appeal proceedings before the Insurance Court
  • Support of the Head of KAST in AVIG implementation
  • Processing of requests for administrative and legal assistance and replying to requests for data protection and access to files
  • Conduct of prudential procedures
  • Participation in internal training of unemployment insurance employees

What sets it apart

  • Completed university degree in law (MLaw, lic.iur.)
  • professional experience in procedural law; preferably experience in public administration with a focus on social security law
  • Interest in political and economic events
  • High performance, resilience and efficient operation

What we offer

  • Independent activity and design space within the function
  • Careful training in a competent team
  • Modern infrastructure at a central and easily accessible location in St.Gallen
  • Advanced working time models and interesting training opportunities
