Full description

Aufgaben

  • Welcome every guest with an authentic "Welcome" and accompany him in style to the "Arrivederci".
  • Manage your station with confidence, elegance and a perfect sense of service rhythm.
  • Tips dishes and wines with passion, telling the story and soul of Nesso.
  • Take care of every step of the service, ensuring an impeccable experience.
  • Create small special moments that surprise and stay in the guest memory.
  • You are a real master / a real hostess and contribute to the team with heart and class.

Anforderungen

  • You speak Italian and feel comfortable in contact with guests.
  • You communicate well in German or English and move safely in an international environment.
  • You already have experience in the service and love the energy of work in the restaurant.
  • Bring true Italianity with you: joy of living, warmth and passion for good food.
  • You are a practical person, proactive and keep your charm even in the most intense moments.
  • Training in the sector is a plus, but it is not indispensable.

Wir bieten

  • Test nights and facilities at Thermal Spa
  • Free tickets to the Tamina Gorge (Tamina Schlucht)
  • Test stay (free night) after overcoming the trial period
  • Discount up to 50% in restaurants
  • Discount up to 50% in the fitness center and at the Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origin in the healing thermal waters of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place represents regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis we develop the resort in a sequential way – with the aim of becoming the main resort of Wellness & Healthy Living in Europe and creating a powerful place for generations.

Approximately 800 employees daily shape the experience of our guests. Different talents, cultures and personalities work here together to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common ambition to make this special place a little better every day – One Resort – One Team.
