Full description

Personnel/in telephone center night service 30%

Department of Real Estate & Operation, Security & Service Management

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Intermediation of internal and external calls in the telephone exchange, especially during night services from 22:00 to 06:30/07:00, sporadic operations in the day service
  • Providing all kinds of information about patient rooms, visiting hours, opening hours, responsibilities, accessibility and location of ambulances and clinics
  • Offering of pikett and special assignments in accordance with the instructions of the hospital areas
  • Triage of emergencies to the competent doctors

What you bring for this job

  • Basic commercial training completed
  • Years of professional experience
  • Good computer science application knowledge and manners as well as strong communication skills and high resilience
  • We require good oral English skills, Italian and French skills are an advantage
  • Flexibility and willingness to work irregular hours (especially night work) on all days of the week

Our offer to you

  • Individual training plan, two months in day service
  • Team-oriented collaboration in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Varied tasks and independent work
  • Modern workplace infrastructure
