Full description

The Spitex organization of the municipality of Berneck is an integral part of the city centre. As a future-oriented service provider, it ensures ambulatory health care in Berneck. Our dedicated team cares for around 30 people of different ages at home. To complement our five-member care team, we are looking for a Care professional HF/FH 40 – 60 % Their tasks As the main task, you are responsible for the holistic care and advice of our clients and provide treatment and basic care. They also maintain contacts with relatives and treating doctors.

They bring with them

  • Empathic interaction with clients and the ability to capture situations holistically
  • Good computer skills
  • RAI/HC Knowledge is beneficial
  • Readiness for flexible deployment times
  • Category B driving licence and, if possible, own vehicle

What we offer you

  • Possibility to work independently
  • Collaboration in a team-oriented environment
  • Exciting challenges with varied and challenging cases
  • Ongoing training opportunities for personal and professional development
  • Work on a maximum of one weekend per month and closing time from 7 pm at the latest (also for shared services)
