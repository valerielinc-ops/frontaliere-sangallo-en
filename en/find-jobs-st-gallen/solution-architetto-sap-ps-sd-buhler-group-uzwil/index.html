Full description

Are you passionate about designing innovative SAP solutions and shaping the next generation of business processes? Join our team and play a key role in shaping and implementing the end-to-end architecture for customer project processes across SAP PS & SD, and surrounding systems. Combining architectural responsibility with hands-on development, you will collaborate with Product Owners, development teams, and business stakeholders to deliver scalable, integrated, and future-ready solutions for our global organization.
