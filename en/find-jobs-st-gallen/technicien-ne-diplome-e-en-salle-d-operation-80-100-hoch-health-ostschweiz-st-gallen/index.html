Dipl. Specialist surgical technique 80-100 %

Orthopedics and Traumatology / Plastic Surgery and Hand Surgery

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Instrumentation and service in the field of orthopaedics and traumatology, Spine Surgery (East Swiss Vertebral Centre), Plastic Surgery and Hand Surgery as well as interdisciplinary applications
  • Professional preparation and follow-up and operation of medical-technical devices
  • Active participation in smooth operating procedures in a dynamic operation, taking into account the hygiene guidelines
  • Ensuring safety standards and preventive measures
  • Taking over of administrative activities

What you bring for this job

  • Training as a Dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF, preferably experience in the field of orthopaedics and traumatology, spine surgery (East Swiss Spinal Center) as well as plastic surgery and hand surgery
  • Professional expertise and interest in personal development
  • Openness to change and joy of innovation
  • Enthusiasm for working with people
  • Resilience and flexibility

Our offer to you

  • Use of innovative technologies such as Velys robotics in knee surgery or augmented reality in shoulder surgery, navigated spinal surgery and lobe surgery in the field of plastic surgery
  • Work in the new and modern operating rooms
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Engaged collaboration in a specialized interdisciplinary environment
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family, various perks, etc.

About us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a versatile working environment. Three operating departments, 22 operating rooms and a working environment where precision, teamwork and modern surgical techniques come together. In HOCH’s operating room, you work interdisciplinary, at university level and in a team that consciously combines experience and new impulses. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to the common future. We focus on team spirit, interdisciplinary cooperation and innovative solutions.
