Full description

Are you excited about technology? Do you not only want to keep plants running, but also optimize and develop them? Then bring your know-how actively with us. A varied task awaits you, a collegial team and a task with a lot of personal responsibility. We look forward to your application!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Gossau headquarters, Industriestrasse 47, 9200 Gossau

Mansioni

  • You ensure that our production facilities run smoothly: With your know-how, you take over preventive maintenance and repair disruptions quickly and sustainably.
  • You are responsible for special orders and actively work on maintenance projects, from the implementation of new plants to conversions and optimization of existing production lines.
  • You develop and create technical concepts, maintenance plans, parts lists as well as comprehensible documentation and thus contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes.
  • You produce customized spare parts and individual devices and keep an overview of our spare parts warehouse.
  • You support the Head of Plant Engineering in the varied day-to-day business and actively contribute your ideas.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in maintenance
  • Completed basic or further training: in the technical field (e.g. as a polymechanic, automatic engineer, operating mechanic or electrician)
  • Appropriate further training in the technical field is desired (e.g. maintenance specialist)
  • Team-capable, initiative personality with practical thinking, structured working methods and interest in new technologies
  • Sound PC user knowledge (Office Palette)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Education and training: offer of a diverse internal course programme as well as financial participation in professional external training
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Handy subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
