Full description

You have experience in clinical research and know the requirements for scientific studies and regulatory documentation? Do you want to develop research projects independently and transfer scientific findings into practice? For an established company with an international orientation, we are looking for a scientifically-savvy personality who develops, accompanies clinical projects and supports the further development of existing products. Activities

  • Developing and accompanying clinical studies and scientific research projects
  • Evaluation of scientific literature, clinical data and research results
  • Identify evidence gaps and derive appropriate research questions
  • Development of study concepts with suitable methods and endpoints
  • Creating and developing clinical and regulatory documentation
  • Assistance in clinical evaluations as well as PMS, PMCF and PSUR
  • Establishment and coordination of cooperation with external research partners and specialist institutions
  • Accompanying scientific projects until the evaluation, publication and use of the results
  • Support in the scientific justification of product claims and in product developments
  • Participation in research collaborations, funding programmes and grant applications

Conditions

  • Completed university studies in biomedicine, medical technology, medicine, biology, life sciences, pharmacy or a comparable natural science discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in Medical Affairs or Clinical Affairs in the regulated medical device environment
  • Demonstrate experience in the development, initiation and scientific support of clinical studies and research projects
  • In-depth knowledge of clinical regulatory requirements and documentation for medical devices
  • Practical experience with clinical evaluation, PMS, PMCF and PSUR
  • Knowledge of study design, research methodology, scientific literature evaluation and statistical interpretation of results
  • Experience in the scientific substantiation of product statements is advantageous
  • Advantageous doctorate or comparable scientific qualification
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Independent, analytical and structured working method

Motivation

  • Responsible position with versatile scientific tasks
  • Possibility to contribute your own ideas and expertise
  • Home office option and flexible working structures
  • High personal responsibility and scope for design
  • International working environment
  • Cooperation with external experts and research organisations
  • Modern working environment and short decision paths
  • Attractive and market-oriented employment conditions
