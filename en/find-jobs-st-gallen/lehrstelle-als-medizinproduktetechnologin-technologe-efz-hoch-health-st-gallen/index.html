Apprenticeship as medical device technologist EFZ

Training centres

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • You clean, disinfect and sterilize surgical instruments and devices for diagnosis and patient care
  • You will learn how to use cleaning and disinfecting equipment (RDG) and contribute to the safety of medical interventions
  • You are responsible for the control and proper functioning of the operating instruments
  • You put medical devices together into sets, check, package and sterilize them with special procedures that you get to know with us
  • Your work also includes the operation of various IT systems

What you bring for this job

  • You have successfully completed the compulsory schooling until the start of training
  • You have skillful hands and like to work extremely carefully and accurately
  • You are physically resilient
  • The safety of our patients and the associated hygiene and order are important to you
  • Irregular working hours are no problem for you
  • For work you must have no problems with color recognition

Our offer to you

  • You will complete your apprenticeship in one of the largest central sterile supplies in Europe
  • The central sterile goods supply is responsible for the entire canton of St.Gallen. You can therefore look forward to a very varied working day
  • You will learn about a wide variety of surgical instruments of all disciplines such as orthopaedic surgery, gynecology, neurosurgery, ophthalmology, neck-nose-ear clinic, etc.
  • For the cost of your school supplies you will receive financial support
  • You are allowed to attend internal training courses and information events

Your application

  • Entrance by 06. August 2027. You can find out more about this exciting apprenticeship on our website or by Jasmin Steingruber, telephone +41 71 494 23 54
  • Take your chance and complete your education at HOCH Health Eastern Switzerland

About us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a versatile working environment. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to the common future. We focus on team spirit, interdisciplinary cooperation and innovative solutions.
