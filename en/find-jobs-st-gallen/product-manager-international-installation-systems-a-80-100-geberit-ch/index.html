Full description

MAIN TASKS

Do you want to actively design international products and successfully bring innovations to the market? As Product Manager International, you are responsible for the product area of control plates. You combine market understanding, technical know-how and entrepreneurial thinking and successfully lead interdisciplinary teams to the goal. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Strategic development of the international product portfolio in the field of operating plates
  • Development and implementation of product and portfolio strategies over the entire product lifecycle
  • Analysis of market, customer and technology trends
  • Development of business cases, prioritization of product initiatives and definition of product requirements
  • Responsibility for product launches, product changes and portfolio adjustments
  • Intensive cooperation with international sales companies as well as internal areas such as development, production, quality, purchasing and logistics
  • Place of work Jona (SG, Switzerland) with occasional international travel

PROFILE

You bring a completed engineering degree, supplemented by a business education. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • Several years of experience in product management, innovation management or product development, preferably with a background in the multi-level B2B business.
  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking and experience in the development and marketing of successful products
  • Ability to translate technical, economic and market requirements into sustainable product solutions
  • High initiative, decision-making strength and implementation orientation combined with a pronounced hands-on mentality
  • Excellent communication, moderation and stakeholder management skills
  • Enjoying international cooperation and leading projects and teams without direct authority
  • Style-confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
