Full description

The Department of Computer Science Cluster of the Department of Education, divided into six regional competence centers (clusters), ensures the computer science operation for all cantonal schools of the secondary level II (vocational and secondary schools). We support schools in the professional implementation of computer science projects and provide tools that ensure the smooth use of computer science resources in teaching and school administration. The team management and administration of the Computer Science Cluster department provides cross-sectional services for the regional competence centers. In addition, the control of the operation of the school administration solution Nesa, based on the product schulNetz, is located here.

What to expect

  • Collection, analysis and change management of specialist requirements of secondary school and offices at Nesa
  • Coordination of all schools in the implementation of adjustments in Nesa
  • Constant process optimization and further development around school administration processes
  • Formulation of specifications, monitoring of software updates and implementation/coordination of quality tests
  • Advice and support in process optimization in the context of the school administration Nesa
  • Ensure the operational readiness of Nesa in the event of absence or discharge of the manager
  • Initiation and coordination of disturbance measures
  • Participation in incident, problem and change management processes
  • Participation in documentation and administrative work

What distinguishes you

  • University degree or secondary technical school (e.g. business informatics, business administration), further education such as the DAS Business Analyst or equivalent training
  • Method-specific certifications (e.g. CPRE for Requirements Engineering) or similar qualifications
  • Experience with school administration solutions, in particular schoolnetwork, and school administration processes
  • High service orientation and commitment
  • Pleasant, correct scope and enforcement
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good knowledge of English are advantageous
  • Teamwork, assertiveness and independent work
  • Strong analytical skills and IT affinity

What we offer

With us you will find a pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated, open-minded team and a versatile and varied activity. In addition, a modernly furnished workplace and contemporary employment conditions with interesting further education opportunities await you. We offer the possibility of home office and remote work by arrangement.

Contact: René Lerch, Operations Manager Nesa, Tel 058 510 97 04 or MS Teams or email: rene.lerch@ic-sg.ch
