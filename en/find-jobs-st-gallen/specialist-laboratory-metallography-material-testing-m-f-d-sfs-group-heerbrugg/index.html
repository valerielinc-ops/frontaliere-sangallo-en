Full description

Progress is often in the detail. In our accredited testing laboratory, we create important foundations for continuous development and optimization by examining a wide variety of raw materials as well as cold-formed and heat-treated metal parts. Would you like to contribute with your sharper eyes, your expertise and your careful work? Your Tasks:

  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of material tests and metallographic investigations according to specifications
  • Investigation of semi-finished and finished products from our production plants, purchased parts, competition patterns and test series with the methods and equipment available in the laboratory
  • Preparation of laboratory test reports including image documentation, evaluation and archiving of results
  • Implementation and documentation of maintenance and calibration work on laboratory equipment
  • Cooperation in the organisation of the accredited laboratory, in inter-laboratory trials, as well as in the evaluation and procurement of new and replacement equipment
  • Recognizing and stimulating corrective measures to improve quality as well as blocking non-compliant products and coordinating the further procedure with the head of laboratory QM-Manager Your profile:
  • Completed apprenticeship as a physics laboratory or materials tester or comparable technical training
  • Professional experience in metallography and materials testing, ideally beneficial in the metalworking industry
  • Experience in general laboratory activities and in carefully documenting and evaluating test results
  • Knowledge of German at native language level and good knowledge of English
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method as well as a strong quality awareness
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of working together in Team We offer:
  • Varied tasks
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible
  • Annual participation in success
  • Own healthy pension fund with individual savings plans
  • Internal training courses & support for external training
  • Other benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
