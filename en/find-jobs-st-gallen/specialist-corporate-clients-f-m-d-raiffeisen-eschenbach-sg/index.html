Full description

06.07.2026 Eschenbach SG

Become part of our team!

At Raiffeisenbank am Ricken, the focus is on the people – our customers as well as our employees. We trust them and take their concerns seriously. In this way, we create space for partnership-based and individual solutions that offer real added value – today and tomorrow. We are committed to our region with conviction. And because we think ahead, we shape a sustainable future together with our employees and our members.

What awaits you?

  • Well-established, established and motivated team with pleasure at the job
  • Self-employment thanks to entrepreneurial freedom
  • Appreciative cooperation based on mutual trust
  • Preparation of loan applications incl. Preparation of loan templates and contract documents for corporate clients, real estate promoters, general contractors and their exponents
  • Management of existing credit positions, preparation and implementation of periodic new approvals, construction loan processing in compliance with regulatory requirements
  • Analysis of annual accounts
  • Support of corporate client consultants in all administrative matters incl. Pre- and post-processing of conversations
  • Telephone and written communications with offices, insurance companies and other banks
  • Opportunity to support the development of apprentices and employees

What do you bring?

  • Fun at work and a good dose of humor and teamwork, fast comprehension and networked and analytical thinking
  • Completed banking education with many years of experience and comprehensive expertise in the field of loans and mortgages. Your experiences from customer advice or credit processing help you in this function
  • High willingness to learn, open to own development
  • Experience in the business / corporate customer sector is an advantage
  • High degree of independence, responsible yet efficient and customer- and service-oriented working methods

Are you looking for a customer-oriented specialist function without a customer book – but with your own skills? Then apply now – a collegial and open-minded team is looking forward to you! Marcel Hubli Head of Corporate Client Services Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote personal and professional development
