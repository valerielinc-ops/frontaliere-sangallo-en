Full description

The General Secretariat is the central department of the Department of Finance. Among other things, it is responsible for planning, coordination and project work in the area of finance and financial policy.

What to expect

  • Interesting and challenging work in the economist team of the finance department at the interface of administration, government and cantonal council
  • Examination and assessment of governmental and cantonal council activities from all departments
  • Support of the department head, the general secretary and the economist team on economic and business issues
  • Participation in working and project groups as a representative of the Financial Department
  • Participation in preparing reports and presentations

What distinguishes you

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in economics (FH/uni), preferably with a focus on economics or public management
  • Interest in financial and political issues
  • Rapid comprehension and independent working methods
  • A stylish oral and written expression in German

What we offer

  • An exciting environment with multiple challenges
  • Excellent opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Interesting tasks at the interface of politics, government and administration as well as an exchange with a wide range of stakeholders
  • Flexible working and contemporary employment conditions and an attractive workplace in the center of the city of St.Gallen The job is particularly suitable for a career start after a completed study. The position is limited in time until the end of March 2028. At this time, a reorganisation will take place in the General Secretariat of the Department of Finance. Depending on the suitability, there is the possibility for further employment.

Contact: General Secretariat | sg.ch
