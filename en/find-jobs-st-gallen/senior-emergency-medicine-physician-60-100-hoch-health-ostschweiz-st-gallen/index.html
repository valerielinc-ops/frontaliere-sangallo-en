Full description

Senior physician 60-100 %

Emergency Centre

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Care and management of emergencies in the largest emergency and HSM trauma center in the whole of Eastern Switzerland with a wide range of internistic/surgical disciplines
  • Shock room guidance in an interdisciplinary team and highly trained emergency care providers
  • Highly specialized emergency medical interventions such as NIV/HFNC, analgosedations, thoracic drapes, system of invasive blood pressure measurement, artery catheter, etc.
  • Diagnosis and therapy of acutely ill and vitally threatened patients for the entire breadth of interdisciplinary emergency medicine
  • Supervision and professional support of assistant doctors of various specialist clinics

What you bring for this job

  • Completed specialist title (internal medicine, anesthesiology, surgery or orthopaedics)
  • ISP Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR or motivation to acquire
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Completed ACLS and ATLS courses and in-depth ultrasound knowledge are beneficial
  • Motivation for cooperation in the interdisciplinary team

Our offer to you

  • Very varied work in an exceptionally wide range of emergency medicine
  • Recognized, strengthened emergency medicine with a lot of competence and responsibility since realignment in 2025 as well as participation in the further development to NFZ 2.0
  • Collegial collaboration in the team of 15 emergency medical professionals in a city with charm, culture and a high quality of life
  • Structured introduction as well as continuous support and promotion of SGNOR learning objectives by an experienced cadre team
  • Ultrasound diagnostics with structured competence building and broad development opportunities as well as opportunity to participate in research projects
  • Continuous professional training and development with great learning potential as well as advantages and attractive offers for employees

Your application

  • Contact us and ask us – you will be surprised how comprehensive and specialized emergency medicine is understood in St. Gallen
  • Entry by immediate or by agreement
  • At your request, temporary assignments at our emergency station are also possible.

  • For questions about the application process, please contact Lucius Graf, Team Leader HR Consulting Clinics, telephone +41 71 494 90 85
