Full description

We create perspectives for people in a responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. Saxerriet is an open prison for men with a total of 135 seats. It has differentiated implementation concepts and a wide range of workplaces.

What to expect

  • Responsibility for human, technical and organisational management of the implementation area
  • Ensuring a legally compliant, humane and orderly penal system, including compliance with forms of detention, performance and duty of care towards detainees
  • Responsibility for reporting and coordination of enforcement, in particular entry and exit planning and associated organisational processes
  • Active participation in the topics of enforcement planning, case management, implementation of enforcement and ROS regulations and forms of therapy
  • As a member of the management, jointly responsible for the proper operation, security and strategic orientation of the institution
  • Responsibility for the technical education and training of the team, active participation in the training of new employees and support in projects

What sets it apart

  • Completed tertiary education in any of the fields of law, psychology or social sciences
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the prison or in a comparable field
  • Project experience desirable, leadership experience beneficial
  • Analytical thinking, motivational ability, assertiveness, organizational and negotiating skills
  • Strong oral and written communication skills
  • In a demanding environment, you act resiliently and calmly
  • Teamwork and collaboration are very important to you
  • Very good knowledge of German and good oral knowledge in at least one foreign language

What we offer

  • A professional and motivated team
  • Modern infrastructure and contemporary employment conditions
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Various training offers
