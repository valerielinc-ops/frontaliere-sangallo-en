23.11.2026 Mels

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Sarganserland, a successful and locally anchored bank, we are looking for a versatile and committed personality as an employee management office (m/f/d). In this varied function, exciting tasks await you in the areas of HR, young talent development, marketing and sales.

What awaits you?

  • Ensure professional HR administration throughout the entire employee lifecycle.
  • Accompaniment of our learners and young talents in their professional and personal development.
  • Development and implementation of marketing, communication and sales activities across all channels.
  • Planning and support for customer events, sponsorship projects and the continuous development of a convincing market presence.
  • Active support and coordination of sales activities as well as participation in campaigns for new and existing customer acquisition.

What do you bring?

  • Banking training, ideally supplemented by further training in HR, marketing or business administration.
  • Enthusiasm for the development of young people
  • Broad interest in people, marketing and sales topics as well as the willingness to assume responsibility in different areas of responsibility.
  • Strong organisational skills, networked thinking and a high level of initiative.
  • Winning personality with a secure appearance, communication skills and pleasure in dealing with employees, customers and external partners.

Do you like working with people and feel comfortable in a versatile area of responsibility between HR, young talent development, sales support and marketing? Then we look forward to learning more about you. Anja Lötscher Head of Management Office Why Raiffeisen?

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
