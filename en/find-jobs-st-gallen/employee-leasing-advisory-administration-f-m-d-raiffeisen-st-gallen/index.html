Full description

Our dedicated team in St. Gallen supports our corporate customers, suppliers, vendors and Raiffeisenbanks with a lot of know-how and team spirit. Together we ensure smooth processes and individual solutions in the leasing business, with a focus on quality, service and cooperative partnership.

In order to strengthen our leasing department, we are looking for a motivated and reliable personality at the St. Gallen location as an employee Leasing Advisory & Administration (f/m/d). Tasks: • Sales-oriented support of regional leasing teams with a focus on Romandie • Telephone advice for business customers, suppliers, vendors and Raiffeisen banks • Preparation of lease quotes and contract documents and examination of incoming applications • Ensuring full customer documentation and regulatory compliance • Agile support across the entire leasing department (credit verification, accounting and sales) Requirements • A completed commercial or banking education • Ideally, first experience in credit or leasing • A structured, forward-looking and independent working method • A good understanding of regulatory requirements such as the Money Laundering Act (GwG) • Very good German and French skills in word and writing are required We offer: The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.
