Tasks

During your teaching

During your 4-year apprenticeship, you will find an extremely versatile and interesting apprenticeship in which you will gain insight into the complex and exciting computer science field of Raiffeisen:

  • The first year of apprenticeship is spent externally in the basic apprenticeship year in St. Gallen.
  • Afterwards, you will go through different departments of Raiffeisen Switzerland and work a lot with customers.
  • You set up computers, servers and networks and keep them running.
  • You search for and fix problems with computers, networks or other IT systems.
  • You install and update hardware and software.
  • You work with modern technologies such as cloud services and IT security.
