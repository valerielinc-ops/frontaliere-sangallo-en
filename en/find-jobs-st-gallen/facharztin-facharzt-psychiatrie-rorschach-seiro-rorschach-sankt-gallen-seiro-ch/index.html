Full description

Psychiatric-psychosomatic medicine thrives on real relationship work, and this is exactly what you will find here. For an outpatient special clinic in Rorschach, Kt. SG, we are looking for a specialist for psychiatry and psychotherapy in senior medical function in the amount of 80 – 100%. You take on a central role in outpatient psychiatric psychosomatic treatment and bear responsibility for your patients. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They conduct indication discussions including medical history, psychopathological findings and diagnosis after ICD-10 / ICD-11
  • You take on self-responsible psychiatric and psychotherapeutic individual treatments including case management
  • You have discussions with relatives, case managers and other involved bodies
  • They create medical histories, exit reports and actuarial assessments
  • They work closely together in the interdisciplinary team of the clinic

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Experience in outpatient psychiatric psychosomatic treatment
  • Enjoying self-responsible case management and interdisciplinary cooperation

That sets them apart.

  • Engaged, responsible personality with pleasure in collegial cooperation
  • Holistic understanding of psychiatric-psychosomatic patient care
  • Careful, structured working methods in documentation and reporting

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Recognized institution for further training in psychiatry and psychotherapy with structured support in specialist training
  • Regular intervisions as well as external and internal supervision
  • Diverse internal training opportunities
  • Interdisciplinary, growing team at a modern outpatient location

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
