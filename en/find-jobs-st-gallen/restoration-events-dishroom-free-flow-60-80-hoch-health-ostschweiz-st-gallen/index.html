Restoration & Events - Dishroom & Free Flow 60-80 %

Department of Real Estate & Operation, Hospitality Management

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Cooperation in the kitchen as well as ensuring the cleaning and cleanliness of all restorations and associated work areas
  • Acceptance of goods, physical inspection and loading of the restorations and free flow areas with foodstuffs and operating materials
  • Support in free flow, replenishment of the offer and general preparation and follow-up work
  • Collaboration in banquets, caterings and other events as well as support of the team in extraordinary work

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training in the catering or hospitality sector (EBA/EFZ) or several years of professional experience in the community catering or hospitality sector
  • Readiness for operations from Monday to Sunday in early, medium and late services
  • Reliable, resilient and team-oriented personality with an independent and careful way of working
  • Enjoyment of a service-oriented activity as well as good oral German skills

Our offer to you

  • A varied job in a modern hospital with a motivated and appreciative team
  • Structured training and attractive internal and external training and development opportunities
  • Progressive employment conditions, attractive employee benefits and reduced food
  • A secure workplace with long-term prospects in an innovative healthcare company
