Full description

Start your HR career at Migros! You can expect a supportive, committed and young team. Together we live the motto "Tämä Erfolgrich": You take over the HR administration, help in recruiting and actively shape processes. You have the willingness to go the extra mile – and don’t worry, we go it together. Become part of the Migros world and grow with us!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Gossau headquarters, Industriestrasse 47, 9200 Gossau

Mansioni

  • As the first point of contact for our employees and managers, you support them with their questions and concerns in written and telephone contact (1st Level Support)
  • You take over the administration of the HR lifecycle – from entry to exit. Carefully create and manage all documents and ensure smooth processes
  • In the area of recruiting, you help with the publication of job advertisements and pre-selection
  • You optimize and develop the HR administration processes further

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a commercial environment
  • Completed basic or further training: commercial vocational apprenticeship with vocational degree or study
  • You are a reliable, committed and inquisitive personality
  • Networked thinking, a high quality of work and an overview in stressful situations are among your strengths
  • Experience in direct customer contact is just as much a part of your profile as a strong IT affinity
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Social benefits: Very good social benefits in the areas of child allowances, sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Sports and fitness offers: discount on subscriptions to leisure and fitness facilities
  • Working atmosphere: More than a team
