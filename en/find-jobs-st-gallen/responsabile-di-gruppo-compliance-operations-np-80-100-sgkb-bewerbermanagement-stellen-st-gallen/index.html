Full description

Do you want to lead a team of five as Group Leader* in Compliance Operations NP at the headquarters in St. Gallen and help shape the further development of our operational compliance area? Our compliance department repositioned itself in September 2026. Since then, the Compliance Operations team has bundled the operational topics into three groups: Compliance Operations NP (natural persons), JP (legal persons) and Risk Reviews. With your experience, you ensure efficient and high-quality processes.

This is what you will do.

  • You lead and develop a group with currently five employees.
  • The quality and efficiency of customer opening, mutation and adjustment processes is your responsibility. You support your team with technical questions.
  • You ensure that legal and regulatory due diligence obligations and our internal instructions are complied with.
  • When dealing with demanding compliance special cases, you bring your technical expertise and find solutions for complex issues.
  • You critically question existing processes, drive improvements and participate in cross-divisional projects.

You bring that with you

  • A completed banking apprenticeship with subject-specific further education or a completed study, preferably in law or business administration, forms your professional basis.
  • You bring in-depth experience in the management of customer master data, know the processing processes of customer openings and stock mutations and have already gained management experience in a comparable function.
  • You are very familiar with the regulatory requirements and standards such as GwG, VSB, AIA, FATCA and QI.
  • A structured, precise and independent way of working distinguishes you as well as your ability to make informed decisions and communicate clearly. You convince with discretion and team orientation.
  • You recognize potential for improvement, question existing processes constructively and implement optimizations on your own initiative and purposefully.

Information Florian Luck, Head of Compliance Operations, telephone 071 231 33 04, is happy to answer your questions.
