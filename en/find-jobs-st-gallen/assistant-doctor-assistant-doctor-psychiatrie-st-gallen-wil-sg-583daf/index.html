Your tasks

After a well-founded familiarization, as a member of the interdisciplinary team, you independently take on medical-psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment tasks for adults with mental health problems, primarily in individual setting, if you are interested in couple, family or group setting, under regular supervision.

Main responsibilities

  • After a well-founded familiarization, as a member of the interdisciplinary team, you independently take on medical-psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment tasks for adults with mental health problems, primarily in individual setting, if you are interested in couple, family or group setting, under regular supervision.
  • In your day-to-day work, you have the opportunity to work in the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and to work closely with the other departments and assignment offices.
  • You plan therapies individually, document them and write medical reports.
  • You will take over medical services and consiliar examinations of patients in the hospitals Grab and Walenstadt. Requirements
  • You have completed medical studies and ideally have two years of experience in inpatient psychiatry.
  • You have a professional training permit from the Canton of St.Gallen or meet the requirements to obtain it before starting your job.
  • You strive for the specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy and have an advanced or already completed federally recognized psychotherapeutic training.
  • Ideally, you have already completed the foreign year.
  • You maintain an open, communicative approach to patients and team members.
  • You are characterized by a high commitment, self-initiative and very good knowledge of German (at least C1).
