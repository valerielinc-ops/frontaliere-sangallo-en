Full description

When Köttbullar meets Tilsammans – then you are at IKEA.

You love our legendary food, even if you saw it every day? Then the job at IKEA Food could be interesting for you. People work better when they are well. At IKEA, we believe that challenges bring joy when you have fun at work and achieve goals together. We want you to feel comfortable with us and to be able to develop personally and professionally.

How do you see it? See if this suits you:

  • Ideally, you have experience in system catering, logistics or retail and are physically resilient.
  • You are sure to communicate in German or English and are motivated to learn German. Improve your German skills.
  • You enjoy working in a dynamic and fast-paced environment and take on logistical tasks around food with pleasure.
  • You are disciplined, flexible and enjoy working in a team.
  • You are open-minded, communicative and promote a positive cooperation with your colleagues with your friendly nature.
  • You work efficiently in a team and at the same time take responsibility for your own tasks.
  • You are ready to work on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 07:30 to 15:30 as well as an additional assignment day if necessary.

Your responsibilities

  • Acceptance and control of food and goods for the entire food sector (restaurant, cuisine, bistro and Swedenshop).
  • Checking delivery notes and ensuring product quality at receipts of goods.
  • Professional storage and supply of products according to the FIFO principle.
  • Working according to established routines to ensure stock accuracy and availability of products for our guests and customers.
  • Filling and presenting food products in the various sales and guest areas of the store.
  • Careful, accurate and safe work to ensure the safety of people, products and workflows in the area of responsibility.
  • Willingness to support in the restaurant, bistro, Swedenshop or in the kitchen.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
