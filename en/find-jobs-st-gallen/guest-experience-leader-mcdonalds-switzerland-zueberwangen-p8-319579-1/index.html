Description of post

As the Customer Experience Leader, you are the sunlight of our restaurant. You welcome guests with a warm smile, present them with our latest offers and help them place their order. You inject your positive energy into your team and ensure that each customer goes back to being satisfied. You notice the small details that make all the difference and are always listening to the wishes and returns of our customers, which you share with the management to make every visit even better.

Key responsibilities · Welcome customers and create a joyful atmosphere · Present new offers and assist in ordering · Share customer feedback with team and management · Support your team with enthusiasm and team spirit · Ensure every guest has a perfect experience at the restaurant

Qualifications required · A radiant and open personality · The passion to make people happy · Team spirit and reliability · Sense of detail and listening capacity · Motivation to learn and progress — no experience required!
