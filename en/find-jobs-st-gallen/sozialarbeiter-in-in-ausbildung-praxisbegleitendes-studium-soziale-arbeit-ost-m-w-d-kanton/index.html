Full description

We create perspectives for people in a self-responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. The Department of Probation Assistance accompanies adults during criminal proceedings, after convictions and provides probation assistance. She leads the counselling centre Domestic Violence, is the Electronic Monitoring Office and offers the learning programme for those who practice violence.

What to expect

  • They support clients from pre-trial detention until completion of the ordered probationary period of probationary assistance
  • They advise and accompany clients in their social integration in the areas of living, work, therapy, etc., develop finances for them and work with you on their debt situation
  • You operate active victim protection by processing the offence with the clients according to methodological specifications
  • You work with us in the further listed tasks of probation assistance

What sets it apart

  • You have completed vocational training and/or a maturity (vocational maturity, specialist maturity or gymnasium maturity)
  • You would like to complete the practical study Bachelor of Science FH OST in Social Work or change to practical work
  • Ideally, they already have their first practical experience in the social field
  • They have good social skills in relationship design and communication. Furthermore, you are ready to further develop these as part of your training
  • They enjoy working in a team and at the same time have an independent and reliable way of working
  • You have stylish German in word and writing. Further knowledge of foreign languages, especially French and English, is an advantage

What we offer

  • Varied, demanding activity with interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Meaningful task in a socially important context
  • High degree of independence, scope for action and design
  • Interesting training and education opportunities
