Full description

For the department of quantitative risk modelling with location in St. Gallen and the possibility to work partly at Zurich Airport (The Circle) as well as in the home office, we are looking for a highly motivated and talented person of the company, who strengthens our team with passion and expertise!

Tasks

  • Further development and backtesting of quantitative PD/LGD risk models (in particular in the IB environment)
  • Implementation of peptide analyses and support for rating conversions
  • Analysis and interpretation of risk data and derivation of sound recommendations for action
  • Close cooperation with departments, IT and regulatory interfaces
  • Hands-on support for monitoring and for model changes

Requirements

  • Master's degree in mathematics, statistics, finance or related quantitative fields
  • At least 2 years of experience in quantitative risk modelling, ideally in the IB context
  • A well-defined understanding of risk and analytical thinking
  • Very good knowledge of R, SQL and Git are of benefit
  • High personal responsibility, structured and solution-oriented working methods with hands-on mentality in the team
  • Very good German skills are required

We offer the work-life balance of our employees is important to us. Therefore, we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time pensions.
