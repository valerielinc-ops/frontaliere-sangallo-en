Full description

30.09.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

We are responsible for strategic and financial banking management issues and participate in Group-wide strategic projects. Strongly interdisciplinary, we also act as a central coordination and support unit in dealing with shareholdings and subsidiaries. In order to strengthen our team based in St. Gallen (with the possibility of home office), we are looking for a committed and conceptually experienced personality as strategic consultant (m/f/d) for strategic controlling topics.

What awaits you?

  • Cooperation in strategic projects and M&A topics
  • Further development of the instruments of investment controlling as well as independent preparation of analyses and reporting on subsidiaries and investments of Raiffeisen
  • Conceptual processing of specialist topics for strategic and financial bank management as well as in the context of emergency planning
  • Close cooperation with internal and external specialists

What do you bring?

  • Successfully completed business education (University/FH)
  • At least 3 years of experience in strategy and financial controlling, beneficial in the banking environment
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Ability to prepare complex issues appropriate to the addressee and to represent them professionally convincingly
  • High initiative, flexible use in various topics and well-versed in dealing with office products

“You think connected and act straight ahead?” Are you more interested in solutions than problems? I look forward to meeting you. Roland Swiss Head of Strategy Controlling, Raiffeisen Switzerland Why Raiffeisen?
