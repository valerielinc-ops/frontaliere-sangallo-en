Full description

MAIN TASKS

As Quality Claims Manager in the field of installation and washing systems (IFS), you ensure group-wide, professional complaint processing in a dynamic, international environment. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Independent processing of cases which are escalated by sales incl. complex cases with a legal background, group-wide significance and involved insurance with the involvement of internal and external specialists, which are escalated by other plants
  • Support all sales companies in the operational handling of customer complaints (create and process 8D reports and technical statements)
  • Validation of the effectiveness of countermeasures as part of the 8D report at suppliers and plants
  • Supporting markets through quality management claims services and proactively monitoring quality performance and actively initiating improvement measures where necessary
  • Active knowledge development and transfer from the area of quality management claims to the service personnel and customer service managers of the sales companies and the internal development teams

PROFILE

You bring a degree as a sanitary installer. In addition, the following points distinguish you:

  • Experience in project management
  • Further training in quality methods is advantageous
  • Business knowledge and legal interest (specific to product liability, safety laws, etc.) is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (word and writing)

You also have system competences to assess overall contexts, communicate decisively and clearly under time pressure and see travel (5-10%) as an enrichment? Then this is the right function for you!

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
