PROFILE

  • Completed studies in mechanical engineering, vehicle engineering or comparable training

  • Support of the project phases (procurement, production, commissioning, type tests, passenger operation) of the vehicles
  • Cooperation with customers, suppliers and approval bodies in an international environment
