Full description

As a Wheat & Rye Process Commissioning Engineer, you’ll dive deep into the heart of the global grain and food business. You start up state-of-the-art milling plants worldwide, experience different countries and cultures, are in close contact with our customers, pass on your expertise in training and thus directly contribute to the fact that mills around the world produce efficiently, safely and in the best quality.

Tasks

  • They independently commission new machines and milling plants worldwide and ensure their successful transfer to our customers.
  • They train and advise our customers and their employees and impart technological and process know-how in a practical manner
  • They perform service and maintenance work on existing machinery and equipment
  • Create detailed process diagrams under guidance with AutoCAD

Requirements

  • Completed training as a grain miller:in or process technologist:in the mill and feed industry; several years of experience in the operation and maintenance of grain mills is an advantage
  • Basic knowledge of electrical engineering, affinity for automation and routine use of digital work equipment such as Microsoft 365
  • Strong customer and service orientation, personal responsibility as well as openness and joy in dealing with different cultures and people
  • Good knowledge of German and English (minimum B1) as well as convincing communication skills in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial
  • High flexibility and willingness to travel worldwide (>60%); in case of positive development, there is the possibility of a financed further education at the Swiss Müllereifachschule

With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of reconciling the needs of the economy, people and nature.
