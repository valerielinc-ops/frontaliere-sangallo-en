Full description

Technology. Advice. Impact. Your job in Eastern Switzerland.

Do you have a flair for the technical advice of customers on large projects and various problems? Do you want to work in a fast-paced environment where you can make a difference? Do you enjoy working in a team that performs outstandingly every day and has a passion for innovative solutions? Then you are the ideal reinforcement! The job is possible in both 100% and job sharing with 50-70% per person.

Your profile

  • Completed studies (FH, ETH) in the field of civil engineering
  • high self-motivation, commitment and goal orientation
  • Readiness to learn and the ability to convey complex technical content in an understandable way
  • Communication strength and high team orientation
  • Affinity and enthusiasm for consulting with customers
  • very good knowledge of German and English
  • Ideally, first professional experience as an engineer:in, project manager:in, construction manager:in or in the planning office (alternatively in the form of internships during study)
