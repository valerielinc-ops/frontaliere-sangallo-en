Full description

We create perspectives for people in a responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. The Altstätten Regional Prison has 45 places for remand and security detention and prison. The regional prison will be expanded to 126 places.

What to expect

  • A responsible, varied leadership position with three subject areas
  • Meaningful, interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external partners, in a demanding environment
  • As a member of the prison management the opportunity to shape the future of the regional prison
  • A functioning prison with imminent growth to three times the size, built to modern standards

What sets it apart

  • Completed training in social work HF/FH or care HF/FH or equivalent training
  • Many years of professional experience and several years of management experience
  • In addition to your expertise, you bring a large portion of creative will and maker mentality
  • High flexibility, resilience and stress resistance as well as risk-oriented thinking and responsible action

What we offer

  • An environment characterized by trust and openness, supported by a contemporary infrastructure
  • Varied and demanding work in an exciting, socially relevant topic area
  • Contemporary employment conditions and the opportunity for personal and professional education and training

Contact: For further information, Silvio Fausch, Co-Head of Prison, telephone 058 229 64 40, is at your disposal.
