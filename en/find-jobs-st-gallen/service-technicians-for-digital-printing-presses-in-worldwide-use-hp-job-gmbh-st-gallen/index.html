Full description

For our customer in 9016 St. Gallen we are looking for you for a permanent job with 100% effort. You get digital label printing machines up and running at customers worldwide. Your assignments take 1-4 weeks each. Your tasks

  • Install the printing presses mechanically and electrically in Switzerland and abroad and put them into operation.
  • Wait for the equipment, find the causes of faults and repair defective components.
  • Check the customer’s printing requirements and optimize the printing process.
  • Create and update training materials and integrate the customer’s employees into the service.
  • Document your work and write troubleshooting and repair instructions.

You bring that with you

  • Training in automation, mechatronics or electronics
  • Practice in regulation and control engineering
  • Experience in international customer service or motivation to move to this area
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Readiness for worldwide assignments of 1-4 weeks each
  • You work independently, bring your own ideas and seek pragmatic solutions.

That is why it is worth it.

  • You are responsible for digital printing presses, from commissioning to repair.
  • You will receive a market-based wage and attractive social benefits.
  • You have a 40-hour week and at least five weeks of vacation. The GAV Swissmem applies.
  • You can use digital learning opportunities and receive support for professional development.
  • You can participate in cultural events and events for employees.
