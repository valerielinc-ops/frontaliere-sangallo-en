Role overview

As a middleware engineer, you design the interfaces that connect our applications and services. You work in a dynamic environment with technologically exciting tasks. We don't just focus on your professional skills - what's most important to us is how we work together as a team, learn from each other and support each other.

To strengthen our Middleware & Integration team in St. Gallen and ZH Airport (Circle), we are looking for a communicative and solution-oriented personality as a Middleware Platform Engineer (m/f/d) immediately or by appointment.

Tasks

  • Engineering, operation and further development of the middleware platform (Kafka, Message, Call and File)
  • Supporting developers in integrating applications
  • Collaboration with experts from network, storage, OS and databases

Requirements

  • Completed training in computer science or comparable professional experience
  • At least 1 year of experience with Kafka, ideally with Red Hat Streams on OpenShift
  • Sound experience in dealing with APIs (design, integration and operation)
  • Proficiency in using enterprise operating systems such as AIX, Linux and Windows
  • Knowledge of Kotlin, Java and Argo CD in conjunction with Helm is an advantage
  • Engineering, operation and further development of the middleware platform (Kafka, Message, Call and File)
