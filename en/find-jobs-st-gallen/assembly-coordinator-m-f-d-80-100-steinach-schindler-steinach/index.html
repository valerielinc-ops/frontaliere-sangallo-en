AS Elevators is a Swiss partner for the assembly, modernization and maintenance of elevators. The diversity and individualization of our products characterizes us. Be as unique as we are!

Experience the AS Spirit as

Assembly coordinator (m/f/d) 80-100% - Steinach

Through your start at AS Elevators you benefit from an appreciative and family environment. In this way, we promote cohesion among employees through our du-culture and various events. Find out more about us here: Video

What to expect

As an assembly coordinator, you are the central interface between customers, installers, assembly management, sales and suppliers. You ensure smooth order processing, coordinate construction site deployments and ensure that appointments, materials and resources are optimally coordinated.

  • You independently coordinate assembly orders from scheduling to successful handover and ensure professional and timely handling.
  • You plan the assignments of installers, subcontractors and third-party companies as well as the material deliveries to the construction sites.
  • You are the central contact for customers and partners and ensure proactive communication during assembly starts, delays and elevator transfers.
  • You handle complaints, coordinate material returns and support the assembly manager in finding solutions.
  • You create construction programs, maintain internal assembly information and ensure a balanced utilization of assembly capacities.

What you bring

  • You have basic technical training in the electrotechnical or mechanical field
  • Elevator knowledge is advantageous.
  • You will bring experience in order planning and processing as well as a good mechanical and technical understanding.
  • You have commercial training and good MS-Office user knowledge; SAP knowledge is desirable.
  • You communicate safely in the local language
  • Knowledge of a second national language and English are beneficial.
  • You convince through organizational talent, service orientation, resilience as well as an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method.

  • Work-life balance: 40h week*, 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and company health management including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Compensation for your lunchtime.
  • Various employee benefits: generous participation in the public transport and fitness subscription, fleet discounts, reduced purchase of Reka money, reduced insurance premiums and various benefits.
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company.
