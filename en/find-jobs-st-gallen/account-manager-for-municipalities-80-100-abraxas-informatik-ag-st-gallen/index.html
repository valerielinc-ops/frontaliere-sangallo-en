Full description

You know the world of public administration – and want to help shape it? Then you are right with us. As an account manager: in communities, you are the central contact for our customers and accompany them in their digital development in the long term. You understand their challenges, recognize opportunities and bring the right solutions, specialists: people together. This is not about short-term sales. We want to understand our customers, create trust and jointly develop solutions that create real added value for public administration. Your role

  • You manage and develop your own portfolio of municipalities and cities and build long-term, trusting customer relationships
  • You analyze customer needs, identify potential and develop suitable marketing concepts
  • You understand the strategic and operational challenges of your customers: inside and accompany them as a sparring partner: in their digital development
  • You will recognize customer needs and potential and develop concrete sales opportunities for our solution and service portfolio.
  • You control the entire sales process from demand clarification and solution finding to offers and negotiations until successful conclusion
  • You bring together the right internal specialists and coordinate the cooperation between customers, departments and project teams
  • You keep an eye on contracts, sales development, opportunities and customer satisfaction and ensure reliable support

Your profile

  • You have experience in sales, account management, public administration, the IT environment or in a comparable customer-oriented function
  • You understand the needs and decision-making processes of municipalities and cities or have the motivation to quickly familiarize yourself with this environment
  • You communicate clearly, appreciatively and convincingly and move safely at different contact and decision levels
  • You enjoy building relationships, developing customers and attracting people to solutions
  • Ideally, you have an existing network or are well-versed in the administrative landscape
  • You bring a commercial, business or comparable education. Further training in sales, business administration or a related field is advantageous
  • You think entrepreneurially, recognize opportunities and pursue them with perseverance and commitment
  • You work independently and structured, but also appreciate the exchange and cooperation in the team

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Generous funding of further training in time or financially
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees

We maintain an open you culture – so feel free to send us your application directly in the you form.
