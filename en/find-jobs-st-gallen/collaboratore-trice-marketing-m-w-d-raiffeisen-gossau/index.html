14.10.2026 Gossau

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Gossau-Andwil-Niederwil stands for personal closeness and regional ties. As an employee marketing (m/f/d), you provide creative ideas, organizational skills and great pleasure in dealing with people so that our bank remains visible, tangible and approachable. From social media to sponsorship to events and campaigns, you’ll be involved in a wide range of tasks and contribute your own ideas.

What awaits you?

  • You support in the planning, implementation and further development of our marketing, communication and sales measures
  • You work at customer events, member events and sponsoring activities from the idea to implementation
  • You support our website and social media channels and develop them further
  • You design content and marketing materials for online and offline channels
  • You work with internal departments, agencies and external partners and contribute to a professional market presence
  • Help create positive customer experiences throughout the customer journey

What do you bring?

  • Commercial basic education or training in marketing, communication, media or a comparable environment
  • You enjoy working with people, communicating at eye level and getting others excited about joint projects
  • Enjoy organizing projects and coordinating different tasks at the same time.
  • Creativity, initiative and an independent working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and pleasure in writing comprehensible and appealing texts
  • Very good knowledge of MS-Office applications
  • Initial experience in marketing, communication or event areas is an advantage, but not a prerequisite
  • Knowledge of graphics, content or web tools (e.g. Adobe Creative Cloud, Adobe Experience Manager, Canva or similar) is a plus

Together we ensure that our bank is not only seen, but experienced. Whether on social media, at events or through local engagements: With creative ideas, we create experiences that remain in memory. Franziska Bless Marketing managers Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We offer flexible working models

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
