Full description

MAIN TASKS

In our building physics laboratory, the foundations for innovative and high-quality Geberit products are created. For the management of this competence center (acoustics, statics, fire protection) we are looking for a technically skilled and leadership-strong personality with a passion for building physics, innovation and interdisciplinary cooperation. The function as Head Building Physics Laboratory includes the following activities:

  • You run the construction physics laboratory in technical, business and personnel terms (3 employees)
  • You are responsible for the building physics know-how in the areas of acoustics, statics and fire protection and with your team you can safely provide professional measurements, tests and external fire tests including approvals
  • You actively drive innovation, bring new technologies and trends and make an important contribution to the further development of our products with innovation projects
  • As a competent contact person, you advise and support product development and sales in demanding construction physics issues
  • You prepare technical results to address and you provide the interface to internal and external stakeholders
  • You contact universities and institutes and support and accompany interns

PROFILE

For this demanding and versatile task, you will be awarded a degree in construction physics, civil engineering or physics or similar fields of study (Uni/ETH). You will also be distinguished by the following points:

  • You have several years of professional experience in building physics, ideally in the industrial environment
  • You think customer- and solution-oriented and find pragmatic and sustainable solutions for demanding questions
  • You bring innovation spirit and enjoy transferring new ideas and technologies into marketable solutions
  • You are happy to have a leadership role and have already successfully led a team
  • You communicate sovereignly on different levels and can also communicate complex technical contexts in a comprehensible and target group-oriented way
  • You communicate in German and English
