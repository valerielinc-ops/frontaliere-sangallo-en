Full description

Do you have a soft spot for the administrative handling of export transactions and would you like to assume responsibility in a dynamic environment? Then you are exactly right in our forwarding team in the field of Manufacturing and Logistics. In this versatile role, you ensure the smooth administrative handling of international transports of machines and spare parts and actively contribute to an efficient, timely and customer-oriented supply chain. Tasks:

  • You handle export and cross-trade shipments for machines and spare parts independently and customer-oriented
  • They manage defined country territories, take on special tasks in the forwarding team and communicate proactively with internal interfaces, international locations, authorities and transport service providers.
  • You create export-compliant shipping and transport documents and together with internal and external partners ensure correct import processing in the receiving country
  • You provide shipping instructions to carriers and logistics partners, monitor shipping dates and coordinate multimodal transports
  • They obtain transport offers, compare and evaluate them according to defined selection criteria and ensure compliance with all customs and export-relevant requirements. Requirements
  • Completed commercial training, ideally supplemented by further education or professional experience in forwarding, export or international logistics
  • Several years of experience in export processing and international transport administration as well as good knowledge in shipping documentation, international transports and administrative logistics processes
  • Knowledge of customs tariff, customs clearance or export control beneficial
  • Structured, independent and proactive personality with high customer and service orientation, communication strength and teamwork
