Your tasks and perspectives

  • Professional support, support and individual support for children and monitoring their development
  • Cross-group work in the three micro-child groups and three age-mixed groups with flexible cooperation in different tandems and teams
  • Designing an age-appropriate and need-oriented, varied day-care and cross-group activities
  • Confident cooperation with guardians
  • Implementation of the pedagogical concept as well as the quality, hygiene and safety standards

What you're doing for this job

  • Completed training as a specialist woman/man Support EFZ, subject area children, or equivalent training
  • Educational expertise and joy in working with children of different ages
  • Flexibility and joy in changing group and team constellations
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Extensive team and communication skills as well as empathy and professional attitude towards children and guardians

Our offer to you

  • Versatile 80% position with insight and cooperation in all six groups
  • Varied cooperation with different tandems and teams
  • A dedicated and supportive working environment
  • Space for initiative and pedagogical co-design
  • Professional and personal training opportunities
