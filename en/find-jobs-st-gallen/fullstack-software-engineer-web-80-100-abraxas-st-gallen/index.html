Full description

Do you enjoy developing modern web applications and want to create concrete solutions for different specialist areas through your work? In our Web Development team, we implement sophisticated custom and specialist applications – from the idea and conceptualization through to the development and ongoing enhancement of operational solutions.

As a Fullstack Software Engineer Web, you contribute your expertise to both the frontend and backend and play an active role in shaping our applications. You work closely with specialist departments, Product Owners and other development teams, and have the opportunity to use modern technologies and develop sustainable software solutions.

Your role

  • As a Software Engineer on the Web Development team, you design and implement custom software solutions
  • You use, among other things, .NET, Angular and Docker/Kubernetes to create and further develop high-performance, secure applications
  • You see yourself as part of a larger team that is responsible for a wide range of solutions and enjoy exchanging ideas
  • You are willing to engage with a wide range of subject areas not only technically but also from a specialist perspective

Your profile

  • You have several years of experience as a Fullstack Engineer
  • You are proficient in .NET and Angular and have knowledge of Continuous Integration and the associated tooling
  • Setting priorities and communicating directly with customers come naturally to you
  • You do not see AI as a threat, but as an opportunity
  • German (fluent, at least C2) and English (at least B1)

Your benefits

  • A great deal of scope to shape things and an environment where you can contribute your ideas
  • Up to 60 % working from home possible with a 100 % workload
  • Purchase of additional vacation (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours for a full-time position
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • 100 % financial or time support for continuing education
  • Free SBB Half-Fare Travelcard for all employees

We foster an open first-name culture – so feel free to send us your application directly using the informal form of address.
