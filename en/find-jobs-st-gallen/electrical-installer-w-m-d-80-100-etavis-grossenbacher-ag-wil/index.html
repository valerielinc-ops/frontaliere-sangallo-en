About us

ETAVIS Grossenbacher AG is one of the leading companies in the Swiss electrical installation and telecommunications market in Eastern Switzerland. In order to continue to maintain our high standard, we are looking for a customer-oriented, flexible and committed person like you!

The branch in Wil is strongly anchored regionally and guarantees optimal service around the clock. The offering is comprehensive and ranges from consulting, through installation to commissioning.

Tasks

  • Execution of electrical systems in industry and commerce, new installations on electrical and telecommunications systems as well as maintenance work and repairs on existing systems
  • Checking installations, carrying out measurements and tests, finding and rectifying possible causes of faults or errors.
  • Passing on your in-depth technical knowledge to our apprentices.

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as electrician installer EFZ or equivalent qualification recognized in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience
  • Strong quality awareness, representative appearance and independent way of working

  • With us you have diverse further education opportunities – we are happy to support you.

This is us

ETAVIS is the optimal partner for innovative electrical engineering, communication and automation solutions in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. We offer tailor-made, high-quality solutions for business customers of all industries and sizes as well as individually tailored services for private customers.
