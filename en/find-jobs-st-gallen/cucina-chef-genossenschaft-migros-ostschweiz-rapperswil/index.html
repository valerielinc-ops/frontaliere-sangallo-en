Full description

Directly on Lake Zurich, we operate the gastronomy of a university of applied sciences with around 600 guests per day. In addition, we organize varied caterings and events for about six months a year, from exclusive dinners to large events with up to 1,500 people. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated personality as a cook.

Luogo di lavoro: Community gastronomy, GG OST Rapperswil, Oberseestrasse 10, 8640 Rapperswil

Mansioni

  • You are passionate about preparing hot and cold food as well as seasonal specialties and you work with our various catering and special events (exclusive to large-volume)
  • You are responsible for the co-creation of food offers and buffets and ensure quality, presentation and hygiene
  • You support in goods management and Mise en Place
  • You are actively working with kitchen & service

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further training: in gastronomy
  • You love being in touch with people and enjoy getting our guests excited
  • Fresh foods are just your thing – you know how to put them perfectly on stage
  • You are a team player, flexible and ready to go anywhere
  • Independent and accurate work is a matter of course for you
  • You always want to develop and learn new things
  • German (very good knowledge)
