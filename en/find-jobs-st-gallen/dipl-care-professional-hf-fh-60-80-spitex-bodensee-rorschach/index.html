Spitex Bodensee is a modern, regionally anchored Spitex operation. We look after the inhabitants of six municipalities and work with dedication, expertise and heart for high-quality care every day. Care takes more than routine. With us you work in a clearly structured environment with responsibility in the care process and close exchange in the team. Care with responsibility. Directly to the people.

In order to strengthen our care team, we are looking for a committed personality as Dipl. Care professional HF/FH 60–80 % Your tasks

  • Cooperation with doctors, therapists and relatives
  • Support and guidance of employees in everyday life

Your commitment
