Inspector:in monitoring road 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Inspector monitoring the road Rapperswil SG, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In the Infrastructure division, you will help shape the backbone of Swiss mobility. You ensure that trains run safely, on time and reliably across the country. In this varied and responsible role, you will make a direct contribution to rail safety. A well-rehearsed, collegial team looks forward to welcoming you. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • You carry out system and in-office inspections in the area of ​​road systems on your own responsibility and document your results using the available tools.
  • With your technical flair, you carry out standardized measurement work on track and switch systems, independently or in a team, and ensure precise protocols in the systems.
  • You take the necessary immediate measures and report safety-related defects immediately so that train traffic remains safe.
  • You support the technical assistant in planning and carrying out route inspections as well as in evaluating and analyzing the results. Your observations are crucial for sustainable solutions.
  • Assignments as head of security and occasionally as a security guard complement your varied area of ​​responsibility. This is what you bring with you.
  • You work independently, reliably and take on responsibility. Safety is your top priority.
  • You are characterized by organizational talent, initiative and flexibility.
  • You have several years of practical experience in the field of road systems and know the challenges on site.
  • You are experienced in dealing with IT applications and are open to further expanding your knowledge. - You have completed an apprenticeship in traffic infrastructure construction/civil engineering as well as a driving license Category B. Further professional training to become a foreman, foreman or chief fitter completes your profile.
