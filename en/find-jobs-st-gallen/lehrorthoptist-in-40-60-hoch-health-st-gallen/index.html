Orthoptist/in 40-60 %

Eye Clinic, Department of Strabology and Neuroophthalmology

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Extensive clarification and care of patients with interesting clinical pictures from the broad orthoptic and neuroophthalmological spectrum
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation and regular internal training
  • Accompanying the students Orthoptik HF in learning practical examinations, recognizing clinical pictures and dealing with patients
  • Possibility for further training as a practical trainer (SVEB certificate)

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Orthoptist/in
  • At least 2 years of professional experience
  • Solid, high level of expertise in the field of orthotics and neuroophthalmology and enjoyment of the challenge of complex clinical pictures
  • Friendly and patient treatment of patients of all ages and cultures
  • High motivation to share your knowledge

Our offer to you

  • Family cooperation in a highly specialized, interdisciplinary environment
  • Future-oriented and innovative company
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various employee offers in the area of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
