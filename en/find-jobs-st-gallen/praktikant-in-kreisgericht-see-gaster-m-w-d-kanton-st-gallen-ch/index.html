Full description

The See-Gaster District Court in Uznach offers two internships for trained lawyers. Internships usually last one year and start on April 1 and October 1.

What to expect

  • Insight into the judicial decision-making activities of a court of first instance
  • Participation in civil, SchKG and criminal matters
  • Preparation of presentations
  • Recording of negotiations with an advisory voice in decision-making
  • Preparation of single judge and collegiate court decisions

What sets it apart

  • Completed law degree (Master)
  • Independent and precise work
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Discretion

What we offer

  • Internship as preparation for the examination for the acquisition of the St.Gallian bar patent
  • interesting and responsible activity
  • Various insights into court practice
  • Attractive, central workplace
  • Collegial team
