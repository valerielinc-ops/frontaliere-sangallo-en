Full description

We create perspectives for people in a responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. In the Bitzi Action Center, criminal measures for adult men are carried out on two closed and three open care departments as well as an external housing group.
