Full description

For our customer in 9016 St. Gallen we are looking for you for a permanent job with 100% effort. You help customers around the world troubleshoot conventional label printing machines. The team works in two shifts and manages the fault number six days a week around the clock. Your tasks

  • Border machine malfunctions on the phone and in writing and help customers and service technicians resolve them.
  • Maintain fault messages from the first contact to the solution and record the measures taken in SAP.
  • Determine which spare parts are necessary for the repair.
  • Write troubleshooting and repair instructions and create training materials for customers and internal teams.
  • If necessary, help directly with the customer on site at short notice.

You bring that with you

  • Training in automation, mechatronics or electronics
  • Experience in technical service or interest in entering this area
  • Knowledge of control, drive and control technology
  • Good user knowledge in Microsoft Office and SAP
  • Readiness for two-shift operation and piquetting services
  • You communicate safely in German and English with people from different countries.
  • You work independently and in a team, take the initiative and stay calm even in tricky disturbances.

That is why it is worth it.

  • You are responsible for various technical cases.
  • You will receive a market-based wage and attractive social benefits.
  • You work 40 hours a week and have at least five weeks of vacation. The GAV Swissmem applies.
  • You can use digital learning opportunities. The company participates in professional training.
  • You can participate in cultural events and events for employees.
