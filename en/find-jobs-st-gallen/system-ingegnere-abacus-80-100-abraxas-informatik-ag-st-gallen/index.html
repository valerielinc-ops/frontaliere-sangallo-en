Full description

Are you passionate about IT infrastructures, systems and the secure operation of business software? Would you like to actively support customers and ensure that their Abacus environment remains stable, secure and always up to date? Then you have come to the right place.

Your role

  • You ensure the secure, stable and high-performance operation of our customers' Abacus systems
  • The installation, configuration and maintenance of the system landscape are your responsibility
  • You plan and implement release upgrades, updates and extensions from preparation through successful commissioning
  • By implementing patches, hotfixes and security updates, as well as conducting comprehensive tests, you ensure smooth operation
  • As a competent contact person, you advise our customers on the performance, security and further development of their Abacus environment and work closely with internal and external stakeholders

Your profile

  • Completed IT training (EFZ, HF, FH or equivalent)
  • Practical experience in operating Windows server environments
  • Very good knowledge of Active Directory, networking and virtualization
  • Experience with Abacus or comparable ERP solutions
  • Independent, structured and customer-oriented personality
  • Very good German language skills, both spoken and written, required (C2 level)

Your benefits

  • Up to 60 % working from home possible with a 100 % workload
  • Purchase of vacation days (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h workweek with flexible working hours for a full-time position
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • Generous support for continuing education, either through time or financial assistance
  • Free SBB Half-Fare Travelcard for all employees

We foster an open, informal culture – so feel free to send us your application using the informal form of address.
