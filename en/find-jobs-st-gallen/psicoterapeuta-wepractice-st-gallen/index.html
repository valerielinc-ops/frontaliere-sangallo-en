Full description

You are as individual as the people you accompany. That's why we create an environment at eye level that offers you therapeutic freedom and a flexible working model

  • so that you can organize your professional and private life according to your needs. WePractice is a leading outpatient practice group with over 140 psychotherapists at 17 locations and part of the Medbase Group. Mansioni
  • Work in a friendly practice and benefit from the individual support of an experienced site manager
  • regular case discussions and supervision are a given.
  • Accompany your patients in outpatient psychotherapy – flexible in terms of time and location.
  • Decide for yourself who you work with. We help you build a patient base that fits your specialization.
  • Develop individual therapy plans, take anamnesis and make diagnoses
  • our practice software developed especially for you gives you more time for the essentials.
  • Requisiti
  • First professional experience: in psychotherapy
  • Completed basic or further training: Federally recognized psychotherapist
  • You meet all the requirements to be able to bill for basic insurance in Switzerland
  • Enjoy working independently
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • Cosa offriamo
  • Central place of work: Our practice rooms are warm and modern
  • a place where you and your patients feel comfortable.
  • Individual further training: We accompany you with supervision, case discussions, development discussions and further training time/budget.
  • Flat hierarchies: We are a young team that works together with fresh ideas and a lot of drive.
  • Freedom of design and decision-making: Choose your patients freely and design the therapy approach so that it fits the care pragmatically and sensibly.
  • Mobile-flexible working model: Carry out therapies/administrative tasks from home and arrange your working hours as they suit you.
