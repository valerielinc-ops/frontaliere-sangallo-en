Tasks

As a houseman/porter, you make sure everything is clean and ready for daily operation. You take on the following tasks

  • Assistance in cleaning guest rooms and ensuring order
  • Ensuring cleanliness in public areas and on floors
  • Filling up the minibar and checking the stock
  • Assistance in transport such as laundry or furniture
  • Carrying out simple repairs in the house
