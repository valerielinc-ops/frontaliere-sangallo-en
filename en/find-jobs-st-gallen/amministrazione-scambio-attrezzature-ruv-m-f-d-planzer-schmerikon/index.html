Full description

For logistics wishes from XXS to XXL, we stand by our customers. With heart, mind, commitment and team spirit, we master the day-to-day challenges with the latest technology and infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • You ensure the correct and timely forwarding and processing of claims and are responsible for handling customer complaints
  • Around the exchange equipment business, you keep the administrative processes running and ensure that all movements are correctly recorded and processed.
  • You process customer orders for empty goods, monitor customer stocks and ensure an ongoing balance of stocks
  • If necessary, you support day-to-day business on schedule and thus contribute to a smooth operation
  • You are responsible for the telephone exchange, accept incoming calls in a friendly and professional manner and forward them to the responsible internal offices

Why do you succeed?

  • A completed basic commercial training forms your basis – ideally supplemented by knowledge in the transport or logistics environment
  • You bring several years of experience in a comparable function and have a good understanding of customers, processes and administrative processes.
  • Networked thinking and an independent working method are among your strengths – you recognize connections, work in a structured way and find suitable solutions
  • Oral and written you can certainly express yourself in German, another national language is a plus
  • When dealing with IT applications, you are well-versed and use digital tools efficiently in your everyday work

Your benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
