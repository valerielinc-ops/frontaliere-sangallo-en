Full description

The Vocational and Continuing Education Centre Toggenburg (BWZT) is an important educational institution in Eastern Switzerland. Around 1200 apprentices in 12 commercial and health professions acquire their vocational, school-specific basic education at the BWZT. The bridge offers and offers of the Vocational Academy complete our educational offer. The bridge offers at the BWZT accompany young people on their way from primary school to basic vocational training.

What to expect

  • Lessons in German and in various BVJ topics
  • Lessons in two classes
  • Accompaniment and promotion of young people in the transition to the professional world
  • active participation in the department, in the department BVJ and in the school

What sets it apart

  • Teaching diploma at secondary level I or equivalent qualification
  • Joy of working with young people in the transition from school to the professional world
  • Independent, flexible and team-oriented working method
  • Interest in a practical and motivating teaching design

What we offer

  • A dedicated and collaborative team
  • Existing teaching materials and teaching materials enabling rapid familiarisation
  • A structured handover by the previous teacher
  • Lessons expected four days per week
  • Possibility to continue working from school year 2027/28 on a smaller scale
