09.06.2026 Nesslau Wildhaus-Alt Sankt Johann

Become part of our team!

Do you want to reach summits together? Good financial advice is about accompanying people and companies on their way step by step. At Raiffeisenbank Obertoggenburg, you are exactly that: A reliable and inspiring companion with a vision. You become part of a well-established team of around 40 colleagues and take over an existing customer portfolio with plenty of freedom for your own way.

What awaits you?

  • You manage your own customer portfolio
  • You accompany commercial customers holistically with a focus on financing, as well as in the topics of investing and figures
  • You recognize opportunities early and give orientation in important decision phases
  • You are actively involved in market development and represent the bank in the region
  • You make sure that ideas become concrete conclusions
  • You keep your customer book free, from preparation to post-processing

What do you bring?

  • Very good, well-founded know-how in the lending business
  • Completed banking training with further training in the financial sector
  • Experience in corporate banking with a focus on real estate financing, ideally with own portfolio
  • Feeling for people and their needs
  • Enjoy taking responsibility and pushing things forward
  • An uncomplicated, sympathetic way and the desire to actively contribute your ideas
