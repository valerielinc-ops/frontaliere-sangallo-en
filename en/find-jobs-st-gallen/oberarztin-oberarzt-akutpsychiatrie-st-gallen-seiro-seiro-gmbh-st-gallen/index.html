Full description

Acute and emergency psychiatry with real creative leeway, in a clinic that not only describes recovery, but lives. In the St. Gallen area, we are looking for a senior physician with a special function or a senior physician with a special function for the psychiatric acute and emergency unit in the amount of 50 – 100%. In a recovery-oriented clinic with around 1,400 employees and university connections, you can expect an interdisciplinary team and demanding acute setting. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You take on cadreic responsibility in the psychiatric acute and emergency unit
  • They ensure the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with a broad spectrum of mental illnesses in inpatient acute setting
  • They guide and accompany assistant doctors through supervision and structured further education
  • You work closely interdisciplinary with nursing, psychology and other specialists
  • You participate in the medical background service and develop the station conceptually

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Experience in acute and emergency psychiatry is beneficial
  • Enjoyment of leadership and continuing education responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, at least level C1

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centred, trusting basic attitude in psychiatric care
  • Safe, calm action in acute and emergency situations
  • Team orientation, communication strength and recovery-oriented thinking

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Senior medical position with design room in a recovery-oriented clinic
  • Academic teaching hospital of the University of Zurich with high competence in education, training and further education
  • Generous promotion of further education and training
  • Interdisciplinary, appreciative working environment with modern treatment concepts
  • Workplace in a scenic region with a high quality of life

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
