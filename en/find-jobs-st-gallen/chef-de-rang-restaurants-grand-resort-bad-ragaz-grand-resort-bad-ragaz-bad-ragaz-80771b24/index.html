Full description

Tasks

In the daily service you run your own station independently and create culinary experiences.

  • Care of guests from reception to adoption including order reception and guest guidance
  • Ensuring professional food and beverage services at Michelin level according to the standards
  • Close and ongoing coordination with the kitchen during service
  • Guest accounting in your own station
  • Preparation of the mise en place for a smooth service flow

Requirements

We are looking for a service-oriented personality with development potential and pleasure in quality.

  • Professional experience in the gastronomy or service environment
  • Willingness to further develop professional knowledge in food and beverage service
  • Independent and structured working method
  • Host personality with team orientation
  • Completed training as a hotel or restoration professional
  • Good knowledge of German and English, additional knowledge of foreign languages beneficial

We offer

  • Taster evenings and perks at Thermal Spa
  • Free Tickets Tamina Gorge
  • Test sleep after passing the test period
  • Up to 50% discount in restaurants
  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origins in the healing thermal water of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place stands for regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis, we continue to develop the resort consistently – with the aim of becoming the leading Wellbeing & Healthy Living Resort in Europe and creating a place of strength for generations.

Around 800 employees shape the experience of our guests every day. Different talents, cultures and personalities work together on one goal: to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common aspiration to make this special place a bit better every day – One Resort – One Team.
