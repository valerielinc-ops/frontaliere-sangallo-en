Senior physician 80-100 %

Clinic of Anesthesiology, Rescue and Pain Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Superior responsibility of everyday hospital life with the implementation of anesthesias during surgical and interventional interventions of all partner clinics within the framework of the service contracts and in accordance with the latest standards
  • Active participation in further education and transfer of your broad clinical experience for the training of assistant doctors and anesthesia nurses
  • Proactive self-management and design of positive working relationships with employees, supervisors and partner clinics

What you bring for this job

  • Specialist title Anesthesiology with proven and broad professional experience
  • Professional, competent and sensitive treatment as well as high professional, personal and social competence
  • Readiness for continuous self-reflection and further development
  • Intrinsic motivation for working in acute medicine in a public-law house in dynamic times
  • Enjoying the proactive participation in the positive clinical everyday life, the positive clinical culture and the training of assistant doctors, anesthesia nurses and other professional groups

Our offer to you

  • Very broad clinical spectrum of a large Swiss center clinic with the possibility of specialization or participation in all areas
  • Strong and positive team and clinic culture
  • Great offer and commitment for continuous professional, personal and social development
  • Various employee offers in the area of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
  • If interested, due to the large international network, there is the possibility of special training (e.g. cardiac anesthesia, etc.)
