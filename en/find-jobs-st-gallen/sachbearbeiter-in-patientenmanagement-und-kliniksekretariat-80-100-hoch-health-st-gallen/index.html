Personnel/patient management and clinic secretariat 80-100 %

Nephrology, transplantation medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Link between the Central Patient Management and the Clinic Secretariat as a dual role
  • Outpatient and inpatient management including coordination of consultation hours and examinations
  • Preparation, management and post-processing of medical documentation and correspondence
  • Ensuring smooth administrative processes along the treatment path through close and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Contact person for patients, referrals and internal and external specialist offices

What you bring for this job

  • Completed education as a Med. Practice assistant/in EFZ, several years of professional experience in a similar function or specialist health with flair for administrative activities
  • Enjoy a versatile interface function and contact with different professional groups
  • Organizational skills and an independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and high IT affinity
  • Teamwork, initiative and responsibility

Our offer to you

  • A newly created, versatile and responsible dual role
  • Exciting combination of patient management and clinic secretariat in a dynamic specialist area
  • Working in a motivated and appreciative team
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Attractive employment conditions and a wide range of offers in the areas of health, family, mobility and employee benefits
