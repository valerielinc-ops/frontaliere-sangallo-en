Role overview

The Buchs Sargans Vocational and Training Centre (BZBS) trains 3,000 apprentices in over 20 professions every year and is attended by around 2,000 adults.

It teaches more than 200 teachers in basic education and about 170 lecturers in full and part-time work in further education. What to expect:

Self-employment of administrative work for the professions of agriculture (location Salez) and other professions of basic education (location Buchs) along the entire school education of the learners, i.e. from the registration of the learners, to the preparation of the start of school up to the qualification procedure and the exit.
