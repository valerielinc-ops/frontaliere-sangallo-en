09.09.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

In the Data Services IWH team, we actively shape data platforms and data warehouse solutions from connecting new data sources to ensuring stable data deliveries for our internal customers. We work closely with departments and IT teams to implement data-driven solutions efficiently and sustainably. In order to strengthen our team at the St. Gallen site, we are looking for a committed and analytically strong personality as DevOps Engineer DWH (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Translating professional requirements into technical solutions and their subsequent implementation
  • Strategic development of the data solution in coordination with corporate and solution architecture
  • Responsibility for reliable and high-quality data delivery
  • Including operation of new and existing interfaces
  • Independent analysis and correction of errors in the data processes
  • A wide range of tasks with strong team support and close cooperation with adjacent specialist areas

What do you bring?

  • Very good knowledge in the use of design approaches (SCD, ETL, CIF,...) and technologies (PL/SQL, Automic, PowerCenter, APEX,...) of data integration solutions
  • Several years of experience in end-to-end working methods: from specification and implementation to stable operation in complex system landscapes
  • Entrepreneurial personality with a cooperative and communicative working style
  • Interested in actively designing your direct working environment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
