Full description

Key responsibilities Are you ready to help shape the future of banking? For the strengthening of our team in the corporate customer business St. Gallen we are looking for a committed and future-oriented personality!

You value the dialogue with entrepreneurs and would like to accompany corporate customers on their way into the future? Do you like to approach people, build new relationships with pleasure and develop suitable solutions together with your customers? Then we look forward to meeting you. For our corporate customer business in St. Gallen, we are looking for a motivated, outgoing and winning personality who strengthens our team with initiative, enthusiasm and entrepreneurial thinking. You can expect a versatile environment with direct customer contact, exciting companies from the region and a lot of space to take responsibility and develop yourself further.

Your main tasks

  • Actively serve corporate clients and build and develop long-term customer relationships
  • Understand the needs and business challenges of our nationally and internationally active customers and jointly develop suitable solutions
  • Deepen existing customer relationships and gain new customers and business opportunities
  • Identify opportunities in the market and continuously expand the network in the region
  • Gaining new customers through your strategic understanding and solution-oriented thinking
  • Working closely with internal specialists and bringing together the right skills for our customers
  • Take responsibility for a customer portfolio and actively contribute your own ideas
  • Accompany our customers on the way to the digital future by inspiring and actively supporting them for innovative solutions

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from returnees. More information about our program: ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team You will become part of our dedicated corporate customer team in St. Gallen. We focus on personal exchange, mutual support and the joy of mutual success. We value different experiences and perspectives and maintain an open, uncomplicated cooperation.

Your skills and experience

  • You have a commercial or higher education, ideally supplemented by further training in banking or finance.
  • You bring experience in customer support in the banking sector.
  • Contact with people and companies gives you pleasure and you approach others openly and confidently.
  • With your motivated, communicative and winning personality, you can inspire others for your ideas.
  • You enjoy the acquisition and building new customer relationships.
  • Companies, business models and economic relationships arouse your interest.
  • You listen carefully, recognize customer needs and think solution- and service-oriented.
  • You show your own initiative, like to take responsibility and want to make a difference.
  • As a team player, you appreciate the exchange and cooperation with different specialist areas.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English.
