Role overview

Graf + Cie AG, the world’s leading supplier of clothing and combs for carding and combing machines for short-stack and long-stack spinning as well as for manufacturers and mechanical engineers for the production of textile composites (Nonwovens), is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group.

Headquartered in Rapperswil, Switzerland, Graf offers added value to its customers with highly developed industry knowledge, innovative solutions, excellent after-sales services and a global presence.

The core competencies of the company lie in the production of flexible flat clothing, all-steel clothing for carding and carding, in know-how in metallurgy and in precise steel processing.
